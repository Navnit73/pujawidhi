Santoshi Mata ka vrat mukhya roop se **shukravar (Friday)** ko rakha jaata hai. Prachlit parampara mein bhakt lagatar **16 shukravar** vrat rakhte hain aur satvein ya solahve shukravar ko udyapan karte hain. Santoshi Mata ko Ganesh ji ki putri maana jaata hai. Vrat ke niyam parivar aur kshetra ke hisaab se alag ho sakte hain; apne bujurg ya pandit ji ki parampara ko pramukhta dein. Yeh page saral vidhi, niyam aur katha ka saar deta hai.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sankalp">
    Shukravar ko subah snan karke saaf kapde pahnein. Sankalp lein: "Main Santoshi Mata ka vrat shraddha se kar raha/rahi hoon." Sankalp ki vidhi [yahan](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) dekh sakte hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sthapana">
    Chauki par kapda bichakar Santoshi Mata ki tasveer ya murti rakhein. Paas mein jal bhara kalash rakhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Puja">
    Deep, dhoop dikhayein aur roli, akshat, phool chadhayein. Gud-chane ka bhog lagayein.
    <Mantra dev={`ॐ श्री संतोषी मात्रे नमः`} rom={`Om Shri Santoshi Matre namah`} arth="Maa Santoshi ko namaskar. Yeh ek saral naam-mantra hai." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Katha">
    Vrat katha padhein ya sunein. Katha ka saar neeche diya gaya hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aarti">
    Aarti karein. Aarti ke shuruaati pad neeche diye gaye hain. Phir gud-chane ka prasad baantein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bhojan">
    Vrat rakhne waale din ek samay satvik bhojan karte hain, jisme khatti cheezein na ho. Prasad bhi khatte se door rakhein. Parivar ke niyam ke anusaar bhojan tay karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="16 shukravar aur udyapan">
    Prachlit parampara mein 16 shukravar tak vrat karke udyapan karte hain. Udyapan ke niyam (jaise kitne bachchon ko bhojan karwana aur khatta na dena) kshetra aur parivar ke hisaab se alag hain; apne pandit ji se poochh lein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Khatte ka niyam:** Prachlit niyam hai ki vrat ke din khatti cheezein na khayein na khilayein. Kshetra ke hisaab se iska paalan alag-alag tareeke se hota hai.
- **Vrat ki sankhya:** 16 shukravar prachlit hai; kuch log kisi manokamna ke sankalp par 11 ya 21 bhi rakhte hain. Parivar ki parampara dekhein.
- **Sehat:** Sehat ke hisaab se bhojan ka tareeka tay karein. Vrat anivarya shart nahi.
- **Anya vivaran:** Santoshi Mata ka vrat aadhunik kaal mein lokpriya hua hai, isliye alag kshetron mein vidhi aur katha ke roop mein antar milta hai.

## Mahatva aur katha

Prachlit vrat katha mein ek mahila ki shraddha aur kathinaiyon ka varnan aata hai. Katha ke anusaar usne Santoshi Mata ka vrat kiya aur uski kathinaiyan door hui. Udyapan se judi ek bhool ka prasang bhi aata hai, jiske karan khatta na dene ka niyam bataya jaata hai. Katha ke paatron ke naam aur vivaran alag-alag chhapi pustakon mein alag milte hain, isliye hum ise sirf saar ke roop mein dete hain aur kisi ek roop ko antim nahi maante. Ganesh ji ke prasang ke liye [Ganesh puja vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-puja-vidhi) dekhein.
