Gayatri Mantra Hindu parampara ke sabse prasiddh Vedik mantron mein se ek hai. Yeh **Rigveda (mandal 3, sookt 62, mantra 10)** mein aata hai aur parampara ise rishi **Vishwamitra** se jodti hai. Iska devta **Savita (Surya ka prerak roop)** maana jaata hai. Gayatri naam ek chhand ka bhi hai (24 akshar ka), aur Gayatri Devi ke roop mein is mantra ki upasana bhi hoti hai. Yeh page mantra, arth aur jaap ki saral vidhi batata hai.

## Arth (meaning)

- **Om:** Pranav, param tattva ka naad.
- **Bhur Bhuvah Svah:** Teen lok: prithvi, antariksh aur swarg. Inhe vyahritiyan kaha jaata hai.
- **Tat Savitur Varenyam:** Us Savita (sabko prerit karne waale, Surya roop) ka varenya (sabse shreshth, vandaniya).
- **Bhargo Devasya Dhimahi:** Dev ke tej (bhargah) ka hum dhyan karte hain.
- **Dhiyo Yo Nah Prachodayat:** Jo hamari buddhi ko prerna dein (prachodayat).

Saaransh: "Hum us divya prakash ka dhyan karte hain, wo hamari buddhi ko ujjwal marg par prerit karein." Pratyek shabd ke arth mein alag acharyon ki vyakhyaen thodi alag ho sakti hain.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

- **Kab:** Parampara mein teen sandhya-kaal (subah, dopahar, shaam) ka ullekh hai. Subah, sooryoday ke aas-paas, jaap karna sabse prachlit hai. Shuruat karne waale subah kisi bhi shaant samay se karein.
- **Kitni baar:** 108 baar (ek mala) prachlit hai. Kum samay ho to 3, 11 ya 21 baar bhi kar sakte hain. Koi sakht sankhya sabke liye nahi.
- **Aasan aur disha:** Saaf aasan par sukhasan mein baithein. Subah poorv disha (Surya ki taraf) mukh karna prachlit hai.
- **Mala:** Tulsi, rudraksh ya chandan ki mala prachlit hai; mala ki parampara sampraday ke hisaab se alag hai.
- **Kram:** Aachman, sankalp, dhyan, phir mantra jaap aur ant mein Surya ko jal arpit karna (arghya). Surya arghya ke tareeke ke liye [Surya Dev ki aarti aur arghya vidhi](/surya-shani-navgraha/surya-dev-ki-aarti-arghya-vidhi) dekhein.
- **Uchcharan:** Spasht uchcharan par dhyan dein. Sandeh ho to kisi guru, pandit ya jaankar bujurg se ek baar sunkar seekh lein.
- **Mann ki sthirta:** Jaap ke samay mobile door rakhein aur dhire, shant swar mein ya mann mein jaap karein.

## Sources aur variants

Mantra ka mool **Rigveda 3.62.10** hai. "Om Bhur Bhuvah Svah" ka jodna baad mein Vedik aur puranik paramparaon mein prachlit hua, isliye mool Rigveda ki rik "Tat Savitur Varenyam" se shuru hoti hai. Uchcharan aur swar (Vedik svar-chinh) alag shakhaon mein bhi thode alag mil sakte hain. Gayatri Devi ke dhyan-shlok aur lambi vidhiyan sampraday ke anusaar alag hain; yahan saral prachlit roop diya hai. Hum page ko reviewer ki proofing ke baad hi final maanenge.
