Hartalika Teej Bhadrapad maas ki Shukla Paksha tritiya ko manaya jaane waala vrat hai, jisme Shiv-Parvati ki puja hoti hai. Mukhya roop se uttar aur madhya Bharat, Nepal aur kuch anya kshetron mein vivahit mahilayen akhand saubhagya ke liye aur kanyayen manchaha var ki kamna se vrat rakhti hain. Vrat ke niyam parivar aur kshetra ke hisaab se alag hote hain. Date aur puja ka muhurat apne shehar ke panchang se dekhein.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Snan aur sankalp">
    Subah snan karke saaf kapde pahnein. Vrat ka sankalp lein: "Main Hartalika Teej ka vrat Shiv-Parvati ki puja ke saath shraddha se kar rahi hoon." Sankalp ke tareeke ke liye [Puja sankalp kaise lein](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) dekhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Murti banana aur sthapana">
    Shaam ko ret ya mitti se Shiv-Parvati (aur Ganesh) ki chhoti murtiyan banayein ya taiyar murti rakhein. Chauki par kapda bichakar murtiyan virajman karein, kalash rakhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ganesh smaran">
    Sabse pehle Ganesh ji ka smaran karein. Vistar ke liye [Ganesh puja vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-puja-vidhi) dekh sakti hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shiv-Parvati ki puja">
    Jal, roli, akshat, bel patra, phool, dhoop, deep se puja karein. Parvati ji ko shringar ka saman arpit karein. Yeh mantra bol sakti hain.
    <Mantra dev={`ॐ नमः शिवाय`} rom={`Om namah Shivaya`} arth="Shiv ji ko namaskar." />

  </Step>
  <Step title="Katha aur aarti">
    Hartalika Teej ki katha sunein ya padhein. Phir [Shiv ji ki aarti](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-aarti) karein aur prasad baantein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Jaagran aur visarjan">
    Kshetra mein raat ko bhajan-jaagran ki parampara hai. Agle din subah puja ke baad murti ka jal mein visarjan karte hain aur vrat kholte hain. Samay aur tareeka parivar ki parampara ke anusaar poochh lein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Date ki jaanch:** Tritiya tithi kabhi do dino mein padti hai. Panchang se dekhein; Ganesh Chaturthi ke saath ka kram bhi badal sakta hai.
- **Sehat:** Lambe nirjala vrat mein sehat ka dhyan rakhein, khaaskar garbhavati, bimar ya bujurg mahilayen. Vrat anivarya shart nahi.
- **Murti ka visarjan:** Prakriti ka dhyan rakhein; mitti ya ret ki murti ghar par bhi saaf bartan mein visarjit ki ja sakti hai.
- **Kshetriya antar:** Bihar, UP, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh aur Nepal mein paramparaen thodi alag hain. Apni parampara dekhein.

## Mahatva aur katha

Prachlit katha ke anusaar Parvati ji ne Shiv ji ko pati roop mein pane ke liye kathin tap kiya. Unke pita Himavan ne unka vivah Vishnu ji se tay karna chaha. Parvati ji ki sakhiyan unhe van mein le gayin taki wo vivah se bach sakein. "Harit" (haran karna) aur "aalika" (sakhi) se is vrat ka naam "Hartalika" bataya jaata hai. Van mein Parvati ji ne ret se Shivling banakar tap kiya aur Shiv ji prasann hue. Katha ke vistar aur naamon mein alag sources mein antar milta hai. Is vrat ka bhaav Parvati ki nishtha aur sankalp ka smaran hai.
