Guru Purnima Ashadh maas ki Purnima ko manayi jaati hai. Is din Maharshi **Ved Vyas** ki aur apne guru ki puja ki parampara hai, isliye ise **Vyasa Puja** bhi kehte hain. Parampara ke anusaar Vyas ji ne Vedon ka vibhajan kiya aur Mahabharat tatha Puranon ki rachna ki (maani jaati hai), isliye unhe "Adi Guru" ke roop mein smaran kiya jaata hai. Yeh page ghar ke liye saral vidhi deta hai. Aapke guru ya sampraday ki vidhi alag ho to wahi apnayein. Date aur muhurat apne shehar ke panchang se dekhein.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Snan aur sankalp">
    Subah snan karke saaf kapde pahnein. Sankalp lein: "Main Guru Purnima par Vyas ji aur apne guru ki puja shraddha se kar raha/rahi hoon." Sankalp ki vidhi [yahan](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) dekh sakte hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sthapana">
    Chauki par kapda bichakar Ved Vyas ji ka chitra ya guru ka chitra ya guru-paduka rakhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ganesh smaran">
    Sabse pehle Ganesh ji ka smaran karein. Vistar ke liye [Ganesh puja vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-puja-vidhi) dekhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Vyas ji ko pranam">
    Phool, chandan aur akshat arpit karke Vyas ji ka smaran karein.
    <Mantra dev={`व्यासाय विष्णुरूपाय व्यासरूपाय विष्णवे ।
नमो वै ब्रह्मनिधये वासिष्ठाय नमो नमः ॥`} rom={`Vyasaya Vishnurupaya Vyasarupaya Vishnave
Namo vai Brahmanidhaye Vasishthaya namo namah`} arth="Vishnu ke roop Vyas ko aur Vyas roop Vishnu ko pranam. Brahmagyan ke bhandar, Vasishth ke vanshaj Vyas ko baar-baar namaskar." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Guru ki puja">
    Guru ke paas ja sakein to charan-sparsh, tilak aur phool-mala se unka sammaan karein. Guru door hon to unke chitra ya paduka ke saamne diya jalakar puja karein.
    <Mantra dev={`गुरुर्ब्रह्मा गुरुर्विष्णुः गुरुर्देवो महेश्वरः ।
गुरुः साक्षात् परं ब्रह्म तस्मै श्रीगुरवे नमः ॥`} rom={`Gurur Brahma Gurur Vishnu Gurur Devo Maheshwarah
Guruh sakshat param Brahma tasmai Shri Gurave namah`} arth="Guru hi Brahma, guru hi Vishnu aur guru hi Maheshwar (Shiv) hain. Guru sakshat param Brahma hain; un Shri Guru ko namaskar." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bhog, dakshina aur aarti">
    Fal-mithai ka bhog lagayein. Guru ko apni shraddha anusaar dakshina ya seva arpit karein. Phir aarti karke prasad baantein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gyan ka smaran">
    Is din guru ke diye gyan ka smaran karein aur shastra ya Gita ka kuch ansh padhein. Gita ke liye [Bhagavad Gita path guide](/krishna-radha/bhagavad-gita-path-daily-guide) dekh sakte hain.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Date ki jaanch:** Purnima tithi kabhi do dino mein padti hai. Panchang se dekhein.
- **Dakshina ka bhaav:** Guru-dakshina ka mahatva shraddha mein hai, rakam mein nahi. Kisi par kisi tay rakam ka dabav na daalein.
- **Guru kaun:** Jinhone aapko vidya ya marg-darshan diya (mata-pita, shikshak, adhyatmik guru), parampara mein sabka smaran kiya jaata hai.
- **Sampraday ki vidhi:** Alag sampradayon (jaise Vaishnav, Shaiv, Smarta, Sai ya anya) ki guru-purnima ki apni vidhi hoti hai. Apne sampraday ki vidhi ko pramukhta dein.

## Mahatva aur katha

Parampara ke anusaar Maharshi Ved Vyas ka janm Ashadh Purnima ko maana jaata hai, isliye yeh din Vyasa Purnima kehlata hai. Unhone ek hi Ved ko chaar bhaagon (Rig, Yajur, Sam, Atharva) mein vibhajit kiya, aisi manyata hai, aur Mahabharat, Puran aur Brahmasutra ki rachna se unka naam jodte hain. Bhagavad Gita Mahabharat ka hi ek ansh hai. Guru Purnima Bharat ke alag-alag sampradayon mein, aur Bauddh aur Jain paramparaon mein bhi alag roop se manayi jaati hai. Yahan hum sirf prachlit Hindu vidhi de rahe hain. Tyoharon ki tithi ke liye [Hindu festival calendar](/puja-basics/hindu-festival-calendar-2027) dekhein.
