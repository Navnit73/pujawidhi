Shirdi ke Sai Baba ke bhakt unki aarti din mein kai baar gaate hain. Shirdi mein roz **chaar aarti** (Kakad, Madhyan, Dhoop aur Shej) ki parampara hai, aur ghar ke bhakt aksar **guruvar (Thursday)** ko vishesh puja karte hain. Sai Baba ki mool aarti **Marathi** mein hai ("Aarti Sai Baba, Saukhyadatar Jeeva"), aur uske Hindi roop bhi prachlit hain. Yeh page aarti ka parichay, guruvar puja ki saral vidhi aur sources ka note deta hai.

## Arth (meaning)

Aarti ke shuruaati pad mein bhakt Sai Baba ko sukh dene waale aur jeevan ke aadhar ke roop mein pukarta hai aur unke charanon mein sharan maangta hai. Aarti Marathi mein hai, isliye alag-alag chhapi pustakon mein Marathi shabdon ke Hindi anuvaad thode alag milte hain. Poore arth ke liye chhapi pustak ka Hindi anuvaad padhein. Sai Baba ke sandesh ke roop mein "Shraddha aur Saburi" (shraddha aur dhairya) ka ullekh Sai Satcharitra ki parampara mein bahut aata hai.

## Kab aur kaise gayein

- **Shirdi ki chaar aarti:** Kakad (subah), Madhyan (dopahar), Dhoop (shaam) aur Shej (raat). Samay ke liye Shirdi Sansthan ke official source par dekhein; hum samay nahi dete.
- **Ghar mein:** Subah ya shaam ko puja ke ant mein aarti gaayein. Diya jalayein, ghanti bajayein aur kapoor ya ghee ki batti se aarti karein.
- **Guruvar puja:** Bahut se bhakt guruvar ko Sai Baba ki tasveer ya murti ke saamne diya jalakar, phool chadhakar, bhog lagakar aarti karte hain. Kuch log is din vrat bhi rakhte hain. Yeh vyaktigat parampara hai.
- **Bhog:** Saaf aur satvik bhog prachlit hai. Sai Baba ke bhakt kai ghar mein roti-sabzi ya mithai ka bhog lagate hain.
- **Kitni baar:** Koi tay sankhya nahi. Ek baar poori aarti kaafi hai.
- **Doosri aartiyan:** [Aarti Sangrah](/puja-basics/aarti-sangrah) mein anya aartiyan hain.
