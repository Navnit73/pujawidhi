Vaishno Devi Mata ka mandir Jammu aur Kashmir ke Trikuta parvat par Katra ke paas sthit hai aur Bharat ke sabse prasiddh Shakti-teerthon mein se ek maana jaata hai. Parampara ke anusaar yahan Mata Vaishno Devi guphaa mein **teen pindiyon** ke roop mein virajman hain, jinhe Maha Kali, Maha Lakshmi aur Maha Saraswati ka roop maana jaata hai. ## Aarti ke bol

Vaishno Devi ke naam par kai bhakti-geet aur aartiyan prachlit hain, jinme alag gayakon aur kshetron ke roop alag hain. Shrine mein hone waali aarti ka apna kram aur paath hota hai. Ghar mein bhakt aksar sabse prachlit Devi aartiyan gaate hain, jaise [Durga ji ki aarti](/durga-maa/durga-ji-ki-aarti), jisme poora verified paath maujood hai. Jab Vaishno Devi ki aarti ka verified paath is page par aayega, wo Devanagari aur Hinglish dono mein diya jayega.

## Arth (meaning)

Vaishno Devi ki aartiyon mein aam taur par yeh bhaav aate hain:

- **Trikuta parvat ki mahima:** Parvat par guphaa mein Mata ka nivas.
- **Teen roop:** Maha Kali, Maha Lakshmi aur Maha Saraswati ka smaran.
- **Sherawali Mata:** Sher par virajman Devi ka roop. Is roop ke liye [Mata Sherawali ki aarti](/navratri-devi/mata-sherawali-ki-aarti) dekh sakte hain.
- **Bhakton ki pukar:** Sankat door karne aur sharan dene ki prarthana.

## Kab aur kaise gayein

- **Kab:** Subah ya shaam ki puja ke ant mein. Navratri aur shukravar ko bhi Devi ki aarti ki parampara hai. Navratri ka poora kram [Navratri puja vidhi](/navratri-devi/navratri-puja-vidhi) mein dekhein.
- **Kaise:** Diya jalayein, ghanti bajayein aur kapoor ya ghee ki batti se thali ko Mata ki tasveer ke saamne ghumate hue gayein. Aarti ke baad prasad baantein.
- **Yatra se pehle:** Vaishno Devi yatra ke liye registration, samay, mausam aur suvidhaon ki jaankari **sirf Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Shrine Board ke official source** se lein. Hum yahan koi samay, kharcha ya pandit-sewa ke daam nahi dete.
- **Kitni baar:** Koi tay sankhya nahi. Ek baar poori aarti kaafi hai.
