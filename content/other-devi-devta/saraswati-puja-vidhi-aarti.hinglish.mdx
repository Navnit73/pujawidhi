Maa Saraswati vidya, vani, sangeet aur kala ki devi maani jaati hain. Unki mukhya puja **Basant Panchami** (Magh maas ki Shukla Paksha panchami) par hoti hai. Vidyarthi, shikshak, sangeetkar aur kalakar is din apni kitaabein, vadya aur upkaran Maa ke saamne rakhkar puja karte hain. Yeh page ghar ke liye saral aur prachlit vidhi deta hai. Bengal, Odisha, Bihar aur anya kshetron mein puja ke tareeke aur pandal-sthapana ki apni vishesh parampara hai; aapke kshetra ki vidhi alag ho to wahi apnayein.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Snan aur taiyari">
    Subah snan karke saaf (aksar peele ya safed) kapde pahnein. Chauki par kapda bichayein aur Maa ko virajman karein. Kitaabein aur vadya saaf karke chauki ke paas rakhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sankalp">
    Hath mein jal, akshat aur phool lekar sankalp lein: "Main Maa Saraswati ki puja vidya aur buddhi ki prarthana ke saath shraddha se kar raha/rahi hoon." Sankalp ki vidhi [yahan](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) dekh sakte hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dhyan aur stuti">
    Maa Saraswati ka dhyan karein. Prasiddh stuti shlok:
    <Mantra dev={`या कुन्देन्दुतुषारहारधवला या शुभ्रवस्त्रावृता
या वीणावरदण्डमण्डितकरा या श्वेतपद्मासना ।
या ब्रह्माच्युतशङ्करप्रभृतिभिर्देवैः सदा वन्दिता
सा मां पातु सरस्वती भगवती निःशेषजाड्यापहा ॥`} rom={`Ya Kundendu-tushara-hara-dhavala ya shubhra-vastravrita
Ya veena-vara-danda-mandita-kara ya shweta-padmasana
Ya Brahmachyuta-Shankara-prabhritibhir devaih sada vandita
Sa mam patu Saraswati Bhagavati nihshesha-jadyapaha`} arth="Jo kunda ke phool, chandrama aur himkan ke haar jaisi shwet hain, shubhra vastra pahne hain, jinke hath veena aur vardan se sushobhit hain, jo shwet kamal par virajti hain, jinki Brahma, Vishnu aur Shankar jaise devta sada vandana karte hain, wo sampoorn jadta (agyan) ko harne waali Bhagwati Saraswati meri raksha karein." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aavahan, snan aur shringar">
    Maa ko aasan arpit karke jal se snan karayein. Haldi, roli, chandan lagayein, peele ya safed vastra aur phool-mala chadhayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Puja">
    Dhoop, deep dikhayein. Kitaabon aur vadya par bhi roli aur akshat lagayein. Saraswati Vandana ka path karein.
    <Mantra dev={`सरस्वति नमस्तुभ्यं वरदे कामरूपिणि ।
विद्यारम्भं करिष्यामि सिद्धिर्भवतु मे सदा ॥`} rom={`Saraswati namastubhyam varade kamarupini
Vidyarambham karishyami siddhir bhavatu me sada`} arth="He varadayini, icchha anusaar roop dharan karne waali Saraswati, aapko namaskar. Main vidya ka aarambh karta/karti hoon; meri vidya mein safalta ho." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bhog aur aarti">
    Peeli mithai aur fal ka bhog lagayein. Phir aarti karein. Aarti ke bol neeche diye gaye hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prasad aur vidyarambh">
    Prasad baantein. Chhote bachchon ka vidyarambh (pehle akshar likhwana, kuch kshetron mein "akshar-abhyas" ya "hathe-khadi") is din karne ki parampara hai. Kitaabein puja ke baad hi uthakar padhai shuru karein, aisi bhi prachlit parampara hai.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Date ki jaanch:** Panchami tithi kabhi do dino mein padti hai. Puja ka din aur muhurat panchang se dekhein.
- **Kitaabon ka sammaan:** Puja ke din kitaabon aur kalam ko Maa ke saamne rakhne ka bhaav vidya ke prati sammaan hai. Inhe pair se na lagayein.
- **Saral samagri:** Mehngi samagri zaroori nahi. Peele phool, jal aur shraddha se puja poori maani jaati hai.
- **Kshetriya antar:** Bengal mein Saraswati puja ek bada utsav hai aur murti-sthapana aur visarjan ki parampara hai. Ghar ki puja mein tasveer se bhi puja ho sakti hai.
- **Bachchon ko shamil karein:** Bachche apni kitaabein khud saja sakte hain, isse unmein padhai ke prati lagaav banta hai. Parinaam ki guarantee nahi di jaati.

## Mahatva aur katha

Saraswati ko Vedon mein vani aur nadi devi ke roop mein jaana jaata hai. Puranik parampara mein unhe Brahma ki shakti aur vidya ki adhishthatri maana gaya hai. Basant Panchami ko Basant ritu ke aagman ka din bhi maana jaata hai; peele rang ka sambandh sarson ke phoolon aur kheton se bhi joda jaata hai. Alag-alag granthon mein Saraswati ke prakat hone ki kathaon mein antar milta hai, isliye hum yahan kisi ek katha ko antim roop mein nahi rakhte. Ganesh ji ki puja ka kram [Ganesh puja vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-puja-vidhi) mein dekh sakte hain, kyunki kai ghar mein Saraswati puja se pehle Ganesh ji ka smaran hota hai.

  <Q q="Kya puja se pariksha mein safalta ki guarantee hoti hai?">Nahi. Puja shraddha aur vidya ke prati sammaan ka tareeka hai. Is site par kisi parinaam ka vaada nahi kiya jaata; padhai aur mehnat zaroori hai.</Q>
