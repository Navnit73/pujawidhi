Ghar par mitti ki Ganesh murti banana ek sundar aur shraddha se bhara kaam hai. Mitti ki murti ko visarjan ke baad ghar ke bartan mein hi gholkar paudhe mein daala ja sakta hai, isliye yeh paani ke srot par kam bojh daalti hai. Yeh page ek saral, shuruaati guide hai, jise bachche aur bade milkar kar sakte hain. Murti banane ki koi ek shastriya vidhi nahi hai; yahan vyavharik tareeka diya gaya hai.

## Mitti ki murti kyun

Kai shehron mein Plaster of Paris aur chemical rang ki murtiyan paani mein jaldi nahi ghulti aur jal-srot ko nuksaan pahunchati hain. Isi karan kai log mitti ki murti chunte hain. Parampara mein bhi mitti ki murti ko pavitra maana jaata hai. Ganesh sthapana ke niyam ke liye [Ganesh Sthapana, Murti aur Disha ke Niyam](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-sthapana-murti-disha-niyam) padhein.

## Samagri

- Shadu mitti ya saaf chikni mitti, kankad aur kachre se mukt. Kumhar se ya shilp ki dukaan se mil sakti hai.
- Saaf paani, mitti gundhne ke liye ek bada thaal.
- Aadhar ke liye ek saaf tray ya lakdi ka patta.
- Aakar dene ke liye lakdi ki tili, toothpick ya chamach, aur ek geela kapda.
- Prakritik rang jaise haldi aur kumkum, agar rang karna ho.
- Sajaane ke liye durva, phool aur mauli.

## Murti banane ke steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Mitti taiyar karein">
    Mitti ko saaf thaal mein daalkar thoda-thoda paani milakar achhi tarah gundhein, jab tak woh aate ki tarah naram aur chikni na ho jaaye. Kankad nikaal dein. Bahut geeli mitti murti ko bigaad degi, aur bahut sookhi mitti mein daraar aayegi.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aadhar aur shareer">
    Ek samtal aadhar par mitti ka gola rakhkar use ek moti, sthir aakriti mein dhalein: neeche chauda aadhar aur upar ki taraf thoda patla shareer. Ganesh ji ka pet gol aur bhaari rakhein, taaki murti santulit rahe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sir, kaan aur sund">
    Alag mitti se sir ka gola banayein. Do bade kaan chapte aakaar mein banakar sir ke dono taraf lagayein. Sund ke liye ek lambi belan jaisi mitti ki patti banakar mod dein. Jode wali jagah par geeli anguli se mitti ko achhi tarah milayein, taaki sookhne par daraar na aaye.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Haath, mukut aur vahan">
    Haath, mukut aur chhote mushak ko alag se banakar jodein. Jaise aap sahi samajhein waise saral ya vistrit roop dein. Murti ka sundar hona zaroori nahi; shraddha se banayi gayi saral murti bhi poojaneey hoti hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sookhne dein">
    Murti ko chhaon mein, hawadar jagah par 1-2 din sookhne dein. Seedhi tez dhoop ya heater ke paas na rakhein, isse daraar pad sakti hai. Chhoti daraar ko geeli mitti se bhar dein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sajavat">
    Rang karna ho to prakritik rang halke se prayog karein. Durva, phool aur mauli se sajayein. Chemical rang, chamakeele rang aur plastic ki sajavat avoid karein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sthapana aur visarjan

Sthapana ki puja ke liye [Ganesh Chaturthi Puja Vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-chaturthi-puja-vidhi) aur visarjan ke liye [Ganesh Visarjan Puja Vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-visarjan-puja-vidhi) dekhein. Mitti ki murti ko ghar par ek bade tub ya balti mein saaf paani mein visarjit karein. Poori tarah ghulne ke baad us mitti ko gamle ya paudhe mein daalne ki prachlit parampara hai, jisse use sammanpoorvak prakriti mein lautaya ja sake. Murti ka visarjan jahan karein wahan ke sthaniya niyam ka dhyan rakhein.

## Dhyan rakhne ki baatein

- Mitti ki murti banane ka koi ek shastriya vidhan nahi hai; parivar ki parampara ko maanein.
- Murti ke andar plastic, dhaatu ya kisi na ghulne wali cheez ka prayog na karein.
- Bachchon ke saath banate samay hath saaf rakhein aur tilli aur toothpick ka dhyan rakhein.
- Agar aap bazaar se murti kharidte hain to kumhar ya bharose-mand vikreta se poochhein ki murti sirf prakritik mitti ki hai ya nahi.
