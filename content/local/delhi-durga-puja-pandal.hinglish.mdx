## Delhi mein Durga Puja ka sandarbh

Delhi mein Durga Puja ka ek bada hissa Bengali samaj ki samitiyan aur colony-level pujayein sanbhalti hain. Chittaranjan Park (CR Park) aur Kashmere Gate jaise ilaake aam taur par Durga Puja ke liye jaane jaate hain, par kaun si samiti kab aur kahan puja karegi, yeh har saal alag ho sakta hai, isliye isko pakka maanke na chalein. Delhi mein kai aur colonies aur sanghathan bhi puja aur mela lagate hain.

Durga Puja ke paanch din ke kram ke liye [Durga Puja 5 Din: Shashthi se Dashami](/durga-maa/durga-puja-5-din-shashthi-dashami) aur [Durga Puja Kya Hai](/durga-maa/durga-puja) padhein. Doosre shehron ki jaankari ke liye [Durga Puja Pandal Near Me](/durga-maa/durga-puja-pandal-near-me) dekhein.

## Pandal ki sahi suchi kahan se payein

- **Puja samitiyon ke official pages**: Zyadatar samitiyan apne social media ya notice par samay aur pata daalti hain. Yahi sabse sahi source hai.
- **Sthaniya prashasan**: Police aur nagar nigam bheed prabandhan aur raaston ki soochna dete hain.
- **Local samachar**: Is saal ke pandal ki suchi aur route ki jaankari kai baar akhbaaron mein aati hai. Dhyan rakhein ki yeh sirf ek saal ke liye hoti hai.
- **Online maps**: Address maps par confirm karein, par purani reviews par poora bharosa na karein.
- **Sthaniya samaj ke log**: Jo log pichhle saal gaye hon unse unka anubhav poochhein, par jaankari ko dobara confirm karein.

## Visit plan karne ke tareeke

- **Samay chunein**: Subah ya dopahar mein darshan aasan hota hai. Raat ko bheed aur jam badh jaate hain.
- **Route banayein**: Ek hi ilaake ke kuch pandal chunein, poore shehar ko ek raat mein dekhna mushkil hai.
- **Parivahan**: Delhi Metro aur local bus ka istemaal aasan rehta hai, par pandal ke aas-paas ke station aur raaste bheed ke samay band ya diverted ho sakte hain. Delhi Metro aur Delhi Police ki taaza soochna dekhein.
- **Saamaan**: Aaramdayak joote, paani, power bank aur zaroori dawai saath rakhein. Qeemti gehne aur badi cheezein na le jayein.
- **Surakshit rahein**: Bheed mein bachchon aur bujurgon ka dhyan rakhein, wallet aur phone surakshit rakhein, aur police ya volunteer ke nirdesh maanein. Aapatkaalin nikaas ka raasta pehle dekh lein.
- **Samman**: Pandal mein photo lene se pehle dekhein ki rok toh nahi hai. Prasad aur darshan ka niyam samiti ke hisaab se rakhein.

## Verify before you go checklist

- Pandal ka naam aur exact address kisi bharose-mand source se confirm karein.
- Darshan ka samay aur ticket ya pass ki zaroorat dekhein.
- Aas-paas ke raaston aur parivahan ki taaza soochna dekhein.
- Mausam ka purvanumaan dekhein. Barish mein bheed aur raste prabhavit ho sakte hain.
- Kisi bhi paid VIP ya "guaranteed entry" ke daavon se saavdhan rahein.
- Apni sehat aur parivaar ki zarooraton ke hisaab se break aur khaane-peene ka plan banayein.
