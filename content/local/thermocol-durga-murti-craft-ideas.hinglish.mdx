## Thermocol aur Durga puja

Parampara mein Durga ji ki murti mitti se banti hai. Is par [Durga Pratima aur Murti Guide](/durga-maa/durga-pratima-murti-guide) padhein. Thermocol ka prayog aam taur par sajavat aur pandal ke dikhave mein hota hai. Kai jagah prashasan aur samitiyan paryavaran ke karan thermocol aur plastic ke prayog ko kam karne ki salah deti hain. Apne shehar ke niyam dekh lein.

## Samagri

- Mote aur patle thermocol ki sheets (alag-alag motai).
- Teekhe cutter ya garam taar wala thermocol cutter. Bachche cutter ka prayog bade ki dekhrekh mein karein.
- Fevicol ya thermocol ke liye upyukt glue, toothpick ya lakdi ki teeliyan.
- Acrylic ya poster rang, brush, golden aur laal sajavat ka kaagaz ya kapda.
- Sajavat ke liye mauli, rangeen kaagaz, moti ya foil. Asli sindoor aur phool alag se arpan ke liye hote hain.

## Craft ke vichar

- **Durga ji ka chehra aur mukut**: Gol thermocol par aankhein, bhrukuti aur tilak banayein, uske upar mukut jodein.
- **Trishul aur astra**: Patle thermocol ya lakdi ki chhadi se trishul ya chakra banakar golden rang karein.
- **Pandal ka dwar**: Chhote thermocol ke tukdon se mehraab ya toran ki sajavat banayein.
- **Diya ki thali sajavat**: Thermocol ke aadhar par kaagaz aur rang se thali sajayein, par diya seedha thermocol par na rakhein.
- **School project**: Durga ke nau roop ya Navratri ke paanch din ka chart banayein. Nau roop ke liye [Nau Durga ke 9 Roop](/navratri-devi/nau-durga-9-roop) padhein.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Design aur naap">
    Pehle kaagaz par design banayein aur naap tay karein. Thermocol par pencil se halka nishan lagayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kaatna">
    Cutter se dheere-dheere kaatein. Cutter ko apne haath se door chalayein aur bachchon ko bade ki nigrani mein hi karne dein. Garam taar wala cutter istemaal karein to hawadar jagah mein karein, kyunki thermocol jalne par dhuan nikalta hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Jodna">
    Alag-alag tukdon ko glue aur toothpick se jodein. Glue sookhne tak rukein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rang">
    Pehle halka prime ya safed rang karein, phir acrylic ya poster rang. Kuch rang thermocol ko gala sakte hain, isliye pehle chhote tukde par parikshan kar lein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sajavat aur sthaan">
    Sajavat jodein aur craft ko aisi jagah rakhein jahan diya, agarbatti aur kapoor se door ho.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Suraksha aur paryavaran

- Thermocol aag pakad sakta hai aur jalne par dhuan deta hai. Diya, agarbatti aur kapoor se door rakhein.
- Cutter aur glue gun ka prayog bachchon se akele na karwayein.
- Thermocol paani mein na bahayein. Use dobara prayog karein ya sthaniya recycling aur kachra niyam ke anusaar nikaalein.
- Kai samitiyan eco-friendly sajavat ke liye kaagaz, kapde, mitti aur prakritik rang ka prayog karti hain. Mitti ki murti ke liye [Eco-Friendly Mitti Ganesh Murti](/local/eco-friendly-mitti-ganesh-murti) ka tareeka bhi dekh sakte hain.
