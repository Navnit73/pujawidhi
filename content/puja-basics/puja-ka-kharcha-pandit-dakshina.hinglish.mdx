## Dakshina kya hoti hai

Parampara mein dakshina ko pandit ji ke samay, gyaan aur mehnat ke prati sammaan ke roop mein diya jaane wala daan maana jaata hai. Ise shraddha aur apni kshamata ke anusaar dene ki baat kahi jaati hai, isliye ise bhaav-taav ya "fees" ki tarah dekhna parampara ke anurup nahi maana jaata. Phir bhi aaj ke samay mein bahut se pandit ji samay aur saaman ki wajah se pehle se rakam bata dete hain. Dono sthitiyon mein aapas mein saaf baat karna sabse behtar hai.

## Kharch kin baaton par nirbhar karta hai

- **Puja ka prakaar**: Chhoti roz ki puja, satyanarayan katha, griha pravesh, ya kai din ka path alag-alag kharch aur samay maangte hain.
- **Samay aur pandit ji ki sankhya**: Kuchh pujaon mein ek se zyada pandit ji lagte hain, jaise rudrabhishek ya akhand path. Zyada samay aur zyada log ho to kharch badhta hai.
- **Samagri kaun layega**: Kuchh pandit ji samagri khud laate hain aur uska alag charge hota hai, kuchh aapse laane ko kehte hain. Poochh lein ki samagri kisne lani hai. Saaman ki suchi ke liye [Puja samagri master list](/puja-basics/puja-samagri-master-list) dekhein.
- **Shehar aur doori**: Bade shehar mein aane-jaane ka samay aur bhaada alag ho sakta hai.
- **Muhurat aur mausam**: Tyohaar ke dinon par pandit ji ki maang zyada hoti hai, isliye pehle se baat kar lein.
- **Bhog, prasad aur bhojan**: Prasad ya brahman bhojan ka bandobast alag kharch hai.
- **Murti ya sajawat**: Agar murti, phool, mandap ya sajawat bhi lagti hai to woh alag hoga.

## Pandit ji se kya poochhein

<Steps>
  <Step title="Puja ka naam aur samay">
    Seedhe batayein ki aap kaun si puja karwana chahte hain, aur kitne ghante lagenge. Muhurat ke liye apna shehar batakar panchang se confirm kar lein. [Aaj ka panchang](/puja-basics/aaj-ka-panchang-shubh-muhurat) tool madad kar sakta hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kya-kya shamil hai">
    Poochhein ki dakshina mein kya-kya aata hai: samagri, havan saaman, aane-jaane ka kharch, aur kitne pandit ji aayenge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Samagri ki suchi lein">
    Pandit ji se samagri ki likhi hui suchi maang lein, taaki bazaar mein bhatakna na pade aur zyada saaman na kharidna pade.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rakam ya shraddha tay karein">
    Agar pandit ji ek rakam bataate hain to usme kya-kya hai yeh samajh lein. Agar woh shraddha par chhodte hain to apni kshamata ke anusaar dein. Bhav badhane ya ghatane ki aadat se bachein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dakshina dene ka tarika">
    Dakshina puja ke ant mein, sammaan ke saath, haath mein dein. Iske saath vastra, phal ya mithai bhi kuchh parivaron mein di jaati hai. Yeh parampara par nirbhar hai.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur sawdhaniyan

- Rate ka koi "standard" chart maan lena. Aisa koi pramanik chart nahi hai.
- Bina poochhe samagri kharid lena ya pandit ji ko fir se yaad na dilana.
- Muhurat aur samay ka vaada na lena. Saaf bol dein ki puja kitne baje shuru aur khatam hogi.
- Dikhawe ke liye zaroorat se zyada bada anushthan rakhna. Shraddha se ki gayi saral puja bhi poori maani jaati hai.
- Nakli ya anjaan online sevaon par bina jaanch ke advance dena. Pandit ji ke bare mein parivaar ya mandir se poochh kar tay karein.

Agar aap [Durga puja at home vidhi](/durga-maa/durga-puja-at-home-vidhi) jaisi poori puja karwa rahe hain to pehle se aur zyada baat karein, kyunki us mein samay aur saaman zyada hota hai.
