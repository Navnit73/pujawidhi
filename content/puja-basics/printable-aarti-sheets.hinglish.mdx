## Is tool ke baare mein

Kai log aarti ko kagaz par padhna pasand karte hain, khaaskar ghar ke mandir ke paas laga kar ya parivaar mein sabko baantne ke liye. Yeh tool aapko apni pasand ki aarti chunkar A4 ya A5 sheet banane deta hai, jise aap print kar sakte hain ya PDF ke roop mein save kar sakte hain.

## Yeh tool kya karta hai

- Aap ek ya zyada aarti chun sakte hain, aur ek page par 1 ya 2 aarti rakh sakte hain.
- Sheet ka size A4 ya A5 chun sakte hain. A5 mandir ke paas rakhne ya chhoti pothi jaisa banane ke liye suvidhajanak hai.
- Print window mein "Save as PDF" chun kar PDF bhi bana sakte hain, jise WhatsApp par parivaar ko bhej sakte hain.
- Bade akshar ka option bujurgon ke liye upyogi hai.

## Print kaise karein

1. Upar tool mein aarti chunein.
2. Sheet ka size aur bhasha tay karein.
3. Print dabayein. Printer na ho to "Save as PDF" chunein.
4. Print nikaal kar saaf jagah par rakhein, aur zaroorat ho to lamination karwa lein taaki diya ya prasad se kharab na ho.

Aarti ki poori suchi [Aarti sangrah](/puja-basics/aarti-sangrah) mein hai, aur screen par padhne ke liye [Aarti reader mode](/puja-basics/aarti-reader-mode) bhi hai. Saaman ki printable suchi ke liye [Puja samagri master list](/puja-basics/puja-samagri-master-list) dekhein.

## Print ka upyog sammaan se karein

Aarti ki chhapi hui sheet ko puja ke saaman ki tarah saaf jagah rakhein. Purani ya phati hui sheet ko kachre mein na phenkein. Parampara mein ise saaf jagah, ped ke neeche ya jal mein visarjit karne ki salah hoti hai. Aap apne parivaar ke bade se poochh sakte hain. Puja ke niyam ke liye [Puja ke niyam](/puja-basics/puja-ke-niyam) bhi padhein.

Dhyaan rahe ki aarti ke bol parampara aur kshetra ke anusaar thode alag ho sakte hain. Agar aapke ghar mein koi alag roop gaya jaata hai to wahi maanein.
