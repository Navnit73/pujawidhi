## Is tool ke baare mein

Hindu tyohaar kal ka calendar angrezi tareekh se nahi, balki panchang se chalta hai. Yeh page aapko samjhata hai ki tyohaar ki tareekh kaise tay hoti hai, aur yeh calendar tool kaise kaam karta hai. Tool mein tareekhein ek licensed panchang se bhari jaati hain. Is page par kisi tyohaar ki tareekh ya muhurat apni taraf se nahi likhi gayi hai.

## Tyohaar ki tareekh kaise tay hoti hai

Zyadatar Hindu tyohaar tithi par adharit hote hain. Tithi chandra ke badhne-ghatne se banti hai, aur maah ko pratipada se poornima ya amavasya tak ginte hain. Isliye har saal ek hi tyohaar angrezi calendar mein alag tareekh par aata hai. Kuchh tyohaar, jaise Makar Sankranti, soorya ki gati par adharit hote hain aur unki tareekh aam taur par kam badalti hai.

Maah ka gadhan kshetra ke anusaar alag ho sakta hai. Kuchh jagah maah poornima se poornima tak (poornimant) ginte hain, aur kuchh jagah amavasya se amavasya tak (amant). Isse kuchh tyohaaron ke maah ka naam badal sakta hai, par tyohaar ka din aam taur par wahi rehta hai.

## Kabhi tithi do din kyon dikhti hai

Tithi ka samay sooryoday se alag ho sakta hai. Isliye kabhi ek tithi do din tak ya do tithi ek din mein aa sakti hain. Aise mein kaunsa din tyohaar ya vrat ka maana jaaye, yeh parampara aur panchang ke niyam ke hisaab se tay hota hai. Yehi wajah hai ki alag shehar ya alag sampradaya mein kabhi ek din ka antar dikh sakta hai.

## Is calendar tool ke bare mein

Yeh tool saal ke mukhya tyohaar aur vrat ek jagah dikhane ke liye hai. Ismein tareekhein ek licensed panchang se bhari jaati hain, taaki aapko galat ya anumani tareekh na dikhe. Jab tak kisi tyohaar ki pushti nahi hoti, tool us tareekh ko khaali ya "jaldi aa raha hai" ke roop mein chhod sakta hai. Hum bina pushti ke kisi tareekh ko nahi likhte.

## Tareekh ko pakka kaise karein

1. Calendar mein tyohaar dekhein.
2. Apne shehar ka maanya panchang ya pandit ji se tithi aur muhurat ki pushti kar lein. [Aaj ka panchang](/puja-basics/aaj-ka-panchang-shubh-muhurat) tool bhi madad karta hai.
3. Puja ki taiyari pehle se shuru karein, jaise [Puja samagri master list](/puja-basics/puja-samagri-master-list) se saaman ikattha karna.
4. Vrat ke niyam apne parivaar ki parampara ke hisaab se maanein.

Roz ki puja ki saral vidhi [Ghar ki daily puja vidhi](/puja-basics/daily-puja-at-home-vidhi) mein hai.
