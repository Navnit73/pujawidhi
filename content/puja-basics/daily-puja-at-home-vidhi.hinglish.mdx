Roz ki puja ka matlab lamba anushthan nahi hai. Das-pandrah minute ki shant, niyamit puja bhi poori maani jaati hai, bas mann lagakar ki jaye. Yeh guide un logon ke liye hai jo ghar mein pehli baar roz puja shuru kar rahe hain. Aapke parivaar ki parampara alag ho to wahi sabse pehle maanein. Yahan **prachlit roop** diya gaya hai.

## Shuru karne se pehle

Sabse pehle tay kar lein ki aap kis ishta devta ki puja karenge. Zyadatar ghar mein ek hi murti ya tasveer ko mukhya rakha jaata hai, baaki devi-devta saath mein sthapit rehte hain. Mandir ki jagah ke liye [Ghar ke mandir ki disha](/puja-basics/ghar-ke-mandir-disha-vastu) page dekhein. Saaman ki poori suchi [Puja samagri master list](/puja-basics/puja-samagri-master-list) par hai, aur thali ki taiyari [Puja thali kaise sajayein](/puja-basics/puja-thali-kaise-sajayein) mein samjhayi gayi hai.

Ek chhoti si bat: roz ki puja ke liye mehnge saaman ki zaroorat nahi. Shastron ki bhavna mein bhaav sabse bada hai, aur ek diya, thoda jal aur ek phool bhi swikar maane jaate hain.

## Roz ki puja ki saral vidhi

<Steps>
  <Step title="Snaan aur shuddhi">
    Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Puja ki jagah jhaadu-ponchha karke saaf rakhein. Agar kisi din snaan sambhav na ho to haath-munh dhokar aur manasik shuddhi karke puja karne ki chhoot parampara mein batayi jaati hai. Apne parivaar ke bade se poochh lein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aasan par baithna aur aachman">
    Saaf aasan par baithein. Daayein haath mein thoda jal lekar teen baar aachman karein.
    <Mantra dev={`ॐ केशवाय नमः ।
ॐ नारायणाय नमः ।
ॐ माधवाय नमः ।`} rom={`Om Keshavaya namah
Om Narayanaya namah
Om Madhavaya namah`} arth="Bhagwan Vishnu ke teen naam lekar jal grahan karna shuddhi ki shuruaat maana jaata hai." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Diya aur dhoop">
    Diya jalayein, agarbatti ya dhoop lagayein. Diya ghee ka ho ya til/sarson ke tel ka, dono parampara mein milte hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sankalp aur dhyaan">
    Chhote roop mein man hi man kahein: aaj main shraddha se apne ishta ki puja kar raha/rahi hoon, meri puja swikar karein. Lamba sankalp ki vidhi [Puja mein sankalp kaise lein](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) mein hai. Phir kuchh der shant hokar ishta devta ka dhyaan karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arpan">
    Jal, phool, akshat, chandan ya roli aur bhog arpit karein. Har vastu ke saath devta ka naam ya mool mantra bolna prachlit hai, jaise Om Namah Shivaya, Om Gan Ganapataye Namah ya Om Namo Narayanaya. Mantra apne ishta ke anusaar chunein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aarti">
    Ghee ki batti ya kapoor se aarti karein. Ghanti bajate hue thali ko devta ke saamne ghadi ki disha mein ghumayein. Aarti ke bol ke liye [Aarti sangrah](/puja-basics/aarti-sangrah) dekhein, ya padhne mein aasani ke liye [Aarti reader mode](/puja-basics/aarti-reader-mode) kholein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pradakshina aur kshama prarthana">
    Jagah ho to apni jagah par hi ek chakkar ghoom kar pradakshina karein. Phir haath jodkar jaane-anjaane hui bhool ke liye kshama maangein, aur prasad sabko baantein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Roz ki puja ka sample samay-tarika

Jinke paas samay kam hai, wo is kram ko apna sakte hain: diya jalana, ek mantra ya naam ki ek mala (agar chahein), aarti, prasad. Jinke paas zyada samay hai wo stotra, chalisa ya path jodte hain. Chalisa aur path ka kram badalte rehna theek hai, par ek hi kram par tikna aadat banane mein madad karta hai.

## Aam galtiyan aur dhyaan dene wali baatein

- Puja ko jaldbaazi mein nipta dena. Kam samay ho to chhoti puja rakhein, par shaant mann se.
- Murti ke saamne purane, murjhaye phool lagaye rakhna. Roz purane phool hata dein aur unhe sammaan se visarjit karein, jaise kisi ped ya saaf jagah par.
- Kisi deity ke liye jo cheez varjit maani jaati hai (jaise Ganesh ji ko tulsi nahi chadhayi jaati) uska dhyaan rakhein.
- Safai ka dhyaan na rakhna. Puja ke saaman ke liye alag saaf bartan rakhna achha maana jaata hai.
- Apni parampara chhod kar internet ke har naye niyam ko ek saath apna lena. Ek-ek cheez jodein aur apne parivaar ke bade se poochhein.

Aur niyam ke liye [Puja ke niyam](/puja-basics/puja-ke-niyam) padhein.
