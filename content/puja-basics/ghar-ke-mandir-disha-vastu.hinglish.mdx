Ghar ka mandir wo jagah hai jahan roz kuchh der shaanti se baithkar puja ki jaati hai. Vastu shastra ki mandir ke bare mein kai baatein prachlit hain, par sabhi parivaron aur kshetron mein ek jaisi nahi hain. Yeh page **prachlit roop** batata hai aur yeh bhi ki flat ya chhote ghar mein kya kiya ja sakta hai. Apne parivaar ke bade ya Vastu jaankar se bhi salah le sakte hain.

## Mandir ki disha

Prachlit Vastu mat mein ghar ka uttar-poorv kon, jise **ishan kon** kehte hain, puja ke liye sabse achha maana jaata hai. Agar wahan jagah na ho to poorv ya uttar disha bhi theek batayi jaati hai. Yeh dishayein isliye shubh maani jaati hain kyunki inka sambandh subah ke soorya aur prakash se joda jaata hai.

Murti ya tasveer ki disha ke bare mein do vichaar milte hain. Ek yeh ki puja karne wala poorv ya uttar ki taraf munh kare, jisse murti ka munh paschim ya dakshin hota hai. Doosra yeh ki murti ka munh poorv ya uttar ho. Is mein jhagda karne ki zaroorat nahi: apne parivaar ki parampara jo bhi ho wahi apnayein. Agar jagah ki wajah se ek disha mushkil ho to doosri chun lein. Koi ek sakht niyam sarvamanya nahi hai.

## Mandir kahan na banayein

Prachlit salah yeh hai ki mandir in jagahon par na ho:

- Seedhi ke neeche, kyunki wahan upar se paon chalte hain.
- Shauchalay ya bathroom ki deewar se sata hua, ya uski seedh mein.
- Bedroom ke andar. Agar bedroom mein hi rakhna ho to ek parda ya band darwaza wali almari rakhne ka upay prachlit hai, aur sote samay paon murti ki taraf na hon.
- Rasoi mein gas ke thik upar ya uske bilkul paas.

Yeh sab salah ke roop mein di jaati hain, kisi ke liye darane wali baat nahi. Mumbai ya Delhi jaise shehron ke chhote flat mein jagah seemit hoti hai. Wahan ek saaf shelf ya wall-mounted mandir bhi puja ke liye poora maana jaata hai.

## Mandir ki banawat aur saaf-safai

Mandir ko zameen se thoda uncha rakhein, jaise chowki ya shelf par. Zameen par seedhe murti rakhna prachlit nahi hai. Murtiyan bahut badi na hon aur ek doosre se sati hui na hon. Aam taur par ek hi devta ki ek se zyada murti saath mein rakhne ke bare mein bhi ghar ki parampara dekhi jaati hai. Mandir ke upar bhaari saaman ya kabaad na rakhein. Mandir ke aas-paas aur andar roz saaf-safai karein, purane phool hatayein aur diya-batti ke jale hue hisse saaf karein.

Roshni ka dhyaan rakhein. Ek chhota diya ya laalten ki tarah halka bulb mandir ko shaant dikhata hai. Mandir ke saamne puja ke liye saaf aasan ke liye thodi si jagah chhodein. Agar jagah na ho to khade hokar bhi puja ki ja sakti hai.

## Sthapna aur roz ki dekhbhaal

Nayi murti ya tasveer rakhne ki vidhi alag-alag parivaron mein alag hai. Kai log shubh din dekhkar ya pandit ji se poochkar sthapna karte hain. Roz ki puja ki saral vidhi [Ghar ki daily puja vidhi](/puja-basics/daily-puja-at-home-vidhi) mein hai. Puja ke samay ke niyam ke liye [Puja ke niyam](/puja-basics/puja-ke-niyam) dekhein. Ganesh ji ki murti rakhne ke khaas niyam [Ganesh sthapana murti disha niyam](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-sthapana-murti-disha-niyam) mein hain.

## Aam galtiyan

- Jagah ki chinta mein puja chhod dena. Disha se zyada niyamitta aur shraddha mahatva rakhti hai.
- Mandir mein tooti ya khandit murtiyan rakhna. Aisi murti ko sammaan se visarjit karne ki parampara hai. Kaise karna hai, yeh pandit ji ya parivaar ke bade se poochh lein.
- Mandir mein zaroorat se zyada saaman bhar dena.
- Dukan ya dost ki baat sunkar bina soche ghar mein tod-phod karwa dena. Pehle chhote upay dekhein.
