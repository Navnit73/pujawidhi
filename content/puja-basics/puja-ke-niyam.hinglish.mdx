Puja ke niyam ka asli maksad hai mann ko shaant aur jagah ko saaf rakhna, taaki dhyaan puja par tike. Yeh niyam kisi ko darane ke liye nahi hain. Parampara, sampraday aur kshetra ke hisaab se kuchh baatein badal jaati hain, isliye yahan **prachlit roop** diya gaya hai. Apne parivaar ke bade ya pandit ji ki batayi vidhi ko pehle maanein.

## Shuddhi: sharir aur jagah

Puja se pehle snaan karna prachlit hai. Agar kisi din snaan sambhav na ho to haath-munh dhokar aur man ki shuddhi karke puja karne ki chhoot bhi parampara mein batayi jaati hai. Puja ka kamra ya kona jhaadu-ponchha karke saaf rakhein. Puja ke saaman ke liye alag saaf bartan rakhein. Roz ki puja ki poori saral vidhi [Ghar ki daily puja vidhi](/puja-basics/daily-puja-at-home-vidhi) mein hai.

## Aasan aur baithne ka tarika

Puja ke liye ek alag saaf aasan rakhein, jaise ooni ya sooti kapda. Aam taur par zameen par seedhe baithkar puja karna prachlit nahi maana jaata, isliye aasan lein. Peer pasarkar ya murti ki taraf paon karke na baithein. Puja karne wala poorv ya uttar disha ki taraf munh kare, yeh bhi prachlit hai. Jinko ghutne ya kamar ki takleef ho wo kursi par baithkar bhi puja kar sakte hain. Shraddha aur aaraam dono zaroori hain. Mandir ki disha ke bare mein [Ghar ke mandir ki disha](/puja-basics/ghar-ke-mandir-disha-vastu) page par padhein.

## Vastra: kya pehnein

Saaf, dhule hue aur sammanjanak kapde pehnein. Kai parivaar mein puja ke liye alag kapde (jaise dhoti, kurta, saree ya dupatta) rakhe jaate hain, par yeh sab ke liye anivarya nahi hai. Aam salah yeh hai ki kapde saaf aur saadhe hon, aur sar dhakne ya na dhakne ke bare mein apne parivaar ki parampara maanein. Gande ya bahut tange kapde se bachein. Rang ki sakht pabandi nahi hai, par kai jagah lal, peela ya safed jaise saadhe rang prachlit hain. Kisi vishesh deity ya vrat mein rang ki parampara alag ho sakti hai.

## Puja ke samay mann aur vyavhaar

- Phone silent karein ya door rakhein. Puja ke beech call lena ya baat karna prachlit nahi hai.
- Jaldbaazi na karein. Zaroorat ho to puja chhoti rakhein, par shaant mann se.
- Puja ke dauran kisi ko bura kehne ya gusse mein baat karne se bachein.
- Tez khushboo wale sprays ya ghar mein shor se bachein.

## Saaman aur arpan se jude prachlit niyam

- Taaze phool arpit karein. Zameen par gire ya soonghe hue phool na chadhayein.
- Har deity ke liye alag vastu priya aur varjit maani jaati hai. Jaise Ganesh ji ko tulsi nahi chadhayi jaati, aur Shiv ji ko kuchh phool varjit maane jaate hain. Khaas deity ke vidhi page par yeh jankari milti hai, jaise [Ganesh puja vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-puja-vidhi).
- Bhog saaf aur satvik bana hona chahiye. Bhog banate samay sunni ya chakhna prachlit nahi hai.
- Diya jalane ke liye ghee ya tel ka istemaal karein. Agarbatti ya dhoop saaf jagah par lagayein.
- Puja ka saaman puja ke baad saaf jagah rakhein, aur bache hue phool sammaan se visarjit karein.

## Kuchh sthitiyon mein parampara ki salah

Kuchh parivaron mein sootak (janm ya mrityu ke baad ke din) ya rajaswala ke samay puja ke niyam alag bataye jaate hain. Yeh parampara aur parivaar par nirbhar karta hai, aur ismein koi ek sarvamanya niyam nahi hai. Aise mein apne parivaar ke bade ya pandit ji se poochh lein. Bimari ya majboori ki sthiti mein mann se ki gayi prarthana bhi swikar maani jaati hai, aur kisi ko doshi mehsoos karane ki zaroorat nahi.

## Aam galtiyan

- Niyam ko dar bana lena. Niyam ka maksad shuddhi aur shraddha hai, ghabrahat nahi.
- Ek hi ghar mein alag-alag logon ko alag niyam thopna. Pehle parivaar mein sehmati banayein.
- Internet par padhi har baat ko ek saath apna lena. Ek-ek cheez jodein aur bade se poochhein.
- Puja ke bhaav ko chhod kar sirf dikhawe par dhyaan dena.

Puja ki taiyari mein [Puja thali kaise sajayein](/puja-basics/puja-thali-kaise-sajayein) bhi madadgaar hai.
