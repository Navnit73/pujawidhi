## Deity ke hisaab se kuch khaas cheezein

- **Ganesh ji:** Durva (21), laal phool, sindoor, modak ya laddu.
- **Durga Maa:** Laal chunri, laal phool (gudhal), shringar samagri, halwa-chana ya mithai.
- **Shiv ji:** Bel patra, jal, kaccha doodh, dhatura aur madar ke phool (parampara anusaar), bhasma ya chandan.
- **Hanuman ji:** Sindoor aur chameli ka tel, laal phool, gud-chana ya boondi ke laddu.
- **Vishnu ji / Lakshmi ji:** Tulsi patra (Vishnu ji ke liye), kamal ya gulab ke phool, kheer ya mishri.

Yeh sirf ek **saral suchi** hai. Har parivar ki parampara alag ho sakti hai. Zaroori ho to pandit ji ya ghar ke bade-buzurgon se poochhein.

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- Sabhi saaman puja se pehle ek jagah rakh lein, taaki beech mein uthna na pade.
- Phool ko dhokar nahi chadhate. Phool taaza aur saaf hone chahiye.
- Bhog taaza aur saaf banayein. Bhog ke baad prasad baant dein.
- Mehnge saaman ki zaroorat nahi. Shraddha sabse zaroori hai.
