Sankalp ka matlab hai puja shuru karne se pehle apne mann mein aur devta ke saamne yeh kehna ki main kaun hoon, kab aur kahan, kis uddeshya se yeh puja kar raha/rahi hoon. Ise puja ka ek mahatvapurna ang maana jaata hai, kyunki isse mann ek lakshya par tikta hai. Yeh page sankalp ka saral aur prachlit roop batata hai. Poora Sanskrit sankalp vakya lamba hota hai aur parampara tatha pandit ji ke anusaar badalta hai.

## Sankalp kya hai aur kyon

Hindu parampara mein har bade karm ki shuruaat sankalp se hoti hai. Ismein hum kaal (samay), sthan aur apni pehchaan ka ullekh karte hain, phir kehte hain ki yeh puja kis bhavna se ki ja rahi hai. Iska ek saral arth yeh bhi hai ki hum apne mann ko sthir karke kehte hain: main yeh puja poore dhyaan se karunga/karungi. Kai parivaar roz ki chhoti puja mein bhi sankalp ko ek vaakya mein kehte hain.

## Sankalp kaise lein: saral vidhi

<Steps>
  <Step title="Taiyari">
    Snaan karke saaf aasan par baithein. Aachman aur diya jalane ke baad sankalp ka samay aata hai. Aachman ka tarika [Ghar ki daily puja vidhi](/puja-basics/daily-puja-at-home-vidhi) mein diya hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Haath mein saamagri lein">
    Daayein haath mein thoda jal, kuchh akshat aur ek phool lein. Kuchh jagah isme supari ya sikka bhi liya jaata hai. Yeh parampara par nirbhar hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sankalp bolein">
    Saral roop mein aap apni bhasha mein is tarah keh sakte hain: Main (apna naam), (gotra agar pata ho), aaj (tithi aur vaar), (shehar ya sthan) mein, apne parivaar ke sukh-shanti aur shraddha ke saath (devta ka naam) ki puja kar raha/rahi hoon. Meri puja swikar karein. Tithi aur vaar ke liye panchang dekhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Jal chhod dein">
    Sankalp ke ant mein haath ka jal zameen par ya kisi paatra mein chhod dein. Phool aur akshat devta ko arpit kar dein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prachlit Sanskrit sankalp vakya (shuruaati hissa)

Pandit ji jo Sanskrit sankalp karwate hain uska shuruaati hissa kuchh is prakaar prachlit hai. Yeh sirf jaankari ke liye hai. ## Sankalp ke samay dhyaan rakhne wali baatein

- Sankalp ka uddeshya shraddha aur shuddh bhavna se jude. Kisi bhi puja ke phal ko guarantee ke roop mein na maanein.
- Gotra pata na ho to apne bade-buzurg ya pandit ji se poochhna behtar hai, kyunki is par parampara alag-alag batati hai.
- Tithi aur vaar apne shehar ke panchang se dekhein, jaise [Aaj ka panchang](/puja-basics/aaj-ka-panchang-shubh-muhurat) tool mein.
- Sankalp ke samay baatein na karein aur mann shant rakhein.

  <Q q="Gotra pata na ho to kya karein?">Bahut se parivaar mein gotra yaad nahi hota. Aap apne bade-buzurg se poochh sakte hain. Na mile to pandit ji se salah lein, kyunki is par parampara alag-alag batati hai.</Q>
  <Q q="Kya Hindi ya apni bhasha mein sankalp le sakte hain?">Haan. Shraddha se apni bhasha mein sankalp liya ja sakta hai. Bade anushthan mein pandit ji Sanskrit vakya karwate hain.</Q>
  <Q q="Sankalp mein jal kyon chhodte hain?">Jal chhodna sankalp ko poora karne ka pratik maana jaata hai. Ise saaf jagah ya paatra mein chhodna chahiye, jahan paon na padein.</Q>
  <Q q="Kya mahilayein bhi sankalp le sakti hain?">Haan, ghar ki puja mein sabhi parivarjan sankalp le sakte hain. Vishesh anushthan mein parampara ke anusaar alag niyam ho sakte hain, jo pandit ji bata denge.</Q>
</FAQ>
