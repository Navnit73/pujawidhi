## Is tool ke baare mein

Panchang Hindu calendar ka woh roop hai jo roz ke samay ko paanch angon se batata hai. Yeh tool aapko aaj ka panchang aur shubh muhurat dekhne mein madad karta hai. Tithi, muhurat aur nakshatra jaisi cheezein shehar aur sooryoday ke hisaab se badalti hain, isliye apna shehar chunna zaroori hai.

## Panchang ke paanch ang

"Panchang" ka arth hi hai paanch ang. Yeh paanch ang hain:

- **Tithi**: chandra ke badhne-ghatne se bane din, jaise ekam, dwitiya aur poornima ya amavasya tak.
- **Vaar**: hafte ka din, jaise Somvar ya Mangalvar.
- **Nakshatra**: chandra jis nakshatra mein sthit ho.
- **Yog**: soorya aur chandra ki sthiti se bana ek vishesh yog.
- **Karan**: tithi ka aadha hissa.

Isme sooryoday, sooryast, Rahu kaal aur abhijit muhurat jaisi jaankari bhi jodi jaati hai. Samay dekhne ke liye [Aaj ka rahu kaal](/puja-basics/aaj-ka-rahu-kaal) page bhi upyogi hai.

## Shubh muhurat kya hota hai

Muhurat ka arth hai kisi kaam ke liye shubh maana gaya samay. Griha pravesh, vivah, naya kaam ya vishesh puja ke liye log muhurat dekhte hain. Yeh tool roz ka saamanya muhurat batata hai. Vivah, griha pravesh jaise bade kaam ke liye apne parivaar ke pandit ji se vistrit muhurat nikalwana behtar hai, kyunki ismein kundli aur parampara ka bhi dhyaan rakha jaata hai.

## Tithi kyon shehar ke hisaab se badalti hai

Tithi ka samay chandra ki gati se tay hota hai aur din ka naam sooryoday se judta hai. Isliye ek hi tithi alag-alag shehar mein thode alag samay par shuru aur khatam ho sakti hai. Vrat ya tyohaar ki tithi kabhi do din bhi dikhai de sakti hai. Aise mein kaunsa din maana jaaye, yeh parampara aur kshetra ke anusaar tay hota hai. Isliye vrat aur tyohaar ke liye apne kshetra ka maanya panchang ya pandit ji ki salah le.

## Tool ka upyog kaise karein

1. Apna shehar chunein.
2. Aaj ki tithi, nakshatra, sooryoday-sooryast aur muhurat dekhein.
3. Puja ke samay ka nirnay lene mein ise ek sandarbh ki tarah istemaal karein.
4. Tyohaar aur vrat ki tareekhein dekhne ke liye [Hindu festival calendar 2027](/puja-basics/hindu-festival-calendar-2027) bhi dekhein.

Puja ki shuruaat karne ke liye [Ghar ki daily puja vidhi](/puja-basics/daily-puja-at-home-vidhi) aur [Puja mein sankalp kaise lein](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) bhi padh sakte hain.
