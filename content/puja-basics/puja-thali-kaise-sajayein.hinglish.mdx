Puja ki thali ek tarah se aapki puja ka chhota sa manch hai. Sab kuchh ek hi jagah saaf-suthra sajaa ho to puja ke beech mein uthna nahi padta aur mann laga rehta hai. Yeh page batata hai ki thali mein kya-kya rakhein aur kaise sajayein. Parampara ghar-ghar aur kshetra-kshetra badalti hai, isliye yahan **prachlit roop** diya gaya hai.

## Thali ka aadhaar

Thali ke liye peetal, taambe, chaandi ya steel ki saaf thali prachlit hai. Kuchh jagah patte ki thali ya kela patta bhi istemaal hota hai. Ek puja ki thali alag rakhein, jo roz ke khaane ke liye na ho. Thali ke beech mein ek saaf kapda ya patta bichha sakte hain, aur uske upar saaman jamayein.

## Thali sajane ka tarika

<Steps>
  <Step title="Thali saaf karein">
    Thali ko dhokar pochh lein. Gili thali par roli ya akshat chipak jaate hain, isliye sukhi thali lein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Beech mein diya rakhein">
    Prachlit roop mein diya thali ke beech ya madhya ke paas rakha jaata hai, taaki aarti ke samay sambhalna aasaan ho.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Roli, chandan aur akshat">
    Chhoti-chhoti katoriyon mein roli, chandan aur akshat rakhein. Jagah kam ho to ek hi katori mein alag-alag hisse bana lein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Phool aur patra">
    Phool thali ke kinare, saaf tareeke se rakhein. Phool ko pehle se tod kar paani mein na bhigoyein. Taaza phool arpit karna achha maana jaata hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kapoor, agarbatti aur ghanti">
    Kapoor ko chhoti katori mein rakhein, agarbatti ke liye stand ya ek saaf ghere ka istemaal karein. Ghanti thali ke paas rakh lein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prasad aur dakshina">
    Prasad aur dakshina ko ek taraf alag rakhein, taaki puja ke baad baantna aasaan ho.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Alag-alag avsar par thali mein badlav

Roz ki puja mein sirf diya, jal, akshat, phool aur bhog kaafi hain. Tyohaar aur vishesh puja mein deity ke hisaab se saaman badal jaata hai. Jaise Ganesh ji ke liye durva aur lal phool, Shiv ji ke liye bel patra aur jal prachlit hai. Har deity ke vidhi page par us deity ke liye alag suchi milegi, jaise [Ganesh puja vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-puja-vidhi) aur [Durga puja at home vidhi](/durga-maa/durga-puja-at-home-vidhi).

## Aam galtiyan

- Thali mein bahut zyada saaman bhar dena. Zaroorat jitna hi rakhein.
- Purane phool ya bachi hui batti thali mein chhod dena. Har baar thali ko saaf karein.
- Thali ko seedhe zameen par rakhna. Saaf aasan, chowki ya kapde par rakhna prachlit hai.
- Thali ka rukh uljha hona. Aarti ke samay thali ko ghadi ki disha mein ghumana hota hai, isliye diya aisi jagah ho jahan se haath aasani se pakad sake.

## FAQ

<FAQ>
  <Q q="Kya puja thali ka koi ek tay roop hai?">Nahi. Parampara aur deity ke hisaab se thali badalti hai. Yahan prachlit roop diya gaya hai. Apne parivaar ya pandit ji ki batayi vidhi ko pehle maanein.</Q>
  <Q q="Kya steel ki thali chalegi?">Haan. Peetal, taambe aur chaandi ki thali prachlit hai, par saaf steel ki thali bhi istemaal hoti hai. Zaroori yeh hai ki thali saaf ho aur sirf puja ke liye ho.</Q>
  <Q q="Aarti ki thali mein diya kitne hone chahiye?">Kai ghar mein ek hi diya ya panch batti ka diya prachlit hai. Koi sakht sankhya tay nahi, parampara ke hisaab se chunein.</Q>
  <Q q="Phool kaunse chadhayein?">Deity ke hisaab se alag hota hai. Aam taur par taaze, saaf phool lein. Zameen par gire ya soonghe hue phool na chadhayein, aisi parampara hai. Khaas deity ke liye unke vidhi page dekhein.</Q>
  <Q q="Puja ke baad thali ka saaman kya karein?">Prasad baant dein. Phool aur patra saaf jagah ya ped ke neeche sammaan se visarjit karein. Thali dhokar agli puja ke liye rakh dein.</Q>
</FAQ>
