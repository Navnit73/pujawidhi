## Is tool ke baare mein

Aarti ya path ke samay haath mein mobile pakadkar chhote akshar padhna aur baar-baar screen scroll karna mushkil hota hai. Reader mode isi pareshani ke liye hai. Yeh aapko aarti ke bol bade font mein dikhata hai aur chahein to apne aap scroll bhi karta hai, taaki aapke haath thali ya ghanti mein lage rahein.

## Reader mode mein kya milta hai

- **Bada font**: Aarti ke bol aasani se padhne ke liye font ka size aap badha-ghata sakte hain.
- **Auto-scroll**: Screen apne aap dheere-dheere neeche jaati hai. Speed apne gaane ya padhne ki gati ke hisaab se badal sakte hain.
- **Devanagari aur Hinglish**: Aap apne anusaar Devanagari ya Roman bol chun sakte hain.
- **Saaf screen**: Beech ki baatein hata kar sirf aarti ke bol dikhaye jaate hain.

## Kaise istemaal karein

1. Upar diye hue picker se aarti chunein.
2. Apni suvidha ke hisaab se font size aur bhasha chunein.
3. Auto-scroll ki speed tay karein aur aarti shuru karein.
4. Zaroorat ho to kisi bhi samay scroll rok sakte hain.

Aarti ki puri suchi [Aarti sangrah](/puja-basics/aarti-sangrah) mein hai. Agar aap kagaz par padhna chahte hain to [Printable aarti sheets](/puja-basics/printable-aarti-sheets) se print nikaal sakte hain.

## Puja ke samay phone ka dhyaan

Puja mein phone ka use kam se kam rakhein. Reader mode kholne se pehle phone ko silent ya do-not-disturb par kar lein, taaki aarti ke beech call ya notification na aaye. Screen ko jaagte rehne dein, aur zaroorat ho to phone ko saaf jagah par thali se door stand par rakhein. Puja ke niyam ke liye [Puja ke niyam](/puja-basics/puja-ke-niyam) padhein.

Dhyaan rahe ki yeh tool aarti ko padhne ki suvidha hai. Aarti ke bol parampara aur kshetra ke anusaar thode alag ho sakte hain. Agar aapke ghar mein koi alag roop gaya jaata hai to wahi maanein.
