## Is tool ke baare mein

Rahu kaal har din ka ek aisa samay-khand maana jaata hai jise prachlit roop mein naye ya shubh kaam ki shuruaat ke liye theek nahi mana jaata. Yeh tool aapke shehar aur din ke hisaab se Rahu kaal dikhane ke liye banaya gaya hai. Natija dekhne se pehle apna shehar chunein, kyunki samay jagah ke anusaar badalta hai.

## Rahu kaal kya hai

Parampara ke anusaar din ke ujale (sooryoday se sooryast tak) ko aath barabar hisson mein baanta jaata hai. Har hissa lagbhag dedh ghante ka hota hai. Inme se ek hissa Rahu kaal kehlata hai. Kaun sa hissa Rahu kaal hoga, yeh hafte ke din par nirbhar karta hai. Prachlit kram mein Somvar ko doosra, Mangalvar ko saatvan, Budhvar ko paanchvan, Guruvar ko chhatha, Shukravar ko chautha, Shanivar ko teesra aur Ravivar ko aathvan hissa maana jaata hai. Kuchh panchang aur kshetron mein thoda antar mil sakta hai.

## Samay shehar ke hisaab se kyon badalta hai

Sooryoday aur sooryast har shehar mein alag samay par hote hain, aur mausam ke saath badalte hain. Isliye Rahu kaal ka samay Delhi, Mumbai ya Kolkata mein ek jaisa nahi hota. Yeh tool aapke chune hue shehar ke hisaab se hisaab lagata hai. Phir bhi vishesh puja ya bade kaam ke liye apne parivaar ke pandit ji ya local panchang se milaan kar lein.

## Tool ka upyog kaise karein

1. Apna shehar chunein.
2. Aaj ka Rahu kaal dekhein.
3. Agar aapko koi puja ya shubh kaam shuru karna hai, to ise ek sandarbh ke roop mein dekhein.
4. Poore din ka vistrit muhurat dekhne ke liye [Aaj ka panchang](/puja-basics/aaj-ka-panchang-shubh-muhurat) page bhi dekhein.

## Dhyaan rakhne wali baatein

- Rahu kaal ek parampara-adharit maanyata hai. Ise darne ki baat na banayein. Kai parivaar ise maante hain, kai nahi.
- Roz ki puja, aarti ya prarthana ke liye Rahu kaal ka koi bandhan nahi hota. Kai log is samay bhi bhagwan ka naam lete hain.
- Tool ka natija jaankari ke liye hai. Tithi, muhurat ya vishesh anushthan ke nirnay ke liye panchang aur pandit ji ki salah zaroor lein.
- Puja ki taiyari ke liye [Ghar ki daily puja vidhi](/puja-basics/daily-puja-at-home-vidhi) bhi dekh sakte hain.
