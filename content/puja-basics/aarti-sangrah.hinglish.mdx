Yeh **aarti sangrah** sabhi devi-devtaon ki aartiyon ka ek jagah ka sankalan hai. Har aarti ka apna page hai jisme Devanagari aur Hinglish dono mein bol, arth, aur aarti gaane ka tareeka diya gaya hai. Jo aartiyan abhi taiyaar ho rahi hain, unhe neeche 'jald aa raha hai' ke saath dikhaya gaya hai.

## Aarti kaise padhein aur gayein

- **Reader mode:** Kisi bhi aarti page par 'Reader mode' dabayein. Bada font, auto-scroll aur screen jaagta rakhne ka option milta hai, taaki mandir mein haath mein mobile pakdna na pade. [Reader mode tool](/puja-basics/aarti-reader-mode) par bhi aap ek saath kai aartiyon mein se chun sakte hain.
- **Print:** Mandir ya ghar mein lagane ke liye [printable aarti sheets](/puja-basics/printable-aarti-sheets) banayein. A4 ya A5, ek ya do aarti per page.
- **Offline:** Site ko mobile par install karein. Jo pages aap ek baar khol lete hain, woh bina signal ke bhi khulte hain.
- **WhatsApp:** Har page par 'WhatsApp par bhejein' button hai, jisse aarti ka link parivaar mein baant sakte hain.

## Aarti ka kram

Aam taur par aarti puja ke ant mein ki jaati hai. Sabse pehle Ganesh ji ki aarti, phir us din ke mukhya devta ki aarti, aur ant mein sabhi devtaon ki saamuhik aarti (jaise "Om Jai Jagdish Hare") gayi jaati hai. Yeh ek **prachlit kram** hai. Parivar ya kshetra ke hisaab se kram badal sakta hai.
