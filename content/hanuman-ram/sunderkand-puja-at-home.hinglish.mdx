Sunderkand ka paath ghar par ek chhoti puja ke saath karna bahut prachlit hai. Yeh page batata hai ki is paath ke liye ghar par kya taiyari karein, kaunsi samagri rakhein aur kya niyam dhyan mein rakhein. Paath ki vidhi aur Sunderkand kya hai, iske liye [Sunderkand path vidhi](/hanuman-ram/sunderkand-path-vidhi) page dekhein. Parivar ki alag parampara ho to wahi follow karein. Yahan **prachlit roop** diya gaya hai.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Taiyari">
    Paath se pehle snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Ghar ka puja sthaan saaf karein, chowki par kapda bichayein aur Ram darbar tatha Hanuman ji ko virajman karein. Pustak ko saaf aasan ya chowki par rakhein, zameen par seedhe nahi. Paath ke samay baithne ke liye saaf aasan lein. Mobile silent kar dein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ganesh aur guru smaran">
    Puja shuru karne se pehle Ganesh ji ka smaran karein, phir apne ishta aur guru ko pranam karein.
    <Mantra dev="वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय सूर्यकोटि समप्रभ । निर्विघ्नं कुरु मे देव सर्वकार्येषु सर्वदा ॥" rom="Vakratund Mahakay Suryakoti Samaprabh, Nirvighnam kuru me Deva sarvakaryeshu sarvada" arth="He vakratund, vishalkaay Deva, mere sabhi kaaryon mein hamesha vighna door karein." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sankalp">
    Diya jalayein. Haath mein jal, akshat aur phool lekar sankalp lein: "Main aaj Shri Ram aur Hanuman ji ki prasannata ke liye Sunderkand ka paath shraddha se kar raha/rahi hoon. Mera paath sweekar karein." Jal zameen par chhod dein. [Sankalp ka vistar yahan](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) dekhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chhoti puja">
    Ram darbar aur Hanuman ji ko tilak karein, phool aur tulsi dal chadhayein, Hanuman ji ko sindoor lagayein. Dhoop aur diya dikhayein. Bhog lagayein aur jal arpit karein. Hanuman puja ke vistar ke liye [Hanuman puja vidhi](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-puja-vidhi) dekh sakte hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paath shuru karein">
    Pustak ko pranam karke Sunderkand ka paath shuru karein. Aam taur par paath ek aasan par baithkar, bina beech mein uthe, shaant man se kiya jaata hai. Agar paath mein beech mein uthna pade to ek chhota vishram theek hai. Parivar mein agar sab milkar padh rahe hain to doha ya shlok baari-baari se padhne ki parampara bhi hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paath ki samapti aur aarti">
    Paath poora hone par Ram darbar ko pranam karein. Pehle [Hanuman ji ki aarti](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-ji-ki-aarti), phir [Ram ji ki aarti](/hanuman-ram/ram-ji-ki-aarti) gayein (ya parivar ke kram ke anusar). Aarti lekar prasad baantein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kshama prarthana">
    Ant mein kshama prarthana karein.
    <Mantra dev={`आवाहनं न जानामि न जानामि विसर्जनम् ।
पूजां चैव न जानामि क्षमस्व परमेश्वर ॥`} rom={`Aavahanam na jaanami na jaanami visarjanam
Poojam chaiva na jaanami kshamasva Parameshwar`} arth="Main na avahan jaanta hoon, na visarjan, na puja ki vidhi. Mujhse jo bhool hui ho use kshama karein." />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Pustak ka samman:** Pustak ko saaf jagah rakhein aur paath ke baad kapde mein lapet kar surakshit rakhein.
- **Shuddhta:** Snaan, saaf kapde aur shaant man mukhya hain. Jin gharon mein bhojan ke baad paath ya vrat ki parampara hai, wo apne tareeke se follow karein.
- **Uchcharan:** Shabdon ko dheere aur saaf padhein. Mushkil shabdon ke liye pandit ji ya bade se madad lein. Gati se zyada shuddhta aur bhav mahatvapurn hai.
- **Tel ya ghee ka diya:** Paath ke dauran diya jalta rahe, aisi parampara hai. Surakshit jagah par rakhein aur dhyan rakhein.
- **Dakshina aur bhojan:** Pandit ji se paath karwane par dakshina ke baare mein pehle baat kar lein. Kharche ke baare mein [puja ka kharcha aur pandit dakshina](/puja-basics/puja-ka-kharcha-pandit-dakshina) page dekhein.
- **Phal ka vaada nahi:** Yeh site paath se kisi vishesh phal ya parinaam ka vaada nahi karti. Yeh shraddha ka vishay hai.

## Mahatva

Sunderkand ko Hanuman ji ki yatra, Sita ji ki khoj aur unke saahas aur buddhi ke prasangon ke karan bahut priya maana jaata hai. Isliye kai log naye kaam, yatra ya man ki shaanti ke liye iska paath karte hain. Yeh parampara aur vishwas ka vishay hai.
