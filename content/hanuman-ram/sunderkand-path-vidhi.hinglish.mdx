## Sunderkand kya hai

Sunderkand Ramcharitmanas ka paanchva kand hai, jise Goswami Tulsidas ji ne likha. Valmiki Ramayan mein bhi paanchva kand Sundar Kand kehlata hai. Prachlit paath aam taur par Ramcharitmanas ke Sunderkand ka hota hai, jo Awadhi bhasha mein doha-chaupai ke roop mein hai aur shuruaat mein kuch Sanskrit shlok bhi hain. ## Sunderkand mein kya varnan hai

Yeh ek saral saar hai, text ka line-by-line anuvad nahi:

- **Samudra langhan:** Hanuman ji Lanka jaane ke liye samudra paar karte hain. Raste mein unhe Surasa, Mainak parvat aur Simhika jaise adchan aate hain, jinhe ve apni buddhi aur bal se paar karte hain.
- **Lanka mein pravesh aur Sita ji ki khoj:** Ve Lanka mein pravesh karte hain, Vibhishan se milte hain aur Ashok Vatika mein Sita ji ko dekhte hain.
- **Sita ji se bhent:** Hanuman ji Ram ji ki mudrika Sita ji ko dete hain aur unhe Ram ka sandesh sunate hain. Sita ji apni chudamani nishani ke roop mein deti hain.
- **Lanka dahan:** Ravan ke darbar mein Hanuman ji ko bandhak banaya jaata hai aur unki poonchh jalane ka aadesh milta hai, jiske baad Lanka mein agni lagti hai.
- **Wapsi:** Hanuman ji Ram ke paas laut kar Sita ji ka samachar dete hain.
- **Ram ka samudra tat par aana:** Ram ji vanar-sena ke saath samudra tat par pahunchte hain aur Vibhishan unki sharan mein aate hain.

## Paath kab aur kaise karein

- **Kab:** Mangalvar aur Shanivar prachlit hain. Naye kaam, griha pravesh, janmadin, ya man ki shaanti ke liye bhi log paath karte hain. Vishesh muhurat ya anushthan ke liye [panchang](/puja-basics/aaj-ka-panchang-shubh-muhurat) dekhein ya pandit ji se poochhein.
- **Samay:** Subah ya shaam ka samay aam hai. Poora Sunderkand padhne mein aam taur par kuch ghante lagte hain, jo gati par nirbhar hai.
- **Kitni baar:** Ek baar poora paath aam hai. Kuch log 3, 5, 7 ya 11 baar ka sankalp lete hain, jo vyaktigat shraddha ka vishay hai, koi anivarya niyam nahi.
- **Taiyari:** Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein, Ram darbar aur Hanuman ji ke saamne diya jalayein, sankalp lein aur saaf aasan par baithkar paath karein. Pustak ko saaf aasan ya chowki par rakhein.
- **Aarti:** Paath ke ant mein [Hanuman ji ki aarti](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-ji-ki-aarti) aur [Ram ji ki aarti](/hanuman-ram/ram-ji-ki-aarti) gayein aur prasad baantein.
- **Chhoti puja:** [Hanuman puja vidhi](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-puja-vidhi) mein dikhaye gaye upchar paath se pehle kar sakte hain.
- **Vrat aur bhojan:** Kuch parivar paath ke din satvik bhojan ya vrat ki parampara rakhte hain. Yeh parivar par nirbhar hai. Swasthya ka dhyan rakhein.
- **Mahilayein aur bachche:** Paath koi bhi shraddha se kar sakta hai.
