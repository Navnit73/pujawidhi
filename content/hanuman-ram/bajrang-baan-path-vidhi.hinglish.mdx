## Paath ka swaroop

Bajrang Baan mein aam taur par ek shuruaati doha, kai chaupaiyan aur ant ka doha hota hai. Iski bhasha Awadhi-mishrit Hindi hai aur shaili josh aur dridh prarthana wali hai: bhakt Hanuman ji se Ram ki dohai dekar sahayata maangta hai. Is stuti ke rachnakar ke baare mein parampara mein ise aam taur par Tulsidas ji se joda jaata hai, par is par vidwanon mein ek mat nahi hai, isliye hum rachnakar ka naam pakke taur par nahi likh rahe.

## Arth (meaning) ka saar

Yeh apne shabdon mein saar hai, line-by-line anuvad nahi:

- **Shuruaati doha:** Bhakt vishwas, prem aur vinay ke saath Hanuman ji ko yaad karta hai aur kehta hai ki jinke kaam Hanuman ji poore karte hain unke kaam sidh hote hain.
- **Chaupaiyon mein:** Hanuman ji ke bal, Ram-bhakti aur unke prasiddh kaaryon ka smaran hai, jaise Lanka yatra, Sita ji ki khoj aur Lakshman ji ke liye Sanjeevani. Bhakt apne sankat, bhay aur badhaon se raksha ki prarthana karta hai.
- **Ant ka doha:** Phir se prarthana aur paath ka sankshipt saar.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

- **Kab:** Mangalvar aur Shanivar prachlit hain. Roz bhi koi padh sakta hai. Subah snaan ke baad ka samay aam hai.
- **Taiyari:** Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Hanuman ji ya Ram darbar ke saamne diya jalayein. Saaf aasan par baithein.
- **Chhoti puja:** Chahein to pehle [Hanuman puja vidhi](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-puja-vidhi) ke anusar chhoti puja aur sankalp karein ([sankalp kaise lein](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein)).
- **Kitni baar:** Ek baar ka paath aam hai. Kuch log 3, 5, 11 ya 21 baar padhne ka sankalp lete hain. Yeh vyaktigat shraddha hai, koi anivarya niyam nahi.
- **Uchcharan aur bhav:** Shabdon ko dheere aur saaf bolein. Is stuti ki shaili josh wali hai, par man shaant aur shraddhapoorn rakhein.
- **Ant mein:** [Hanuman ji ki aarti](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-ji-ki-aarti) gayein aur prasad baantein.
- **Pehle kya padhein:** Agar aap paath mein naye hain to pehle [Hanuman Chalisa](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-chalisa) se shuru karna saral rehta hai.

## Savdhaniyan aur niyam

- **Bhay ya jaldbaazi mein na padhein:** Paath shraddha aur shaanti se karein. Is stuti ko kisi dar ya zabardasti ki vajah se karne ki zaroorat nahi.
- **Kisi ke liye bura na sochein:** Paath ka bhav apne aur parivar ki raksha aur shaanti ka hona chahiye, kisi ko nuksan pahunchane ka nahi.
- **Parampara:** Kuch parivar is paath ke saath vishesh niyam (jaise vrat ya sankhya) rakhte hain. Apne bade ya pandit ji se poochhein.
- **Swasthya ki samasya:** Mansik ya shaareerik pareshani ke liye paath ke saath-saath chikitsak ki salaah zaroor lein. Paath upchar ka vikalp nahi hai.
- **Phal ki guarantee nahi:** Yeh site kisi vishesh phal ya parinaam ka vaada nahi karti.
