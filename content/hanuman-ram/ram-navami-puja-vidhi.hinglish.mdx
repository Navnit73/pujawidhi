Ram Navami Bhagwan Shri Ram ke janm ka utsav hai. Yeh **Chaitra mas ke Shukla paksha ki Navami tithi** ko manayi jaati hai. Parampara ke anusar Shri Ram ka janm madhyahna (din ke beech) mein hua tha, isliye puja ka mukhya samay aam taur par dopahar ka hota hai. Is saal ki tithi aur madhyahna muhurat apne shehar ke panchang se dekhein, kyunki samay shehar ke hisaab se badalta hai. Yeh page ghar ke liye saral vidhi aur sankshipt katha deta hai.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Taiyari">
    Subah snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Ghar aur puja sthaan ki safai karein. Chowki par kapda bichayein aur Ram darbar ko virajman karein. Kai parivar is din ghar ko phool ya toran se sajate hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aachman aur sankalp">
    Teen baar aachman karein. Diya jalayein. Haath mein jal, akshat aur phool lekar sankalp lein: "Main aaj Ram Navami par Shri Ram ji ki puja shraddha se kar raha/rahi hoon. Meri puja sweekar karein." Jal zameen par chhod dein. [Puja sankalp kaise lein](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) page par vistar hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ganesh smaran aur dhyan">
    Puja se pehle Ganesh ji ka smaran karein. Phir Ram ji ka dhyan karein.
    <Mantra dev={`रामाय रामभद्राय रामचन्द्राय वेधसे ।
रघुनाथाय नाथाय सीतायाः पतये नमः ॥`} rom={`Ramaya Ramabhadraya Ramachandraya vedhase
Raghunathaya nathaya Sitayah pataye namah`} arth="Ram, Ramabhadra, Ramachandra, srishti ke rachayita, Raghunath, sabke nath aur Sita ji ke pati ko mera pranam." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Avahan, snaan aur abhishek">
    Avahan karein: <Mantra dev="ॐ श्री रामाय नमः, आवाहयामि" rom="Om Shri Ramaya namah, aavahayami" arth="Main Shri Ram ji ka avahan (aamantran) karta/karti hoon." />
    Murti ho to jal se snaan karayein, chahein to panchamrit se abhishek karke saaf jal se phir snaan karayein. Tasveer ho to saaf kapde se halke se poochhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Vastra, chandan, phool aur tulsi">
    Vastra ya mauli arpit karein. Chandan aur tilak lagayein. Phool, haar aur tulsi dal chadhayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ram janm ka samay">
    Panchang se bataye madhyahna ke samay par Ram janm ka bhav karein. Prachlit hai ki us samay shankh, ghanti ya taali bajayi jaati hai aur "Bhaye prakat kripala deen dayala" ka path ya gaan hota hai. Kai ghar mein Ram ji ko palne mein jhulate hain. Yeh sab parivar ki parampara par nirbhar hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dhoop, deep, naivedya">
    Dhoop, agarbatti aur diya dikhayein. Kheer, panjiri, mithai ya phal ka bhog lagayein, tulsi dal ke saath.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paath aur jaap">
    Ramcharitmanas ke Ram janm prasang ya Ramayan ke kisi ansh ka paath karein. [Sunderkand](/hanuman-ram/sunderkand-path-vidhi) bhi prachlit hai. Ram naam ka jaap karein, jaise "Shri Ram Jai Ram Jai Jai Ram". Jaap ke liye 11, 21 ya 108 ki gintee prachlit hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aarti aur prasad">
    [Ram ji ki aarti](/hanuman-ram/ram-ji-ki-aarti) gayein, uske baad [Hanuman ji ki aarti](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-ji-ki-aarti). Aarti lekar prasad baantein. Ant mein kshama prarthana karein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Samay ka dhyan:** Ram janm ka muhurat madhyahna ka hai. Apne shehar ka sahi samay panchang se dekhein, kisi aur shehar ka samay na lein.
- **Vrat:** Vrat aur phalahar ke niyam parivar aur kshetra ke hisaab se alag hain. Swasthya ke hisaab se sochkar vrat rakhein, aur zaroorat ho to apne chikitsak aur bade se salaah lein.
- **Tulsi:** Ram puja aur bhog mein tulsi dal rakhne ki parampara hai.
- **Navratri se judav:** Ram Navami Chaitra Navratri ka antim din hota hai, jaisa ki panchang mein tithi anusar aata hai. Kuch parivar Navratri ke nau din Ramcharitmanas ka paath karte hain.
- **Samagri:** Jo na mile uski chinta na karein, jal, tulsi dal aur shraddha kaafi maani jaati hai.

## Mahatva aur katha

Parampara ke anusar Ayodhya ke raja Dasharath ne putra-prapti ke liye yagya karaya tha. Uske fal swaroop Chaitra Shukla Navami ko Rani Kaushalya ke yahan Shri Ram ka janm hua, aur Kaikeyi aur Sumitra ke putron, Bharat, Lakshman aur Shatrughn, ka janm bhi usi samay maana jaata hai. Shri Ram ko maryada purushottam kaha jaata hai, jo dharm, satya aur maryada ke pratik hain. Ramcharitmanas mein Ram janm ka varnan bhi milta hai, jise is din gaya jaata hai. Katha ke vistar mein Valmiki Ramayan aur Ramcharitmanas ke anusar kuch antar milte hain. Yeh page vishwas aur parampara ke baare mein jaankari ke liye hai.
