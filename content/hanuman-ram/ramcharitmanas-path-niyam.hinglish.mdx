Ramcharitmanas Goswami Tulsidas ji ki rachna hai aur bahut se ghar mein roz ya vishesh avsaron par iska paath hota hai. Yeh page ghar mein paath ke saamanya niyam batata hai. Niyam parivar, sampraday aur kshetra ke hisaab se alag ho sakte hain, isliye jahan sandeh ho wahan parivar ke bade ya pandit ji se poochhein. Yahan **prachlit roop** diya gaya hai.

## Ramcharitmanas kya hai

Ramcharitmanas Awadhi bhasha mein Shri Ram ki katha hai, jo sat kandon mein baanti gayi hai: Bal Kand, Ayodhya Kand, Aranya Kand, Kishkindha Kand, Sundar Kand, Lanka Kand aur Uttar Kand. Is page par poora text nahi diya gaya hai. **Is site par Ramcharitmanas ka verified text nahi hai.** Paath ke liye Gita Press jaisi kisi pramaanik chhapi pustak ka upyog karein.

## Ghar mein paath ke niyam

- **Pustak ka sthaan:** Pustak ko saaf aasan, chowki ya pustak-pith par rakhein, zameen par seedhe nahi. Paath ke baad use saaf kapde mein lapet kar surakshit rakhein.
- **Shuddhta:** Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Puja sthaan saaf rakhein. Mobile silent karein.
- **Aasan aur disha:** Saaf aasan par baithein. Aam taur par purv ya uttar ki taraf munh karna prachlit hai, par parivar ki parampara dekhein.
- **Shuruaat:** Diya jalayein, Ganesh ji aur guru ka smaran karein, Ram darbar ko pranam karein aur sankalp lein ([sankalp kaise lein](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein)). Kai gharon mein paath se pehle Ram ji ki chhoti puja ki jaati hai.
- **Paath ka tareeka:** Shabdon ko dheere aur saaf bolein. Doha-chaupai ka kram na badlein. Agar uchcharan par sandeh ho to pandit ji se poochhein.
- **Nirantarta:** Aam taur par baithkar ek hi baithak mein ek nishchit ansh padhne ki parampara hai. Beech mein zarurat par chhota vishram theek hai.
- **Samapti:** Paath ke ant mein [Ram ji ki aarti](/hanuman-ram/ram-ji-ki-aarti) aur [Hanuman ji ki aarti](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-ji-ki-aarti) gayein, prasad baantein aur kshama prarthana karein.

## Paath ke prachlit roop

- **Nitya paath:** Roz thoda ansh, jaise kuch doha-chaupai padhna. Koi tay sankhya nahi.
- **Navah parayan:** Nau din mein poore Ramcharitmanas ka paath. Aam taur par Navratri ya Ram Navami ke aas-paas kiya jaata hai. Ise Navahn parayan bhi kehte hain. Din-wise vibhajan alag-alag pustakon mein diya hota hai.
- **Masparayan:** Ek mahine mein poora paath, jisme har din ka ansh nishchit hota hai.
- **Akhand paath:** Bina ruke paath. Iske baare mein [Akhand Ramayan path](/hanuman-ram/akhand-ramayan-path-vidhi-kharcha) page dekhein.
- **Sunderkand paath:** Sirf paanchve kand ka paath. [Sunderkand path vidhi](/hanuman-ram/sunderkand-path-vidhi) dekhein.

Din-wise vibhajan ke liye apni pustak ki suchi ya pandit ji ki salaah lein, kyunki alag chhapi pustakon mein vibhajan alag ho sakta hai.

## Aam galtiyan

- **Jaldbaazi:** Gati badhane ke liye shabd na chhodein. Shuddhta aur bhav mahatvapurn hain.
- **Pustak ka anadar:** Pustak ko zameen par ya jhoothe haath se na rakhein.
- **Ek hi jagah par ruke bina paath ko zaroori maan lena:** Swasthya ya zaroori kaam ke liye uthna pade to uthna theek hai. Sakht niyam sabhi parampara mein nahi hain.
- **Phal ki guarantee:** Paath se kisi vishesh phal ki guarantee nahi di ja sakti. Yeh bhakti aur parampara ka vishay hai.

## Srot aur bhinntayein
