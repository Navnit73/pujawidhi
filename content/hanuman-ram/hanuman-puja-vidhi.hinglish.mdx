Hanuman ji ki puja ghar mein aam taur par **Mangalvar** aur **Shanivar** ko ki jaati hai, haalaanki bahut se bhakt roz bhi karte hain. Yeh page ek saral ghar-ki-puja ki vidhi deta hai. Alag-alag parivaron aur kshetron ki paddhati mein antar hota hai, isliye jo aapke yahan chalta ho wahi follow karein. Yahan **prachlit roop** diya gaya hai.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Taiyari">
    Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Puja ki jagah saaf karein, chowki par laal kapda bichayein aur Hanuman ji ko virajman karein. Apne liye saaf aasan par baithein. Puja karte samay aam taur par purv ya uttar ki taraf munh karna prachlit hai, par parivar ki parampara dekhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aachman aur sankalp">
    Daayein haath mein jal lekar teen baar aachman karein. Phir diya jalayein. Haath mein jal, akshat aur phool lekar saral sankalp lein: "Main aaj Shri Hanuman ji ki puja shraddha se kar raha/rahi hoon. Meri puja sweekar karein." Phir jal zameen par chhod dein. Sankalp ke vistar ke liye [puja sankalp kaise lein](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) dekhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dhyan">
    Haath jodkar Hanuman ji ka dhyan karein.
    <Mantra dev={`मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम् ।
वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शिरसा नमामि ॥`} rom={`Manojavam marutatulyavegam jitendriyam buddhimatam varishtham
Vatatmajam vanarayuthamukhyam Shri-Ramadootam shirasa namami`} arth="Jo man ke samaan tez aur vayu ke samaan vegwaan hain, jitendriya hain, buddhimanon mein shreshth hain, Pawan ke putra aur vanar-yuthon ke pradhan hain, un Shri Ram ke doot ko main sir jhukakar pranam karta hoon." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Avahan, aasan aur snaan">
    Avahan karein: <Mantra dev="ॐ श्री हनुमते नमः, आवाहयामि" rom="Om Shri Hanumate namah, aavahayami" arth="Main Shri Hanuman ji ka avahan (aamantran) karta/karti hoon." />
    Murti ho to jal se snaan karayein (chahein to panchamrit se abhishek ke baad phir saaf jal se) aur saaf kapde se poochhein. Tasveer ho to saaf kapde se halke se poochh lein. Har upchar ke saath "Om Shri Hanumate namah" bol sakte hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Vastra, sindoor aur phool">
    Laal vastra ya mauli arpit karein. Sindoor (tel mein milakar) ek-do baar lagayein. Laal phool aur haar chadhayein. Roli aur akshat ka tilak karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dhoop, deep aur naivedya">
    Dhoop aur agarbatti dikhayein, phir diya. Laddu, gud-chana ya phal ka bhog lagayein, saath mein tulsi dal rakhein aur thoda jal arpit karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paath">
    Shraddha anusar [Hanuman Chalisa](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-chalisa) ka paath karein. Samay ho to [Hanuman Ashtak](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-ashtak-sankat-mochan) ya [Sunderkand](/hanuman-ram/sunderkand-path-vidhi) bhi padh sakte hain. Chahein to "Om Shri Hanumate namah" ka jaap karein. Jaap ke liye 11, 21 ya 108 ki gintee prachlit hai, par koi tay niyam nahi.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aarti">
    Kapoor ya ghee ki batti se thali ghumate hue [Hanuman ji ki aarti](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-ji-ki-aarti) gayein. Ghanti bajayein. Aarti lekar prasad baantein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pradakshina aur kshama prarthana">
    Apni jagah par teen baar ghoom kar pradakshina karein aur ant mein kshama prarthana karein.
    <Mantra dev={`आवाहनं न जानामि न जानामि विसर्जनम् ।
पूजां चैव न जानामि क्षमस्व परमेश्वर ॥`} rom={`Aavahanam na jaanami na jaanami visarjanam
Poojam chaiva na jaanami kshamasva Parameshwar`} arth="Main na avahan jaanta hoon, na visarjan, na puja ki vidhi. Mujhse jo bhool hui ho use kshama karein." />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Shuddhta aur satvik bhav:** Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Kai parivar Mangalvar ya Shanivar ko satvik bhojan aur sanyam rakhte hain. Yeh vyaktigat shraddha ka vishay hai.
- **Vrat:** Mangalvar vrat rakhne ki parampara hai, par vrat ke niyam parivar aur kshetra ke hisaab se alag hain. Apne parivar ke bade ya pandit ji se poochhein. Swasthya ki sthiti ho to vrat sochkar rakhein.
- **Sindoor aur tel:** Prachlit roop mein tel mein milakar sindoor chadhaya jaata hai. Agar aap alag tareeka maante hain to wahi karein.
- **Mahilayein aur murti-sparsh:** Kuch parampara mein Hanuman ji ki murti ko mahilaon ke sparsh ke baare mein alag niyam hain, jabki kai gharon mein koi rok nahi hai. Yeh kul-parampara ka vishay hai, apne bade se poochhein. Paath, jaap aur aarti sab shraddha se kar sakte hain.
- **Tulsi:** Hanuman ji ke bhog mein tulsi dal rakhne ki parampara hai.
- **Samagri ki fikr:** Na ho to jal, phool aur shraddha kaafi maani jaati hai.

## Mahatva

Hanuman ji ko Shri Ram ka param bhakt, bal, buddhi aur seva ka pratik maana jaata hai. Unhe "Sankat Mochan" bhi kaha jaata hai. Mangalvar aur Shanivar ko unki puja ki parampara kai kshetron mein hai. Is page ka uddeshya parampara aur shraddha ke baare mein jaankari dena hai. Yeh site kisi phal ya parinaam ka vaada nahi karti.
