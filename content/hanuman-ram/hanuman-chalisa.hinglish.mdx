## Arth ka saar

Chalisa ke shuruaati dohe mein Guru ka smaran aur Hanuman ji se bal, buddhi aur vidya ki prarthana hai. Pehli chaupai se lekar lagbhag 11vi chaupai tak Hanuman ji ke swaroop, gun aur Ram-kaaj (Sita ji ko khojna, Lanka dahan, Sanjeevani) ka varnan hai. Aage Sugriv aur Vibhishan par unke upkar, bal-leela ka Surya wala prasang aur Ram ke dwar ke rakhwale roop ka zikr hai. Beech ke pad bhakt ki raksha aur sankat-mochan ke vishwas ke hain. Ant ki chaupaiyon mein phalashruti, yaani paath ka phal, aur Tulsidas ji ki apne hriday mein vaas ki prarthana hai. Antim doha mein Ram-Lakshman-Sita sahit Hanuman ji se hriday mein basne ki vinati hai.

## Paath kab aur kaise karein

- **Kab:** Aam taur par subah snaan ke baad. Mangalvar aur Shanivar ko kai parivar vishesh roop se paath karte hain. Roz ek baar bhi paath kiya ja sakta hai.
- **Kaise:** Saaf kapde pehnein, Hanuman ji ya Ram darbar ke saamne diya jalayein, saaf aasan par baith kar paath karein. Chahein to pehle sankalp aur ek chhoti puja karein ([Hanuman puja vidhi](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-puja-vidhi)). Paath ke baad [Hanuman ji ki aarti](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-ji-ki-aarti) gayein.
- **Kitni baar:** Koi tay sankhya nahi hai. Ek baar, 3, 7, 11 ya 108 baar, jo shraddha aur samay se ban sake. Chalisa mein "sat baar" ka zikr hai, par ise kisi niyam ki tarah thopna zaroori nahi.
- **Uchcharan:** Shabdon ko dheere aur saaf bolein. Agar kisi shabd par shanka ho to kisi bade ya pandit ji se sunkar theek kar lein.
- **Mahilayein aur bachche:** Hanuman Chalisa ka paath koi bhi shraddha se kar sakta hai. Murti-sparsh ya puja ke kuch niyam parivar ki parampara par nirbhar karte hain. Apne parivar ke bade se poochh lein.
