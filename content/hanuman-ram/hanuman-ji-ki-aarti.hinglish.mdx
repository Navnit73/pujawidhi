## Arth

- **Hanuman lala, dusht dalan:** Hanuman ji ki aarti karein, jo dushton ka daman karne waale aur Raghunath (Ram) ki shakti ka roop maane jaate hain.
- **Jake bal se girivar kanpe:** Jinke bal se parvat bhi kaanp jaate hain, aur jinke paas rog-dosh jaise kasht aane se jhijhakte hain, yeh bhakton ka vishwas hai.
- **Anjani putra:** Anjani ke putra, bal dene waale aur santon ke sadaa sahayak.
- **Bida, Lanka, Sita sudhi:** Ram ji ka kaam ka bida (zimmedari) lekar Lanka gaye, use jalaya aur Sita ji ki khabar laye. Samudra jaisi khai aur kile jaisi Lanka mein pravesh karne mein unhe der nahi lagi.
- **Lakshman murchhit, Sanjivan:** Lakshman ji ke murchhit hone par Sanjeevani lakar unke pran bachaye.
- **Ahiravan:** Patal mein pravesh karke Ahiravan ka ant kiya (Ahiravan ka prasang Ramayan ki lokpriya kathao mein milta hai).
- **Baayin bhuja, daahine bhuja:** Ek bhuja se asur-dal ka sanhar aur doosri se santon ki raksha ka bhav.
- **Sur nar muni aarti utaren:** Dev, manushya aur muni sabhi aarti utarte hain aur "Jai Hanuman" bolte hain.
- **Anjana mai:** Kanchan ke thaal mein kapoor ki lau se Anjana mata aarti karti hain, aisa varnan hai.
- **Antim pad:** Jo shraddha se yeh aarti gata hai, uske liye param pad ki prarthana aur vishwas hai.

## Aarti kab aur kaise gayein

- **Kab:** Puja ke ant mein, Hanuman Chalisa ya Sunderkand ke baad. Mangalvar aur Shanivar ki subah ya shaam ko prachlit hai. Hanuman Jayanti par bhi gayi jaati hai ([Hanuman Jayanti puja vidhi](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-jayanti-puja-vidhi)).
- **Kaise:** Diya jalayein, phir kapoor ya ghee ki batti se thali ko Hanuman ji ke saamne ghumate hue aarti karein. Ghanti bajana prachlit hai. Aarti ke baad haath se aarti lekar aankhon par lagayein aur prasad baantein.
- **Kitni baar:** Koi tay sankhya nahi. Ek baar poori aarti kaafi hai.
- **Puri puja:** Poori vidhi ke liye [Hanuman puja vidhi](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-puja-vidhi) dekhein. Aur aartiyan [Aarti Sangrah](/puja-basics/aarti-sangrah) mein hain.
