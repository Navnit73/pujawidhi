## Paath ka swaroop

Ashtak ka matlab hai aath padon wali rachna. Is stuti mein aath pad hote hain, aur har pad Hanuman ji ke kisi prasiddh prasang ko yaad karta hai. Aam taur par uske saath ek ant ka doha bhi gaya jaata hai. Har pad ke ant mein dohrayi jaane wali pankti ka bhav hai ki "sansar mein koi aisa nahi jo aapke Sankat Mochan naam ko na jaanta ho." Bhasha Awadhi-mishrit Hindi hai.

## Arth (meaning) ka saar

Yeh apne shabdon mein saar hai, line-by-line anuvad nahi:

- **Bal-leela:** Bachpan mein Hanuman ji ne Surya ko phal samajhkar nigalne ka prayas kiya, jisse sansar mein andhera ho gaya aur devtaon ne unse prarthana ki.
- **Sugriv ki sahayata:** Hanuman ji ne Sugriv ko Ram se milwaya aur unke sankat door karne mein madad ki.
- **Sita ji ki khoj:** Ram ji ki mudrika lekar Hanuman ji Sita ji ki khoj mein gaye.
- **Lanka ka prasang:** Lanka mein unka pravesh, Sita ji se bhent aur Lanka dahan.
- **Lakshman ke liye Sanjeevani:** Lakshman ji ke murchhit hone par Sanjeevani buti lakar unke pran bachana.
- **Ahiravan ka prasang:** Hanuman ji ne Ram aur Lakshman ko Ahiravan ke bandhan se bachaya.

Har pad ka bhav yeh hai ki jab devta ya Ram jaise param bhi sankat mein pade, tab Hanuman ji ne hi sahara diya, isliye bhakt apne sankat mein bhi unse prarthana karta hai. Pad-wise arth ke liye kisi pramaanik tika wali pustak ka sahara lein.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

- **Kab:** Mangalvar aur Shanivar prachlit hain. Roz bhi koi padh sakta hai. Subah ya shaam ka samay aam hai.
- **Taiyari:** Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein, Hanuman ji ke saamne diya jalayein aur saaf aasan par baithein.
- **Kitni baar:** Ek baar ka paath aam hai. Kuch log 3, 5, 11 baar padhne ka sankalp lete hain, jo vyaktigat shraddha ka vishay hai, koi anivarya niyam nahi.
- **Kram:** Aam taur par [Hanuman Chalisa](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-chalisa) ke saath ya uske baad padha jaata hai, aur ant mein [Hanuman ji ki aarti](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-ji-ki-aarti) hoti hai. Poori puja ke liye [Hanuman puja vidhi](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-puja-vidhi) dekhein.
- **Uchcharan:** Shabdon ko dheere aur saaf padhein. Sandeh ho to pandit ji ya bade se poochhein.
