Akhand Ramayan path mein Ramcharitmanas ka paath bina ruke, ek ke baad ek paathak badalte hue, nirantar kiya jaata hai. Ise aam taur par ghar mein kisi shubh avsar par karwaya jaata hai, jaise griha pravesh, janmadin, vivah ya manokamna poorn hone par abhaar prakat karne ke liye. Yeh page batata hai ki akhand paath kya hai, kaise hota hai, kin baaton ki taiyari chahiye aur kharche ko kaise samjhein. Isme koi nishchit rakam nahi di gayi hai kyunki kharcha shehar, paathak mandali aur aayojan par nirbhar karta hai. Yahan **prachlit roop** diya gaya hai.

## Akhand Ramayan path kya hai

Isme Ramcharitmanas ke saaton kand ka paath nirantar hota hai. Aam taur par isme lagbhag ek din aur ek raat ya usse kuch zyada samay lagta hai, jo paathakon ki gati par nirbhar hai. Paath ke dauran Ramcharitmanas ki pustak par diya aur jyoti jalti rehti hai aur paathak baari-baari se padhte hain. Is page par poora text nahi diya gaya hai. **Ramcharitmanas ka verified text is site par nahi hai**; paath Gita Press jaisi pramaanik pustak se karein.

## Vidhi (kram)

1. **Muhurat aur taiyari:** Paath shuru karne ka din aur samay [panchang](/puja-basics/aaj-ka-panchang-shubh-muhurat) ya pandit ji se tay karein. Paathak ya paathak mandali ko pehle se nimantrit karein aur baari-baari ka kram tay kar lein.
2. **Sthapana:** Puja sthaan saaf karke Ram darbar ko virajman karein. Kalash sthapit karein, diya jalayein.
3. **Sankalp aur chhoti puja:** Ganesh ji, guru aur Ram darbar ka pujan karke sankalp lein ([sankalp kaise lein](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein)).
4. **Paath shuru:** Pehla paathak pustak ko pranam karke Bal Kand se paath shuru karta hai. Paathak baari-baari se badalte hain, par paath ka silsila na toote, yeh dhyan rakha jaata hai.
5. **Beech ki dekhbhaal:** Diya jalta rahe, shuddhta bani rahe aur paathak aaram se baith kar padhein. Raat mein bhi paath chalta hai, isliye prakash aur baithne ki vyavastha rakhein.
6. **Samapti:** Uttar Kand ke ant par paath poora hota hai. Uske baad [Ram ji ki aarti](/hanuman-ram/ram-ji-ki-aarti) aur [Hanuman ji ki aarti](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-ji-ki-aarti), prasad, aur parampara ho to havan aur bhojan hota hai.
7. **Dakshina:** Paathakon ya pandit ji ko apni shraddha anusar dakshina aur samman dein.

## Kharcha: kin baaton par nirbhar karta hai

Yahan koi nishchit rakam nahi di gayi hai, kyunki yeh bahut si baaton par nirbhar karta hai:

- **Paathak mandali ya pandit ji:** Kya aap parivar ke sadasyon se paath karwa rahe hain ya bahar ki mandali bula rahe hain. Mandali ki sankhya aur unki apni dakshina alag ho sakti hai.
- **Shehar aur sthaan:** Alag shehar mein kharche alag hote hain.
- **Samay aur avadhi:** Paath ki avadhi aur kitne paathak lagenge.
- **Samagri aur sajavat:** Diya, ghee, phool, prasad aur sajavat par kitna karna hai.
- **Bhojan aur bhandara:** Agar paath ke baad bhojan ya prasad vitran rakhna hai to mehmanon ki sankhya par.
- **Sound aur vyavastha:** Mic, tent ya baithne ki vyavastha.

**Kaise poochhein:** Pehle se saaf baat karein ki dakshina aur anya kharche mein kya-kya shamil hai, aur kisi se bhi likhit ya spasht baat kar lein. Alag-alag logon se poochh kar tulna karein. Dakshina ke baare mein aur jaankari ke liye [puja ka kharcha aur pandit dakshina](/puja-basics/puja-ka-kharcha-pandit-dakshina) dekhein. Shraddha hi mukhya hai, bade aayojan ki bajaye saral ghar ka paath bhi poora maana jaata hai.

## Aam galtiyan

- **Paathak ki kami:** Raat ke samay paathak kam ho sakte hain. Pehle se baari tay karein.
- **Diya bujhna:** Surakshit jagah par aur zaroori batti ki vyavastha rakhein, aag se sambandhit savdhani barten.
- **Kharche par spasht baat na karna:** Pehle hi tay kar lein.
- **Phal ki guarantee:** Paath se kisi vishesh phal ki guarantee nahi di ja sakti. Yeh shraddha aur parampara ka vishay hai.
