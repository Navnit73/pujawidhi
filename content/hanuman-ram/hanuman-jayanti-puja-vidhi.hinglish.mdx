Hanuman Jayanti Hanuman ji ke janm-utsav ke roop mein manayi jaati hai. Uttar Bharat mein yeh aam taur par **Chaitra mas ki Purnima** ko manayi jaati hai, jabki dakshin Bharat ke kuch kshetron mein aur kuch anya parampara mein ise doosri tithiyon par manaya jaata hai. Isliye is saal ki tithi aur puja ka samay apne shehar ke panchang se hi dekhein. Yeh page ghar ke liye ek saral vidhi deta hai.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Taiyari">
    Subah snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Ghar ya mandir ki safai karein. Chowki par laal kapda bichayein aur Hanuman ji ko virajman karein. Chahein to Ram darbar ki tasveer bhi saath rakhein, kyunki Hanuman ji Ram ke param bhakt maane jaate hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sankalp">
    Diya jalayein. Haath mein jal, akshat aur phool lekar sankalp lein: "Main aaj Hanuman Jayanti par Shri Hanuman ji ki puja shraddha se kar raha/rahi hoon. Meri puja sweekar karein." Jal zameen par chhod dein. Vistar ke liye [puja sankalp kaise lein](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) dekhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dhyan aur avahan">
    <Mantra dev={`मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम् ।
वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शिरसा नमामि ॥`} rom={`Manojavam marutatulyavegam jitendriyam buddhimatam varishtham
Vatatmajam vanarayuthamukhyam Shri-Ramadootam shirasa namami`} arth="Jo man ke samaan tez aur vayu ke samaan vegwaan hain, jitendriya aur buddhimanon mein shreshth hain, un Shri Ram ke doot Pawan-putra ko main sir jhukakar pranam karta hoon." />
    Phir avahan: <Mantra dev="ॐ श्री हनुमते नमः, आवाहयामि" rom="Om Shri Hanumate namah, aavahayami" arth="Main Shri Hanuman ji ka avahan karta/karti hoon." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Snaan, vastra aur sindoor">
    Murti ho to jal se snaan karayein aur saaf kapde se poochhein. Laal vastra ya mauli arpit karein. Sindoor (tel mein milakar) lagayein, roli-akshat ka tilak karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Phool, dhoop, deep, naivedya">
    Laal phool aur haar chadhayein. Dhoop, agarbatti aur diya dikhayein. Laddu, gud-chana ya phal ka bhog lagayein, tulsi dal rakhein aur jal arpit karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paath">
    Shraddha aur samay anusar [Hanuman Chalisa](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-chalisa) ka paath karein. Kai log is din [Sunderkand](/hanuman-ram/sunderkand-path-vidhi) ya [Hanuman Ashtak](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-ashtak-sankat-mochan) ka bhi paath karte hain. Ram naam ka jaap, jaise "Shri Ram Jai Ram Jai Jai Ram", bhi prachlit hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aarti aur prasad">
    [Hanuman ji ki aarti](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-ji-ki-aarti) gayein, chahein to ant mein [Ram ji ki aarti](/hanuman-ram/ram-ji-ki-aarti) bhi. Aarti lekar prasad baantein. Pass-padosi aur parivar mein prasad baantna prachlit hai.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Tithi ko lekar bhram:** Hanuman Jayanti ki tithi alag-alag kshetron aur parampara mein alag hai. Apne kshetra ki parampara aur panchang ke hisaab se din chunein.
- **Bheed:** Mandir mein bheed ho sakti hai. Samay se pehle jayein aur dhairya rakhein. Ghar par puja bhi poori maani jaati hai.
- **Vrat:** Vrat rakhna ya na rakhna parivar ki parampara aur apni sehat par nirbhar hai. Jinhe koi swasthya samasya ho wo apne chikitsak aur bade se salaah lein.
- **Shuddhta:** Snaan karke saaf kapde, shaant man aur shraddha mukhya hain.
- **Samagri:** Jo na mile uski chinta na karein. Jal, phool aur shraddha kaafi maani jaati hai.

## Mahatva aur katha

Hanuman ji ko Pawan-putra, Anjani-nandan aur Ram-bhakt kaha jaata hai. Ramayan mein unki seva, bal, buddhi aur nishtha ke prasang prasiddh hain, jaise Sita ji ki khoj aur Lanka mein unka pravesh. Hanuman Jayanti par bhakt unke in gunon ka smaran karte hain. Alag-alag kshetron mein is din se judi katha aur manyatayen alag ho sakti hain, isliye yeh page vishwas aur parampara ke baare mein jaankari ke liye hai.
