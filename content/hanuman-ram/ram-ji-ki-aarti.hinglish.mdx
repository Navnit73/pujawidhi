Ram ji ki sabse prachlit aarti-stuti **"Shri Ramchandra Kripalu bhaju man"** hai, jo Ram Navami, Ram-darbar ki puja aur Ramayan ya Sunderkand paath ke ant mein gayi jaati hai. Yeh Goswami Tulsidas ji ki rachna maani jaati hai (Vinay Patrika se). Neeche iske pehle paanch pad Devanagari aur Hinglish mein, arth ke saath, diye gaye hain. Mandir mein gaate samay upar **Reader mode** kaam aa sakta hai.

## Arth ka saar

Is stuti mein Ram ji ke roop ka sundar varnan hai: kamal jaise netra, mukh, haath aur charan, megh jaisa shyam varn aur bijli jaisa peetambar. Unhe deenbandhu, Suryavansh ke tilak aur Dashrath-nandan kaha gaya hai. Ant ke pad mein Tulsidas ji prarthana karte hain ki Ram ji unke hriday-kamal mein nivas karein aur kaam-krodh jaise vikaron ko nasht karein.

## Aarti kab aur kaise gayein

- **Kab:** Ram Navami par ([Ram Navami puja vidhi](/hanuman-ram/ram-navami-puja-vidhi)), Ram-darbar ki roz ki puja mein, aur Ramcharitmanas ya [Sunderkand](/hanuman-ram/sunderkand-path-vidhi) paath ke ant mein.
- **Kaise:** Ram, Sita, Lakshman aur Hanuman ji ke saamne diya jalayein. Kapoor ya ghee ki batti se thali ghumate hue stuti gayein. Ghanti ya taali bajana prachlit hai. Ant mein aarti lekar prasad baantein.
- **Kitni baar:** Koi tay sankhya nahi. Ek baar kaafi hai.
- **Aur bhi:** Ram ji ke baad [Hanuman ji ki aarti](/hanuman-ram/hanuman-ji-ki-aarti) gane ki parampara kai gharon mein hai.
