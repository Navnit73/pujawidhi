Durga Saptashati, jise **Devi Mahatmya** ya **Chandi** bhi kehte hain, Maa Durga ki mahima ka sabse pramukh granth hai. Ismein 13 adhyay aur lagbhag 700 shlok hain (isliye "sapta-shati", yaani saat sau). Yeh Markandeya Purana ka ek bhaag maana jaata hai. Yeh page 13 adhyay ka saar, path ka kram aur path ke niyam batata hai. Poora Sanskrit text is page par nahi diya gaya hai. Ise Gita Press jaisi pramanik chhapi hui pustak se padhein.

## Arth (meaning): 13 adhyay ka saar

Saptashati teen charitron mein baanti jaati hai. Neeche adhyay-wise saar apne shabdon mein diya gaya hai:

**Prathama charitra: Mahakali**
1. **Adhyay 1:** Raja Suratha aur Samadhi vaishya ek rishi ke paas pahunchte hain. Rishi Madhu-Kaitabh ki katha sunate hain, jinhe Vishnu ne Devi Yoganidra ke prabhav se jaagkar maara.

**Madhyama charitra: Mahalakshmi**

2. **Adhyay 2:** Mahishasur devtaon par vijay pa leta hai. Devtaon ke tej se ek mahashakti prakat hoti hain.
3. **Adhyay 3:** Mahishasur aur uski senaon ke saath Devi ka yuddh, aur Mahishasur ka vadh.
4. **Adhyay 4:** Vijay ke baad devtaon dwara Devi ki stuti.

**Uttama charitra: Mahasaraswati**

5. **Adhyay 5:** Shumbh aur Nishumbh asur Devi ke saundarya ka sun kar unhe paane ka prayas karte hain. Devtaon dwara Devi ki stuti hoti hai aur dut Devi se baat karne aate hain.
6. **Adhyay 6:** Senapati Dhumralochan ka vadh.
7. **Adhyay 7:** Chand aur Mund ka vadh, jisse Devi ka naam Chamunda pada.
8. **Adhyay 8:** Raktabeej ke saath yuddh. Devi ke saath matrikaon aur Kali ka roop bhi yuddh mein aata hai.
9. **Adhyay 9:** Nishumbh ka vadh.
10. **Adhyay 10:** Shumbh ka vadh.
11. **Adhyay 11:** Devtaon dwara Narayani stuti aur Devi ka aashirvad.
12. **Adhyay 12:** Devi Mahatmya ke path ke mahatva ka varnan (phalashruti).
13. **Adhyay 13:** Suratha aur Samadhi ko Devi ka darshan aur varadaan.

Adhyay-wise yeh saar sankshipt hai. Kisi adhyay ki vistrit katha ke liye chhapi hui pustak ya pandit ji ka sahara lein.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

- **Kab:** Navratri mein path sabse prachlit hai. Ashtami ya Navami par bhi bahut se parivar karte hain. Tithi apne panchang se dekhein.
- **Kaise:** Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Devi ki murti ya kalash ke saamne diya jalayein aur sankalp lein. Saaf aasan par baithein, purv ya uttar ki taraf munh karna prachlit hai. Poori puja ka kram [ghar par Durga Puja vidhi](/durga-maa/durga-puja-at-home-vidhi) mein hai.
- **Kram:** Prachlit kram mein Devi Kavach, Argala, Keelak, Navarna jaap, Ratri Sukta, phir 13 adhyay, aur ant mein Devi Sukta aur Rahasyatray padhe jaate hain. Kuch parampara mein kram alag hota hai. Is vishay mein [Chandi Path vidhi](/durga-maa/chandi-path-vidhi) vistar se batata hai.
- **Kitna:** Koi anivarya sankhya nahi hai. Kai parivar nau dinon mein baant kar padhte hain. Kuch log ek hi din mein poora path karte hain, jisme kai ghante lagte hain. Baantne ka tareeka parampara ke anusar alag hai.
- **Asan aur shuddhi:** Saaf aasan, saaf vastra, aur shaant mann.
