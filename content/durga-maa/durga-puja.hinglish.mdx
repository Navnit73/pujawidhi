Durga Puja Maa Durga ki aaradhana ka bada utsav hai. Isme Devi ko Mahishasuramardini roop mein poojte hain, jinhone devtaon ko asur ke bhay se mukt kiya. Yeh tyohaar sabse zyada Bengal mein jaana jaata hai, par Odisha, Assam, Tripura, Bihar, Jharkhand aur Delhi, Mumbai, Lucknow jaise shehron ke Bengali samajon mein bhi dhoomdhaam se manaya jaata hai. Yeh page tyohaar ka itihas, uski prachlit manyata aur paanch dinon ka saar saral bhasha mein batata hai.

## Durga Puja ka itihas aur manyata

Durga Puja ki jad **Devi Mahatmya** (Durga Saptashati) mein milti hai, jo Markandeya Purana ka ek bhaag maana jaata hai. Usme katha hai ki Mahishasur naam ke asur ne varadaan ke bal par devtaon ko swarg se nikaal diya. Tab sabhi devtaon ke tej se ek mahashakti prakat hui, jinhe sabne apne-apne astra diye aur jinhone Mahishasur ka vadh kiya. Isi vijay ka smaran Durga Puja mein hota hai.

Bengal ki parampara mein ek aur katha prachlit hai: Bhagwan Ram ne Ravan se yuddh se pehle shaktipuja ki, jo vasant ki jagah sharad ritu mein hone ke kaaran **akal bodhan** kahlayi. Is katha ka ullekh Krittivasi Ramayan jaisi Bengali parampara mein milta hai. Alag-alag kshetron mein alag kathayein suni jaati hain, isliye apne parivar ya kshetra ki manyata ko mahatva dein.

Bengal mein Maa ko **beti** ki tarah bhi dekha jaata hai, jo in dinon apne maika aati hain. Isiliye pratima mein Durga ji ke saath unki santan, yaani Ganesh, Kartikeya, Lakshmi aur Saraswati, bhi dikhaye jaate hain. 2021 mein "Kolkata ki Durga Puja" ko UNESCO ki Intangible Cultural Heritage list mein shamil kiya gaya, jo is utsav ki sanskritik pehchaan ka praman hai.

## Paanch dinon ka sankshipt kram

Tyohaar ki shuruaat aam taur par **Mahalaya** se maani jaati hai, jab pitron ko tarpan diya jaata hai aur Devi ke aagman ka swar goonjta hai. Mukhya puja Shashthi se Dashami tak hoti hai:

- **Shashthi:** Devi ka bodhan aur aamantran, pratima ki pran-pratishtha ka aarambh.
- **Saptami:** Nabapatrika (kola bou) snaan aur sthapana, mukhya puja ka aarambh.
- **Ashtami:** Pushpanjali, kumari puja aur sandhi puja ka din.
- **Navami:** Mahapuja, havan aur bhog ka din.
- **Dashami:** Sindoor khela aur pratima ka visarjan.

Har din ka vistaar [Durga Puja ke 5 din](/durga-maa/durga-puja-5-din-shashthi-dashami) page par hai. Ashtami ki vidhi ke liye [Maha Ashtami puja vidhi](/durga-maa/maha-ashtami-puja-vidhi) aur kanya pujan ke liye [Kumari puja vidhi](/durga-maa/kumari-puja-kanya-pujan-vidhi) dekhein.

## Ghar ki puja aur pandal ki puja

Pandal wali puja samudaayik hoti hai, jisme pratima, sajaavat, dhaak aur aarti ka bada aayojan hota hai. Ghar mein log kalash sthapana karke, chhoti murti ya tasveer ke saamne saral vidhi se puja karte hain. Ghar ke liye [ghar par Durga Puja vidhi](/durga-maa/durga-puja-at-home-vidhi) aur saaman ke liye [Durga Puja samagri list](/durga-maa/durga-puja-samagri-list) kaam aayegi. Pandal dekhne ka plan banane ke liye [pandal near me guide](/durga-maa/durga-puja-pandal-near-me) padhein. Pratima kaisi chunein, iske liye [pratima guide](/durga-maa/durga-pratima-murti-guide) hai.

## Navratri aur Durga Puja ka sambandh

Uttar Bharat mein log nau din vrat rakhkar kalash sthapana, nau Durga roopon ki puja aur Ashtami ya Navami par kanya pujan karte hain. Bengal mein mukhya utsav Shashthi se Dashami tak hota hai. Dono parampara ek hi Sharad Navratri kaal mein aati hain aur Dashami par Vijayadashami (Dussehra) bhi manayi jaati hai. Nau roopon ki jaankari ke liye [Nau Durga ke 9 roop](/navratri-devi/nau-durga-9-roop) padhein.

## Tithi kaise pata karein

Durga Puja ki tithiyan chandra panchang par chalti hain aur har saal badalti hain. Kabhi-kabhi kisi tithi ka kshay ya vriddhi hone se Ashtami-Navami ka sandhi samay alag-alag shehron mein alag padta hai. Isliye is page par koi tay tarikh nahi di gayi hai. Apne shehar ke panchang ya [aaj ka panchang](/puja-basics/aaj-ka-panchang-shubh-muhurat) se tithi dekhein, aur pooja ke liye apne pandit ji se poochhein. Saal bhar ke tyohaar [Hindu festival calendar](/puja-basics/hindu-festival-calendar-2027) mein bhi dekh sakte hain.
