## Durga pratima ke prachlit roop

Mahishasuramardini roop mein Devi sher ya singh par virajman, haathon mein alag-alag astra liye aur Mahishasur ka vadh karte hue dikhayi jaati hain. Bengal ki **ek-chala** pratima mein Maa ke saath Ganesh, Kartikeya, Lakshmi aur Saraswati ek hi frame mein hote hain. Ghar ki puja ke liye bahut se parivar sirf Maa ki murti ya tasveer rakhte hain. Dono roop prachlit hain, isliye apni parampara ya suvidha ke anusar chunein. Alag-alag kshetron mein bhujaon ki sankhya aur shringar mein antar hota hai.

## Material kaisa chunein

- **Mitti (clay):** Parampara se Bengal mein nadi ki mitti se pratima banti hai. Ghar mein visarjan ke liye yeh suvidhajanak hai kyunki jal mein ghul jaati hai.
- **Plaster of Paris:** Jal mein dheere ghulti hai aur rang paani ko pradushit kar sakte hain. Isliye bahut se shehron mein iske vikalp apnaye ja rahe hain. Apne shehar ke niyam dekh lein.
- **Dhaatu (peetal, panchdhatu):** Ghar mein sthayi roop se rakhne ke liye. Aise murti ka visarjan nahi hota, uski niyamit saaf-safai aur puja hoti hai.
- **Prakritik rang:** Agar rang ka vikalp ho to pani ya haldi jaise prakritik rang pasand karein.

Craft ya thermocol se pratima banane ke vichaar ke liye [thermocol Durga murti craft ideas](/local/thermocol-durga-murti-craft-ideas) dekh sakte hain. Madhyam saaf rakhne ki soch ke saath mitti ki murti ke liye [eco-friendly mitti Ganesh murti](/local/eco-friendly-mitti-ganesh-murti) page bhi madadgaar hai.

## Murti chunte samay dhyan dene yogya baatein

1. **Khandit na ho:** Murti mein daraar ya tuta hua hissa na ho.
2. **Chehre ka bhaav:** Devi ka mukh shant, saumya aur sundar ho. Yeh vyaktigat pasand ka vishay hai.
3. **Aadhar majboot ho:** Murti seedhi aur sthir khadi ho, chowki par hile nahi.
4. **Size:** Ghar mein sthapana aur visarjan ke liye uthane layak size ho.
5. **Shringar:** Chunri, mukut aur abhushan alag se bhi lagaye ja sakte hain, isliye bhaari saaz-sajja zaroori nahi.

## Sthapana kahan aur kaise karein

Murti ko ghar ke poorv ya uttar-poorv kone mein saaf chowki par sthapit karna prachlit hai. Puja karne waala poorv ya uttar ki taraf munh kare. Mandir ki disha ke baare mein [ghar ke mandir ki disha](/puja-basics/ghar-ke-mandir-disha-vastu) page padhein. Sthapana ke samay kalash rakhna aur pran-pratishtha ka sankalp lena prachlit hai. Kalash vidhi ke liye [Navratri kalash sthapana vidhi](/navratri-devi/navratri-kalash-sthapana-vidhi) dekhein, aur poori puja ke liye [ghar par Durga Puja vidhi](/durga-maa/durga-puja-at-home-vidhi).

## Visarjan ke baare mein

Visarjan prachlit roop mein Dashami ya Navratri ke ant mein hota hai, par tithi parivar aur kshetra ke anusar badalti hai. Ghar ki mitti ki murti ko ghar mein baalti ya tub mein jal mein visarjit karke, us mitti ko gamle ya paudhe mein daalna kai parivaron mein apnaya jaata hai. Nadi ya talab mein visarjan ke apne shehar ke niyam pehle pata kar lein, kyunki kai shehron mein tay visarjan sthal banaye gaye hain.

## Ghar ke liye murti kharidne se pehle

- Murti ka size aur sthapana ki jagah naap lein.
- Dukaandaar ya kalakar se material pooch lein.
- Murti ki booking, advance aur delivery ki baat likhit mein tay karein. Rate shehar ke anusar badalte hain, isliye yahan koi rakam nahi di gayi.
- Visarjan ka plan pehle se soch lein.
