Durga Puja ka mukhya utsav Shashthi se Dashami tak paanch din chalta hai. Har din ka apna kram hai, jo Bengal ki parampara mein sabse vistaar se nibhaya jaata hai. Yeh page paanchon dinon ka saral calendar-style saar deta hai. Kisi bhi din ki tithi ya muhurat yahan nahi di gayi, kyunki woh har saal aur har shehar mein badalte hain. Unhe apne panchang se dekhein.

## Mahalaya: shuruaat se pehle

Utsav se kuch din pehle **Mahalaya** aata hai, jab pitron ko tarpan diya jaata hai. Bengal mein is subah Devi ke aagman ke path aur geet sunne ki parampara hai. Tarpan ke liye [Mahalaya puja tarpan vidhi](/navratri-devi/mahalaya-puja-tarpan-vidhi) dekhein.

## Din 1: Shashthi

Shashthi ki shaam Devi ka **bodhan** hota hai, yaani unhe jagane aur padharne ka aamantran. Bengal ki parampara mein bel vriksh ke neeche bodhan aur phir adhivas ki rasm hoti hai. Pratima ka mukh aksar isi samay anavrit kiya jaata hai, jise kai jagah pran-pratishtha ka prarambh maana jaata hai. Ghar mein log kalash sthapana ya Devi ke aavahan ke saath puja shuru karte hain.

## Din 2: Saptami

Saptami ki subah **Nabapatrika** (kola bou) ko nadi ya talab ke jal se snaan karaya jaata hai. Nabapatrika mein nau prakar ke paudhon ke patte hote hain, jinhe saadi pehnakar Ganesh ji ke paas sthapit kiya jaata hai. Iske baad pran-pratishtha aur mukhya puja shuru hoti hai. Nau paudhon ko nau Durga roopon se jodkar dekha jaata hai, jis par vishesh vistaar aapko apne pandit ji se milega.

## Din 3: Ashtami

Ashtami Durga Puja ka sabse mahatvapurn din maana jaata hai. Subah **Pushpanjali** hoti hai, jisme bhakt haath mein phool lekar mantra ke saath Maa ko arpit karte hain. Is din **kumari puja** aur Ashtami-Navami ke milan par **sandhi puja** hoti hai. Prachlit manyata mein sandhi puja ki avadhi Ashtami ke ant aur Navami ke aarambh ke lagbhag 48 minute ki hoti hai. Iska sahi samay har saal panchang se nikalna padta hai. Vistaar ke liye [Maha Ashtami puja vidhi](/durga-maa/maha-ashtami-puja-vidhi) aur [Kumari puja vidhi](/durga-maa/kumari-puja-kanya-pujan-vidhi) padhein.

## Din 4: Navami

Navami ko mahapuja aur havan hota hai. Kai jagah Ashtami ki sandhi puja ka samay Navami tak jaata hai, isliye dono tithiyon ke beech ki avadhi dhyan se dekhi jaati hai. Navami ke din Devi ko vishesh bhog arpit kiya jaata hai aur pandalon mein aarti, dhaak ke saath dhunuchi nritya jaisi parampara dekhi jaati hai. Uttar Bharat mein isi din ya Ashtami par kanya pujan hota hai.

## Din 5: Dashami

Dashami ko Devi ko vidaai di jaati hai. Prachlit kram mein antim puja, darpan visarjan, **sindoor khela** aur phir pratima ka visarjan hota hai. Isi din Vijayadashami (Dussehra) bhi hai. Bengal mein ek doosre ko Shubho Bijoya kehkar badhai dene aur bado ke charan chhoone ki parampara hai. Visarjan ke samay nadi ya talab ko saaf rakhne ke liye ghar ki mitti ki murti aur prakritik rang chunna achha vikalp hai, iske baare mein [pratima guide](/durga-maa/durga-pratima-murti-guide) mein bataya gaya hai.

## Yeh kram ghar mein kaise nibhayein

Ghar mein paanch din ka poora Bengal-style kram aam taur par nahi hota. Zyadatar parivar chhoti murti ya tasveer rakhte hain aur roz subah-shaam diya, phool aur aarti karte hain. Ashtami par bhog ya kanya pujan kar lete hain. Shuruaat ke liye [ghar par Durga Puja vidhi](/durga-maa/durga-puja-at-home-vidhi) aur saaman ke liye [samagri list](/durga-maa/durga-puja-samagri-list) dekhein. Tyohaar ki saamanya jaankari ke liye [Durga Puja kya hai](/durga-maa/durga-puja) padhein.

## Tithi aur yatra ka plan

- Apne shehar ka panchang dekhkar Shashthi se Dashami tak ki tithiyan likh lein.
- Sandhi puja ka samay, pushpanjali ka samay aur visarjan ka samay aayojak samiti ya pandit ji se confirm karein.
- Pandal dekhne jaana ho to [pandal near me guide](/durga-maa/durga-puja-pandal-near-me) ki checklist dekhein.
