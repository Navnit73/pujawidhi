Aapke parivar ya pandit ji ki parampara mein kuch cheezein alag ho sakti hain, to unhe jodein ya hatayein.

## Saaman ka matlab aur kuch vikalp

- **Kalash:** Devi ka pratik maana jaata hai. Jal, aam ke patte aur nariyal ke saath sthapit hota hai. Kalash sthapana ki vidhi ke liye [Navratri kalash sthapana vidhi](/navratri-devi/navratri-kalash-sthapana-vidhi) dekhein.
- **Laal rang:** Laal chunri aur laal phool Devi ko priya maane jaate hain. Gudhal ka phool prachlit hai. Na mile to jo bhi saaf, taaza phool mil sake, shraddha se arpit karein.
- **Suhag saamagri:** Kai ghar mein Devi ko sindoor, choodi aur bindi jaisa shringar saaman arpit kiya jaata hai. Yeh parampara kshetra ke hisaab se alag hoti hai.
- **Naivedya:** Parivar ki parampara ke anusar halwa, kheer ya mithai arpit karein. Vrat ke dinon mein anaaj ya sabzi ke niyam ghar-ghar mein alag hote hain, isliye apne bado se poochh lein.
- **Vikalp:** Koi cheez na mil sake to uski jagah akshat (chawal) ya phool arpit karne ki parampara hai. Shraddha sabse mukhya maani jaati hai.

## Kharch par asar daalne waali baatein

Murti ka size aur material, phool aur mala ki matra, havan ka aayojan, pandit ji ki dakshina aur bhojan ki sankhya, in sab se kharch badalta hai. Kisi tay rakam ka daava yahan nahi kiya gaya. Pandit ji se dakshina ke baare mein pehle se baat kar lein, iske liye [puja ka kharcha aur dakshina](/puja-basics/puja-ka-kharcha-pandit-dakshina) page padhein.

## Puja se pehle ki checklist

1. Tithi aur muhurat apne shehar ke panchang se dekh lein.
2. Phool aur naivedya ek din pehle ya subah-subah le aayein, taaki taaza rahein.
3. Puja ka sthaan saaf karke chowki par kapda bichayein.
4. List ko print karke ek-ek saaman par tick karein.
5. Puja ka poora kram [ghar par Durga Puja vidhi](/durga-maa/durga-puja-at-home-vidhi) mein padh lein. Puja ke ant mein [Durga ji ki aarti](/durga-maa/durga-ji-ki-aarti) gayein.

Roz ki puja ki sabhi vastuon ke liye [puja samagri master list](/puja-basics/puja-samagri-master-list) bhi dekh sakte hain.
