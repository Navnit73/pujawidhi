Chandi Path ka matlab hai **Durga Saptashati** ka path, jise Devi Mahatmya bhi kehte hain. Yeh Markandeya Purana ka ek bhaag maana jaata hai aur Shakti upasana ka sabse pramukh granth hai. Navratri mein bahut se parivar aur pandit iska path karte hain. Yeh page batata hai ki Chandi Path kya hai, kab aur kaise karte hain, aur kaun sa kram prachlit hai. Poora Sanskrit path is page par nahi diya gaya hai, kyunki yeh bahut lamba hai aur ek bhi galti se bachne ke liye ise chhapi hui, pramanik pustak se padhna behtar hai.

## Poora paath: kahan se padhein

Chandi Path ke 13 adhyay aur 700 shlok chhapi hui pustakon mein sanskrit mein milte hain. Hum seedhe yahan poora text nahi de rahe. Aap **Gita Press, Gorakhpur** jaisi pramanik chhapi hui "Shri Durga Saptashati" se padhein, jisme Sanskrit shlok aur Hindi arth saath hote hain. Is site par verified text reviewer ke proofing ke baad jodne ki yojana hai.

Poore path ke vistaar, adhyay-wise saar aur anya jaankari ke liye [Durga Saptashati path: 13 adhyay guide](/durga-maa/durga-saptashati-path) dekhein.

Neeche kuch prasiddh shlok diye ja rahe hain, jinhe path ke dauraan padha jaata hai. Yeh Devi Mahatmya ke jaane-pehchane stuti-shlok hain.

## Arth (meaning) aur path ka saar

Chandi Path teen charitron mein baanta jaata hai:

- **Prathama charitra (adhyay 1):** Madhu aur Kaitabh asuron ka vadh, jahan Devi Yoganidra roop mein pratit hoti hain.
- **Madhyama charitra (adhyay 2 se 4):** Devtaon ke tej se Devi ka prakat hona aur Mahishasur ka vadh.
- **Uttama charitra (adhyay 5 se 13):** Shumbh-Nishumbh, Chand-Mund aur Raktabeej ke vadh ki katha, Devi ki stutiyan aur ant mein Suratha raja aur Samadhi vaishya ko varadaan.

Adhyay-wise vistrit saar [Durga Saptashati path](/durga-maa/durga-saptashati-path) page par diya gaya hai.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

<Steps>
  <Step title="Kab karein">
    Navratri ke nau dinon mein path sabse prachlit hai. Kuch parivar Ashtami ya Navami par, ya kisi vishesh avsar par bhi karte hain. Tithi aur muhurat apne panchang ya pandit ji se poochhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shuddhi aur sthapana">
    Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Puja sthal saaf karein, chowki par Devi ki murti ya tasveer aur kalash sthapit karein. Sankalp lein ki aap path kis uddeshya aur shraddha se kar rahe hain. Sankalp ke liye [puja sankalp kaise lein](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) dekhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aasan aur disha">
    Saaf aasan par baithein. Purv ya uttar ki taraf munh karna prachlit hai. Kitaab ko lakdi ke rehal ya saaf kapde par rakhein, seedhe zameen par nahi.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Path ka prachlit kram">
    Prachlit kram mein pehle Devi Kavach, Argala Stotra aur Keelak Stotra, phir Navarna mantra ka jaap padha jaata hai. Uske baad Ratri Sukta aur 13 adhyay ka path hota hai. Ant mein Devi Sukta, Rahasyatray aur kshama prarthana padhi jaati hai. Kuch parampara mein kram mein antar hota hai, isliye apne pandit ji ya chhapi hui pustak ka kram follow karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Nau din mein baantna">
    Kai parivar 13 adhyay ko nau dinon mein baant kar padhte hain. Baantne ka tareeka alag-alag hota hai. Pandit ji ya pustak mein diya gaya vibhajan apnayein. Kuch log ek hi din mein poora path karte hain, jismein kai ghante lagte hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ant mein">
    Path ke baad aarti karein, kshama prarthana karein aur prasad baantein. [Durga ji ki aarti](/durga-maa/durga-ji-ki-aarti) gayein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Niyam aur savdhaniyan

- **Uchcharan:** Sanskrit ka uchcharan shuddh ho to behtar. Agar aap shuru kar rahe hain to pehle kisi jaankar se sunna ya seekhna madadgaar hai.
- **Samay aur sankhya:** Path ki koi anivarya sankhya tay nahi hai. Shraddha aur shanti se jitna sambhav ho, utna karein.
- **Shuddhta:** Shuchita aur saaf-safai ka dhyan rakhein. Yeh puja mein aam maani jaati hai.
- **Phal ki guarantee nahi:** Yeh site kisi phal ya parinaam ka vaada nahi karti. Path shraddha aur bhakti ka karm hai.
- **Pandit ji:** Poore vidhi-vidhan wale path ke liye, khaas kar jab havan ya purascharan jaise bade anushthan ho, anubhavi pandit ji ki madad lein.

## Sources aur variants

Chandi Path ke text ki pramanik sources mein Markandeya Purana ka Devi Mahatmya khand aur uski chhapi hui pustakein (jaise Gita Press ki Durga Saptashati) maani jaati hain. Path ka kram, Kavach-Argala-Keelak ka sthaan, aur nau din ka vibhajan alag-alag parampara mein alag ho sakta hai. Hum is page par jis kram ka varnan kar rahe hain, woh **prachlit roop** hai. Aarti aur chalisa ki tarah isme ek hi tay roop nahi hai, isliye apne pandit ji ya parivar ki parampara ko pradhanta dein. Poora text reviewer ke sign-off ke baad hi is site par jodne ki yojana hai.
