Ghar par Durga puja ek chhoti si, shraddha bhari puja ho sakti hai, jise aap Navratri ke kisi bhi din ya Durga Puja ke dinon mein kar sakte hain. Yeh page ek **saral ghar ki vidhi** deta hai: kalash sthapana se aarti aur kshama prarthana tak. Bengal ki pandal wali Durga Puja ka vidhaan alag aur bahut vistrit hota hai, aur yahan uska poora varnan nahi hai. Hum yahan **prachlit ghar ki puja** bata rahe hain. Apne parivar ya pandit ji ki parampara ko pradhanta dein.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Taiyari aur safai">
    Puja sthaan ki safai karein. Snaan karke saaf (aam taur par laal ya peele) kapde pehnein. Chowki par laal kapda bichayein aur Maa Durga ko virajman karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aachman aur sankalp">
    Teen baar aachman karein. Haath mein jal, akshat aur phool lekar apna naam, gotra (agar pata ho), sthaan aur tithi bolte hue sankalp lein: "Main Maa Durga ki puja shraddha se, apne parivar ki sukh-shanti ki prarthana ke saath kar raha/rahi hoon." Jal zameen par chhod dein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kalash sthapana">
    Kalash mein jal bharein, usmein supari, ek sikka aur akshat daalein. Munh par aam ke patte rakhein aur uspar laal kapde mein lipta nariyal sthapit karein. Kalash par roli se swastik banayein aur mauli bandhein. Kalash ko chowki ke paas rakhein. (Kalash sthapana ka vistar [Navratri kalash sthapana vidhi](/navratri-devi/navratri-kalash-sthapana-vidhi) page par aayega.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dhyan aur avahan">
    Haath jodkar Maa ka dhyan karein.
    <Mantra dev={`या देवी सर्वभूतेषु शक्तिरूपेण संस्थिता ।
नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमो नमः ॥`} rom={`Ya Devi sarvabhooteshu shakti-roopena sansthita
Namastasyai namastasyai namastasyai namo namah`} arth="Jo Devi sabhi praniyon mein shakti ke roop mein sthit hain, unhe baar-baar pranam." />
    Phir avahan karein: <Mantra dev="ॐ दुर्गायै नमः, आवाहयामि" rom="Om Durgayai namah, aavahayami" arth="Main Maa Durga ka avahan (aamantran) karta/karti hoon." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Snaan, vastra aur shringar">
    Maa ko jal se snaan karayein (tasveer ho to jal ke chhinte dein). Laal chunri odhayein, sindoor, bindi aur shringar samagri arpit karein. Haar aur laal phool chadhayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dhoop, deep aur naivedya">
    Dhoop aur agarbatti dikhayein, phir diya. Phal, mithai aur halwa ka bhog lagayein. Bhog ke saath jal rakhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mantra jaap">
    Apni shraddha ke anusaar 11, 21 ya 108 baar jaap karein.
    <Mantra dev="ॐ ऐं ह्रीं क्लीं चामुण्डायै विच्चे" rom="Om Aim Hreem Kleem Chamundayai Vichche" arth="Navarna mantra, jo Devi ke teen roop (Saraswati, Lakshmi, Kali) ki upasana se juda maana jaata hai." />
    Chahein to Devi ke liye prachlit shloka bhi padhein.
    <Mantra dev={`सर्वमङ्गलमाङ्गल्ये शिवे सर्वार्थसाधिके ।
शरण्ये त्र्यम्बके गौरि नारायणि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥`} rom={`Sarvamangala-mangalye Shive sarvartha-sadhike
Sharanye Tryambake Gauri Narayani namo 'stu te`} arth="Sabhi mangalon ki mangal roop, Shiva, sabhi arthon ko siddh karne wali, sharan dene wali, teen netron wali Gauri Narayani, aapko pranam." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aarti">
    Kapoor ya ghee ki batti se thali ghumate hue [Durga ji ki aarti](/durga-maa/durga-ji-ki-aarti) gayein. Ghanti bajayein. Aarti lekar prasad baantein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kshama prarthana aur visarjan">
    Ant mein kshama prarthana karein: "Avahanam na jaanami, na jaanami visarjanam, poojam chaiva na jaanami, kshamasva Parameshwari." Navratri mein kalash ka jal puja ke baad ghar mein chhidak sakte hain aur baaki jal tulsi ya paudhe mein daal sakte hain. Poori puja ke antim din murti ya jau ka visarjan parivar ki parampara ke anusaar karein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Saaf-safai:** Puja sthaan aur bartan saaf hone chahiye. Puja se pehle snaan karein.
- **Jau ka dhyan:** Agar Navratri ke liye jau boye hain to unhe roz thoda jal dein.
- **Kanya pujan:** Ashtami ya Navami par kanya pujan ki parampara hai. Uski vidhi [Kumari puja / Kanya pujan](/durga-maa/kumari-puja-kanya-pujan-vidhi) page par aayegi.
- **Mantra ka uchcharan:** Agar uchcharan par bharosa na ho to saral naam jaap ("Jai Mata Di" ya "Om Dum Durgayai Namah") karein. Pandit ji se sikh lena achha rehta hai.
- **Samagri ki fikr:** Jo samagri uplabdh ho, usi se shraddha se puja karein. Mehnge saaman ki zaroorat nahi hai.

## Mahatva aur katha

Devi Durga ko shakti ka roop maana jaata hai. Devi Mahatmya (Durga Saptashati) mein unki Mahishasur, Shumbh-Nishumbh aadi par vijay ki kathayen hain. Navratri aur Durga Puja mein bhakt unki alag-alag roopon mein upasana karte hain. Yeh page shraddha, parampara aur vidhi ki jaankari ke liye hai aur kisi phal ki guarantee nahi deta.
