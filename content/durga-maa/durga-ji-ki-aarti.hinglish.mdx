## Arth (meaning)

- **Jai Ambe Gauri, Shyama Gauri:** Maa ki jai ho, jo Gauri (gori) aur Shyama (saanwli, Kali roop) dono roopon mein pujya hain. Brahma, Vishnu aur Shiv bhi unka dhyan karte hain.
- **Maang sindoor, chandravadan:** Maa ki maang mein sindoor aur mastak par kasturi ka tilak hai. Unke netra ujjwal aur mukh chandrama jaisa sundar hai.
- **Kehari vahan, khadg khappar:** Maa sher par virajman hain aur haath mein talwar tatha khappar dharan karti hain. Dev, manushya aur muni sab unki seva karte hain.
- **Shumbh-Nishumbh, Mahishasur, Chand-Mund, Madhu-Kaitabh:** In panktiyon mein Devi ke un roopon ka smaran hai jinhone asuron ka sanhar karke devtaon ko nirbhay kiya.
- **Chaunsath yogini:** Chaunsath yoginiyan gaati hain aur Bhairav nritya karte hain, jabki mridang aur damru ki taal bajti hai.
- **Tum hi jag ki mata:** Maa hi jagat ki mata aur palanhaar hain. Aarti ke ant mein jo bhakt shraddha se gaata hai, uske liye sukh-shanti ki prarthana hai.

## Kab aur kaise gayein

- **Kab:** Subah ya shaam ki puja ke ant mein. Navratri aur Durga Puja ke dinon mein subah-shaam dono samay gayi jaati hai. Shukravar ko bhi bahut se parivaar Devi ki aarti karte hain.
- **Kaise:** Pehle diya jalayein, phir kapoor ya ghee ki batti se thali ko ghumate hue aarti karein. Aarti ke dauraan ghanti bajana prachlit hai. Aarti ke baad thali par haath se aarti lekar aankhon par lagayein aur prasad baantein.
- **Kitni baar:** Koi tay sankhya nahi hai. Ek baar poori aarti gaana kaafi hai.
- **Puja ka poora kram:** Agar aap ghar par poori puja karna chahte hain to [Ghar par Durga Puja vidhi](/durga-maa/durga-puja-at-home-vidhi) dekhein.
