## Arth (meaning) aur saar

Durga Chalisa mein Maa Durga ki mahima ka varnan hai. Iske saar mein aam taur par yeh vishay aate hain:

- **Stuti aur pranam:** Shuruaat Maa ko baar-baar pranam aur unki nirakar jyoti ke gunagaan se hoti hai.
- **Devi ke roop:** Maa ke alag-alag roop, jaise Ambe, Bhavani, Kali aur Gauri ka smaran.
- **Asuron ka sanhar:** Mahishasur, Shumbh-Nishumbh, Chand-Mund aur Raktabeej jaise asuron ke vadh ki yaad, jo Devi Mahatmya (Durga Saptashati) mein varnit hai.
- **Bhakt ki prarthana:** Dukh, bhay aur kasht door karne ki vinay, aur Maa ki sharan mein jaane ka bhaav.
- **Phalashruti:** Ant mein path karne waale ke liye shubh kaamna ke shabd aate hain. Yeh site kisi phal ki guarantee nahi deti.

Chaupai-wise vistrit arth chhapi hui tika ya Gita Press ki pustak mein dekhein. Humne sankshipt saar apne shabdon mein diya hai.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

- **Kab:** Navratri ke nau dinon mein, Shukravar ko, ya Ashtami-Navami par prachlit hai. Roz subah ya shaam ki puja ke saath bhi padh sakte hain. Tithi aur muhurat apne panchang se dekhein.
- **Kaise:** Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Maa ki murti ya tasveer ke saamne diya jalayein, phool arpit karein aur saaf aasan par baithkar path karein. Purv ya uttar ki taraf munh karna prachlit hai.
- **Kitni baar:** Koi anivarya sankhya nahi hai. Ek baar shraddha se padhna kaafi hai. Kuch log 11 baar ya nau din tak roz ek baar bhi padhte hain, yeh parampara par nirbhar hai.
- **Ant mein:** Chalisa ke baad [Durga ji ki aarti](/durga-maa/durga-ji-ki-aarti) gayein.
- **Poori puja:** Agar aap puja karna chahte hain, to [ghar par Durga Puja vidhi](/durga-maa/durga-puja-at-home-vidhi) dekhein.
