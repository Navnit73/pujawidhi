## Is saal ke pandal kaise dhoondhein

1. **Puja samiti ke official page:** Bade pandal aam taur par apne page ya social media par samay aur pata daalte hain. Naam ke saath "puja samiti" likhkar khojein.
2. **Bengali association ya club:** Kai shehron mein Bengali samaj ki sansthayein puja ka aayojan karti hain aur unki website par jaankari milti hai.
3. **Sthaniya prashasan ya police:** Bheed wale shehron mein kuch din pehle yatayat ya suraksha ki saarvajanik soochna di jaati hai.
4. **Map ya review sites:** Inpar dikhne wali jaankari purani ya galat ho sakti hai. Ise sirf shuruaati sanket maaniye, aur jaane se pehle samiti se dobara confirm karein.
5. **Padosi aur parichit:** Sthaniya log aksar sabse bharosemand salaah dete hain.

## Verify before you go: checklist

- Pandal ka pata, entry gate aur samay aayojak ke official source se confirm kiya.
- Aarti, sandhi puja aur bhog ke samay pata kiye.
- Pandal tak jaane ka rasta, road closure ya diversion pata kiya.
- Parking ya public transport ka vikalp dekha.
- Bheed ke samay ke liye vaikalpik samay tay kiya.
- Parivar ke saath milne ka tay sthaan aur emergency number tay kiya.
- Bachchon aur buzurgon ke liye paani, snacks aur baithne ki yojana banayi.

## Bheed, suraksha aur yatayat ke saamanya tips

- Saptami, Ashtami aur Navami ki shaam sabse zyada bheed hoti hai. Zyada bheed ho to ruk kar ya doosre samay aana behtar hai.
- Bachchon ko seedhe haath se pakad kar rakhein aur unke paas parivar ka phone number likha kaagaz rakhein.
- Qeemti saaman kam se kam le jaayein. Jeb aur bag par dhyan rakhein.
- Aayojakon aur volunteers ke nirdeshon ka paalan karein, aur emergency exit ki jagah pehle dekh lein.
- Zyada bheed wale rasto ke liye metro ya sthaniya public transport zyada suvidhajanak ho sakta hai, jahan available ho. Rasta band hone ki khabar pehle dekh lein.
- Pandal ke bahar saaf-safai ka dhyan rakhein aur kachra tay jagah par hi daalein.
