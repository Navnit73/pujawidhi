"Sherawali" ka matlab hai sher par sawaar hone wali, yaani Maa Durga. Uttar Bharat mein, khaaskar Punjab, Haryana, Delhi, Himachal aur Jammu ke kshetra mein, Maa ko "Sherawali Mata" kehkar pukara jaata hai, aur "Jai Mata Di" ke jaykaare ke saath aarti aur bhajan gaye jaate hain. Yeh page batata hai ki Sherawali Mata ki aarti kya hai, kaise aur kab gayi jaati hai, aur iske bol kahan se milaayein.

## Aarti ke bol

"Mata Sherawali" ke naam se kai alag-alag aarti aur bhajan gaye jaate hain, aur har mandir, jagran mandali aur parivar mein unke bol thode alag hote hain. Humein is naam ki kisi ek aarti ka ek hi maanak paath **poore bharose ke saath** pakka nahi mila, isliye hum yahan koi aisa paath nahi likh rahe jo galat ya adhoora ho sakta hai. ## Arth (meaning)

Sherawali Mata ki aarti ka bhaav har roop mein lagbhag ek jaisa hota hai. Aarti mein Devi ke sher par sawaar roop ka varnan hota hai, unke dayalu roop ki stuti hoti hai, aur bhakt apne sankat door karne ki praarthna karta hai. Beech-beech mein "Jai Mata Di" ka jaykara lagta hai. Kai jagah Vaishno Devi, Jwalaji, Naina Devi jaise shaktipeethon ka bhi zikr aata hai. Kaun si pankti kis aarti mein hai, yeh sanskaran par nirbhar hai, isliye hum yahan pankti-dar-pankti arth tab denge jab satyapit paath jodenge.

## Kab aur kaise gayein

- **Kab:** Navratri mein subah-shaam ki puja ke ant mein, jagran aur Mata ki chowki mein, aur aam din Devi ki puja ke baad.
- **Kaise:** Devi ke saamne diya ya kapoor jalayein, thali mein phool aur akshat rakhein, aur thali ko ghadi ki disha mein ghumate hue gayein. Jaykare ke samay haath jodkar "Jai Mata Di" kahein.
- **Kitni baar:** Aarti ek baar poori gayi jaati hai.
