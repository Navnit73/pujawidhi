Aigiri Nandini, jo "Mahishasura Mardini Stotram" ke naam se bhi jaana jaata hai, Devi Durga ke Mahishasur ka vadh karne wale roop ki ek bahut lokpriya stuti hai. Iski pehli pankti **"Ayi giri-nandini nandit-medini"** se hi yeh pehchana jaata hai. Navratri aur Durga Puja ke dinon mein iska gayan ya paath bahut sunne ko milta hai. Is page par iska parichay, ek stanza ka paath, saar, aur paath ki vidhi di gayi hai.

## Arth (meaning)

Stotra ka mool bhaav yeh hai ki Devi Durga ne Mahishasur naam ke ahankari asur ka sanhar kiya aur devtaon ko bhay se mukt kiya. Is katha ka vistar [Durga Saptashati](/durga-maa/durga-saptashati-path) mein milta hai. Stotra mein Devi ko alag-alag sambodhanon se pukara jaata hai: parvat-putri, jagat ko aanand dene wali, Shiv ki sangini, sher par sawaar, asuron ka daman karne wali. Har stanza mein kuch sambodhan aur Devi ke gunon ka varnan hota hai, aur ant mein refrain Mahishasur-mardini ki jai-jaykar karta hai. Is page par hum sirf pehle stanza ka arth de rahe hain. Baaki stanza ka arth satyapit paath ke saath jodenge.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

- **Kab:** Navratri aur Durga Puja ke dinon mein subah ya shaam ki puja mein. Iske liye koi kadaa niyam nahi hai. Bhakt ise kisi bhi din shraddha se gaa sakte hain.
- **Kaise:** Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Devi ki tasveer ke saamne diya jalayein, ek aasan par baithein aur shaant man se paath karein. Uchcharan dhire aur saaf rakhein. Yeh stotra aksar sur aur taal ke saath bhi gaya jaata hai.
- **Kitni baar:** Is ke liye koi tay sankhya shastra mein hamare dwara nishchit roop se nahi kahi ja sakti. Parivar ya pandit ji ke anusaar ek baar ya adhik baar paath kiya jaata hai.
- **Asan aur disha:** Saaf aasan par baithkar, aam taur par purv ya uttar ki taraf munh karke baithna prachlit hai.
- **Puja ke saath:** Poori puja mein ise [Navratri puja vidhi](/navratri-devi/navratri-puja-vidhi) ke saath jodkar padha ja sakta hai.
