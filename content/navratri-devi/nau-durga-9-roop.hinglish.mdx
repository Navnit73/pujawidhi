Navratri ke nau dinon mein Maa Durga ke nau swaroopon ki puja ki jaati hai, jinhe **Nav Durga** kehte hain. Yeh page har roop ka naam, din, swaroop, prachlit bhog aur mantra saaf bhasha mein deta hai. Alag-alag kshetron aur granthon mein kuch baatein (khaaskar bhog aur rang) bhinn hoti hain, isliye jo aapke parivar mein chalta hai wahi follow karein. Yahan **prachlit roop** diya gaya hai. Dhyan shlok jo yahan diye gaye hain, pandit proofing ke liye chinhit hain.

## Poori jaankari

Nau roopon ka prasiddh kram ek shloke mein bhi milta hai, jo Devi Kavach (Durga Saptashati ke saath padha jaane wala) mein aata hai. Iske anusaar Devi ke nau naam Shailputri se Siddhidatri tak kramwar hain. Har roop ke saath ek dhyan shlok aur ek naam-mantra bhi prachlit hai. Kuch parampara mein roz ke saath ek chakra ya graha bhi jode jaate hain. Yeh maanyata alag-alag hai, isliye hum yahan sirf prachlit swaroop aur bhog bata rahe hain.

Poora puja kram [Navratri puja vidhi](/navratri-devi/navratri-puja-vidhi) mein, aur kalash sthapana [yahan](/navratri-devi/navratri-kalash-sthapana-vidhi) di gayi hai.

### 1. Shailputri

**Din:** Pratham din. **Bhog (prachlit):** Desi ghee.

Himalaya ki putri (shail = parvat). Vrish (bail) par sawaar, daayein haath mein trishul aur baayein mein kamal.

Vrisharudham shuladharam Shailputrim yashasvinim`} arth="Main ardh-chandra se sushobhit mastak wali, vrish par sawaar, trishuldhari yashasvini Shailputri ko pranam karta hoon." />

<Mantra dev="ॐ देवी शैलपुत्र्यै नमः" rom="Om Devi Shailputryai namah" arth="Namavali mantra ke roop mein prachlit. Jaap ki sankhya parivar ya pandit ji ki parampara se tay karein." />

### 2. Brahmacharini

**Din:** Doosra din. **Bhog (prachlit):** Cheeni ya mishri.

Tapasya ka roop. Safed vastra, daayein haath mein jap-mala aur baayein mein kamandalu. Tap, sanyam aur vairagya ki pratik maani jaati hain.

<Mantra dev={`दधाना करपद्माभ्यामक्षमालाकमण्डलू ।

Devi prasidatu mayi Brahmacharinyanuttama`} arth="Jo kamal jaise haathon mein akshamala aur kamandalu dharan karti hain, woh shreshth Brahmacharini Devi mujh par prasann hon." />

<Mantra dev="ॐ देवी ब्रह्मचारिण्यै नमः" rom="Om Devi Brahmacharinyai namah" arth="Namavali mantra ke roop mein prachlit. Jaap ki sankhya parivar ya pandit ji ki parampara se tay karein." />

### 3. Chandraghanta

**Din:** Teesra din. **Bhog (prachlit):** Doodh ya kheer.

Mastak par ghanta ke aakar ka ardhchandra, isliye yeh naam. Sher par sawaar, dus bhujaon wali, yuddh ke liye taiyaar mudra mein, par bhaktaon ke liye shaanti dene wali maani jaati hain.

<Mantra dev={`पिण्डजप्रवरारूढा चण्डकोपास्त्रकैर्युता ।
प्रसादं तनुते मह्यं चन्द्रघण्टेति विश्रुता ॥`} rom={`Pindaja-pravararudha chandakopastrakair-yuta
Prasadam tanute mahyam Chandraghanteti vishruta`} arth="Shreshth vahan par sawaar, astron se yukt, Chandraghanta naam se prasiddh Devi mujh par krupa karein." />

<Mantra dev="ॐ देवी चन्द्रघण्टायै नमः" rom="Om Devi Chandraghantayai namah" arth="Namavali mantra ke roop mein prachlit. Jaap ki sankhya parivar ya pandit ji ki parampara se tay karein." />

### 4. Kushmanda

**Din:** Chautha din. **Bhog (prachlit):** Malpua.

Maanyata hai ki apni halki muskaan se brahmand ki rachna ki. Ashtabhuja, sher par sawaar, haathon mein kamandalu, dhanush, baan, kamal, amrit-kalash, chakra aur gada.

<Mantra dev={`सुरासम्पूर्णकलशं रुधिराप्लुतमेव च ।

Dadhana hastapadmabhyam Kushmanda shubhadastu me`} arth="Jo kamal jaise haathon mein kalash dharan karti hain, woh Kushmanda Devi mere liye shubh dene wali hon." />

<Mantra dev="ॐ देवी कूष्माण्डायै नमः" rom="Om Devi Kushmandayai namah" arth="Namavali mantra ke roop mein prachlit. Jaap ki sankhya parivar ya pandit ji ki parampara se tay karein." />

### 5. Skandamata

**Din:** Paanchva din. **Bhog (prachlit):** Kela.

Skand (Kartikeya) ki mata. Kamal ke aasan par virajman, god mein baal Skand, chaar bhujaon wali. Isliye ise Padmasana Devi bhi kehte hain.

<Mantra dev={`सिंहासनगता नित्यं पद्माश्रितकरद्वया ।

Shubhadastu sada Devi Skandamata yashasvini`} arth="Simhasan par virajman, kamal liye hue Skandamata Devi sada shubh dene wali hon." />

<Mantra dev="ॐ देवी स्कन्दमातायै नमः" rom="Om Devi Skandamatayai namah" arth="Namavali mantra ke roop mein prachlit. Jaap ki sankhya parivar ya pandit ji ki parampara se tay karein." />

### 6. Katyayani

**Din:** Chhatha din. **Bhog (prachlit):** Shahad.

Maharshi Katyayan ki putri ke roop mein prakat. Mahishasur ka vadh karne wali Devi ke roop mein bhi jaani jaati hain. Sher par sawaar, chaar bhujaon wali. Vivah sambandhi puja mein bhi inka smaran parampara mein milta hai.

<Mantra dev={`चन्द्रहासोज्ज्वलकरा शार्दूलवरवाहना ।

Katyayani shubham dadyad Devi danavaghatini`} arth="Chandrahas talwar se chamakte haathon wali, sher par sawaar, danavon ka sanhar karne wali Katyayani Devi shubh pradaan karein." />

<Mantra dev="ॐ देवी कात्यायन्यै नमः" rom="Om Devi Katyayanyai namah" arth="Namavali mantra ke roop mein prachlit. Jaap ki sankhya parivar ya pandit ji ki parampara se tay karein." />

### 7. Kalaratri

**Din:** Saatva din. **Bhog (prachlit):** Gud.

Gehre kaale varn ki, bikhre baalon wali, ugra dikhne wali, par bhaktaon ke liye shubh phal dene wali maani jaati hain, isliye ise Shubhankari bhi kaha jaata hai. Gadha inka vahan maana jaata hai.

<Mantra dev="ॐ देवी कालरात्र्यै नमः" rom="Om Devi Kalaratryai namah" arth="Namavali mantra ke roop mein prachlit. Jaap ki sankhya parivar ya pandit ji ki parampara se tay karein." />

### 8. Mahagauri

**Din:** Aathva din (Ashtami). **Bhog (prachlit):** Nariyal.

Atyant gaur (safed) varn wali, safed vastra pehne, vrish par sawaar, chaar bhujaon wali. Maanyata hai ki kathin tapasya ke baad inka varn gaur hua. Ashtami par inki puja aur kanya pujan ki parampara hai.

<Mantra dev={`श्वेते वृषे समारूढा श्वेताम्बरधरा शुचिः ।

Mahagauri shubham dadyan Mahadeva-pramodada`} arth="Safed vrish par sawaar, safed vastradhari, pavitra, Mahadev ko aanand dene wali Mahagauri shubh pradaan karein." />

<Mantra dev="ॐ देवी महागौर्यै नमः" rom="Om Devi Mahagauryai namah" arth="Namavali mantra ke roop mein prachlit. Jaap ki sankhya parivar ya pandit ji ki parampara se tay karein." />

### 9. Siddhidatri

**Din:** Nauva din (Navami). **Bhog (prachlit):** Til.

Siddhiyan dene wali Devi. Kamal par virajman, chaar bhujaon wali. Maanyata hai ki Devi Puran ke anusaar Shiv ji ne bhi inki upasna se siddhiyan prapt kin. Navami par inki puja aur kanya pujan ya havan ki parampara hai.

<Mantra dev={`सिद्धगन्धर्वयक्षाद्यैरसुरैरमरैरपि ।

## FAQ

<FAQ>
  <Q q="Nav Durga ke nau roop kaun se hain?">Shailputri, Brahmacharini, Chandraghanta, Kushmanda, Skandamata, Katyayani, Kalaratri, Mahagauri aur Siddhidatri. Navratri mein Pratipada se Navami tak ek-ek din ek roop ki puja hoti hai.</Q>
  <Q q="Kya har din ka bhog tay hai?">Prachlit maanyata mein har roop ka ek bhog bataya jaata hai, par yeh parivar aur kshetra ke anusaar badal sakta hai. Shraddha se jo bhog sambhav ho, wahi arpit karein.</Q>
  <Q q="Roz ke rang kaun se hote hain?">Rangon ki parampara alag-alag kshetron mein alag hai aur saal ke var ke anusaar bhi badalti hai. Hum koi ek suchi nahi de rahe. Apne mandir ya parivar ki parampara dekhein.</Q>
  <Q q="Kya ek din mein do roopon ki puja ho sakti hai?">Tithi ke ghatne-badhne par kabhi-kabhi ek hi din do tithiyan aa jaati hain. Us sthiti mein panchang ya pandit ji ki salaah lein.</Q>
  <Q q="Kya main sirf ek roop ki puja kar sakta hoon?">Haan. Kai log apne ishta roop ya Ashtami-Navami ki Devi ki puja vishesh roop se karte hain. Shraddha sabse pradhan hai.</Q>
</FAQ>
