Navratri vrat Maa Durga ke prati shraddha aur sanyam ka vrat hai. Isme bhakt nau din tak niyam se puja, upvaas ya falahar aur sattvik jeevan ka paalan karte hain. Yeh page vrat ke prachlit niyam, puja ka saral kram aur vrat se jude kathon ka saar deta hai. Vrat ke niyam parivar, kshetra aur sampradaay ke hisaab se badalte hain, isliye apne parivar ke bade ya pandit ji se zaroor poochhein. Yahan **prachlit roop** diya gaya hai.

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Sanyam aur saadgi:** Vrat ke dinon mein krodh, jhooth aur kadve vachan se bachne ki salaah di jaati hai. Shuddh aur shaant man hi vrat ka sar hai.
- **Aahar:** Lehsun, pyaaz, maans-madira aur tamasik bhojan se door rehna prachlit hai. Falahar mein bhi bahut zyada talna ya bhaari khana na khayein.
- **Zabardasti na karein:** Bimar, garbhvati, vriddh aur bachhon ke liye kadaa upvaas zaroori nahi maana jaata. Apne doctor aur bade se poochhein. Unke liye vrat ka roop halka kiya ja sakta hai.
- **Bal-kesh aur shringar:** Kuch parivar nau din baal ya nakhun na kaatne, jooton ya chamde ki vastuon se bachne jaise niyam maante hain. Yeh sab parivar ki parampara par nirbhar hai, anivaarya nahi.
- **Din mein sona:** Kai parivar vrat mein din mein na sone ki salaah dete hain. Yeh bhi parampara hai.
- **Vrat tootne par:** Anjaane mein vrat toot jaaye to ghabrayein nahi. Kshama prarthana karke agle din se shraddha se jaari rakhein.

## Mahatva aur katha

Navratri vrat se jude kai katha-roop prachalit hain, aur sab kshetron mein ek hi katha nahi sunayi jaati. Do prasiddh prasang is prakar hain.

**Mahishasur vadh:** Devi Mahatmya (Durga Saptashati) mein varnan hai ki Mahishasur naam ke asur ne devtaon ko parajit kar diya. Tab devtaon ke tej se Devi Durga prakat huin aur unhone Mahishasur ka vadh kiya. Is vijay ki smriti Navratri aur Durga Puja mein manayi jaati hai.

**Raja Suratha aur Samadhi vaishya:** Devi Mahatmya ki shuruaat mein raja Suratha aur vaishya Samadhi ki katha aati hai. Dono apne apnon se dukhi hokar van mein Medha rishi ke paas pahunchte hain, jo unhe Devi ke mahatmya ka upadesh dete hain. Kaha jaata hai ki dono ne Devi ki upasna ki.

Kuch kshetron mein Navratri ko Ram ji dwara Devi ki aaradhna se bhi joda jaata hai, aur Dashami ko Vijayadashami ke roop mein manaya jaata hai. In kathaon ke vistar alag-alag granthon aur kshetron mein bhinn milte hain. Yeh page vishwas aur parampara ke baare mein jaankari ke liye hai. Kisi phal ya sankalp ki guarantee nahi di ja rahi.
