Navratri ke vrat mein sabse bada sawaal hota hai: "Kya khayein aur kya nahi?" Is page mein falahar ki aam parampara saaf bhasha mein samjhayi gayi hai. Yeh sirf parampara aur aam jaankari hai, koi swasthya ya diet salaah nahi. Vrat ke niyam parivar aur kshetra ke hisaab se alag hote hain, isliye jo aapke yahan chalta hai wahi follow karein. Yahan **prachlit roop** diya gaya hai.

## Poori jaankari

### Vrat ke khane ka aam kram

Navratri vrat mein anaaj (gehun, chawal, daal, makke ka atta) aam taur par nahi liya jaata. Uski jagah aise khaane liye jaate hain jinhe vrat mein anumat maana jaata hai. Kya anumat hai, is par kuch bhinnata parivaron mein milti hai, isliye yeh suchi aam parampara ki hai.

### Aam taur par liye jaane wale khaane

- **Phal aur sookha meva:** Sabhi taaze phal, kishmish, badaam, kaju, khajoor.
- **Doodh se bani cheezein:** Doodh, dahi, chhaachh, paneer, ghee, makhan.
- **Vrat ke aate aur anaaj:** Kuttu ka atta, singhare ka atta, rajgira (chaulai) ka atta, samak ke chawal (sama ke chawal ya vrat ke chawal), sabudana (tapioca).
- **Sabziyan:** Aloo, shakarkandi, lauki (ghiya), kaddu (petha), arbi, kheera, kela kaccha. Kya sabzi chalti hai, ismein parivaron mein antar hota hai.
- **Namak:** Sendha namak (rock salt). Kuch parivar saadha namak ki jagah sirf yahi lete hain, kuch namak bilkul nahi lete.
- **Masale aur tel:** Jeera, kali mirch, hari mirch, adrak, dhaniya patta aam taur par chalte hain. Tel ke liye ghee ya moongfali ka tel kai ghar mein prachlit hai. Kuch parivar sirf ghee lete hain.
- **Meetha:** Cheeni, gud, makhane ki kheer, sabudana kheer, singhare ka halwa.

### Aam taur par parhez kiye jaane wale khaane

- Gehun, chawal, daal, besan, maida, sooji, makke ka atta
- Saadha (rai wala) namak, Navratri mein sendha namak se badla jaata hai
- Lehsun aur pyaaz
- Maans, ande, madira
- Kuch parivar haldi, hing aur kuch masale bhi nahi lete

### Parampara ke hisaab se kya alag hai

Ek hi dhaarmik parampara ke andar bhi vrat ke niyam alag hote hain. Kuch parivar sirf phal aur doodh lete hain. Kuch din mein ek baar phalahari khana khate hain aur raat ko puja ke baad vrat kholte hain. Kuch parivar roz ek hi samay khana khate hain (ekabhukta). Kuch log nirjala vrat rakhte hain, jo kathin hota hai aur sabke liye uchit nahi. Is liye apne parivar ke bade se poochhein.

### Khane ke saral vichar

Vrat ke dauraan ghar mein bane kuch aam vyanjan: sabudana khichdi, kuttu ki puri ya roti, aloo jeera, samak ke chawal ki khichdi, makhane ki kheer, rajgira ki puri, singhare ka halwa, dahi aloo aur fal chaat. Yeh sirf udaaharan hain, aur unki matra ya kalori ki baat hum nahi kar rahe.

### Saavdhaaniyan

- **Taaze aur bharose ki dukaan:** Kuttu aur singhare ka atta taaza aur bharose ki dukaan se lein. Purana ya kharab atta pet kharab kar sakta hai. Kharab ya sandehaspad atta khane se bachein.
- **Label dekhein:** Bazaar ke packed vrat ke saaman par saamagri aur taareekh dekhein.
- **Paani aur halka khana:** Poore din bahut kam khane se kamzori mehsoos ho sakti hai. Paani, nimbu paani, nariyal paani aur halke phal beech-beech mein lete rehna aam salaah hai.
- **Tala hua zyada na khayein:** Vrat ke khane mein bhi bahut zyada talna ya bhaari khane se pet bhaari ho sakta hai.
- **Swasthya:** Madhumeh, bp, garbhavastha, bachhon ya vriddhon ke liye upvaas ke baare mein apne doctor se zaroor poochhein. Vrat anivaarya nahi hai, shraddha se halka roop bhi manya hai.

Vrat ke poore niyam [Navratri vrat katha aur niyam](/navratri-devi/navratri-vrat-katha-niyam) mein, aur 9 din ka puja kram [Navratri puja vidhi](/navratri-devi/navratri-puja-vidhi) mein diya gaya hai.
