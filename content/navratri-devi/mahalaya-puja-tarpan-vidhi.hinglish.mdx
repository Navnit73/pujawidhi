Mahalaya Ashwin maas ki Amavasya ko manaya jaata hai, jo Pitru Paksha ka antim din hota hai aur ise Sarva Pitru Amavasya bhi kehte hain. Is din pitron ke liye tarpan aur shraddh karne ki parampara hai. Iske agle din se Devi Paksha shuru maana jaata hai, jo Navratri aur Durga Puja ki taraf le jaata hai. Bengal mein Mahalaya ka vishesh mahatva hai. Yeh page tarpan ki saral vidhi aur Devi ke aavahan ka bhaav batata hai. Tarpan aur shraddh ki paddhati kul aur kshetra ke anusaar alag hoti hai, isliye apne parivar ke pandit ji se poochhein. Yahan **prachlit saral roop** diya gaya hai.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Snaan aur taiyari">
    Subah snaan karke saaf vastra pehnein. Saaf sthaan par aasan par baithein. Kai parampara mein tarpan nadi ya tirth par bhi kiya jaata hai. Ghar par bhi saaf jal se kiya ja sakta hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pavitri aur sankalp">
    Daayein haath ki anamika ungli mein kusha ki pavitri pehnein. Haath mein jal, til aur kusha lekar sankalp lein ki aap apne pitron ki trupti ke liye tarpan kar rahe hain. Apna naam, gotra aur pitron ke naam (agar pata ho) bolein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tarpan ki dishayen">
    Prachlit kram mein pehle devtaon ke liye purv disha mein, phir rishiyon ke liye uttar disha mein, aur ant mein pitron ke liye dakshin disha mein tarpan karte hain. Pitron ke tarpan mein janeu aur til ka prayog alag tareeke se hota hai. Janeu dhaari log is baare mein apne pandit ji se vistar se poochhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pitron ko jal arpit karna">
    Kusha aur kaale til ke saath jal anjali mein lekar angoothe aur tarjani ke beech se dheere se patra mein chhodein. Aam taur par har pitra ke liye teen anjali di jaati hai. Naam ke saath bolein: "[Naam] gotre [pitra ka sambandh] ... tripyatam" ya parivar ke pandit ji ke anusaar. Naam na pata ho to "Sarva pitru" bhaav se bhi tarpan ki parampara hai.
    <Mantra dev="ॐ पितृभ्यः स्वधा नमः" rom="Om Pitribhyah swadha namah" arth="Pitron ko swadha ke saath namaskar. Tarpan ke mantra ka roop parivar aur pandit ji ki parampara se tay hota hai." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bhojan aur daan">
    Is din Brahman bhojan, garib ya zaruratmand ko bhojan ya daan ki parampara hai. Gaay, kauve aur kutte ko bhi bhojan dene ki parampara bataayi jaati hai. Yeh parivar ki shraddha par nirbhar hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shaam ko Devi ka smaran">
    Mahalaya se Devi ke aagaman ka bhaav jodkar kai ghar mein shaam ko diya jalakar Maa Durga ka smaran kiya jaata hai. Navratri ki taiyari [Navratri puja vidhi](/navratri-devi/navratri-puja-vidhi) aur [Navratri kalash sthapana vidhi](/navratri-devi/navratri-kalash-sthapana-vidhi) mein dekhein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Pandit ji se poochhna:** Tarpan aur shraddh ki vidhi kul ke anusaar alag hoti hai. Gotra, naam ke uchcharan aur janeu ki sthiti ke liye apne kul ke pandit ji se zaroor poochhein.
- **Muhurat:** Amavasya tithi aur uske kutup ya aparahna jaise samay panchang se dekhein. Hum koi samay nahi bata rahe.
- **Shuddhta aur shraddha:** Sachchi shraddha se kiya gaya saral tarpan bhi manya maana jaata hai. Na ho sake to bhaav se jal arpit karna bhi parampara mein hai.
- **Kaale til ka dhyan:** Kaale til pitru tarpan mein prachlit hain, devtaon ke tarpan mein alag niyam hote hain.
- **Samay par dhyan:** Subah ya madhyahna se pehle tarpan karne ki parampara hai. Samay ka vistar panchang ya pandit ji se dekhein.

## Mahatva aur katha

Hindu parampara mein maana jaata hai ki Pitru Paksha mein pitron ka smaran aur tarpan kiya jaata hai, aur Amavasya par sabhi gyaat-agyaat pitron ke liye tarpan hota hai. Bengal mein Mahalaya ko Durga Puja ki shuruaat maana jaata hai. Is din subah-subah Chandi path aur "Mahishasura Mardini" ka paath sunne ki lokpriya parampara hai, jo radio par bahut lokpriya raha hai. Kai kshetron mein Mahalaya par murtikar Devi ki murti ki aankhein banate hain, jise "Chokkhu Daan" kaha jaata hai. In rasmon ka roop alag-alag jagah par alag hai. Yeh page vishwas aur parampara ke baare mein jaankari ke liye hai.
