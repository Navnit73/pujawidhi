"Ambe tu hai Jagdambe Kali" Devi ki sabse lokpriya aartiyon mein se ek hai, jo Navratri ke dinon mein mandiron aur gharon mein bade bhaav se gayi jaati hai. Iski dhun saral hai aur har ant mein refrain **"O Maiya, hum sab utaarein teri aarti"** aata hai, isliye poori mandli aasani se saath gaa leti hai. Neeche iska prachlit paath Devanagari aur Hinglish mein, saral arth ke saath diya gaya hai.

## Arth (meaning)

Is aarti ke teen bhaav hain. Pehla, Devi ke teen roopon ka smaran: **Ambe** (maa), **Kali** (anyay ka naash karne wali) aur **Durga** (sankat se bachane wali), jinhe khappar aur sher ke saath yaad kiya jaata hai. Doosra, maa aur santaan ke rishte ki baat: bhakt kehta hai ki sansar mein kapoot santaan ho sakti hai, par maa kabhi kumata nahi hoti. Teesra, maang ki saadgi: bhakt dhan-daulat nahi, sirf maa ke charanon mein thoda sa sthaan maangta hai. Isi saadgi ki wajah se yeh aarti bahut dilon ko chhoo leti hai.

## Kab aur kaise gayein

- **Kab:** Navratri mein subah ya shaam ki puja ke ant mein. Aam din mein bhi Devi ki puja ke baad gayi ja sakti hai. Mangalwar aur shukrawar ko Devi ke bhakt vishesh roop se aarti karte hain, par yeh koi kadaa niyam nahi hai.
- **Kaise:** Pehle diya ya kapoor jalayein, phir thali mein diya, phool aur akshat rakhkar Devi ki murti ya tasveer ke saamne ghadi ki disha mein (clockwise) thali ghumate hue gayein. Ghanti ya taali bajana prachlit hai.
- **Kitni baar:** Poori aarti ek baar gayi jaati hai. Refrain par sab saath mil jaayein to achha lagta hai.
- **Aarti ke baad:** Aarti lekar prasad baantein. Poori [Navratri puja vidhi](/navratri-devi/navratri-puja-vidhi) mein aarti ant ka hissa hai.

Agar aapko "Jai Ambe Gauri" wali aarti chahiye, to woh [Durga ji ki aarti](/durga-maa/durga-ji-ki-aarti) page par hai. Doosri aartiyon ke liye [Aarti Sangrah](/puja-basics/aarti-sangrah) dekhein.
