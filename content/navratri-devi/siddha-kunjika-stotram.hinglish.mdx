Siddha Kunjika Stotram Devi upasna ka ek prasiddh stotra hai, jo Durga Saptashati (Chandi path) se jude vishesh upaayon mein bahut charcha mein rehta hai. Parampara ke anusaar ise **Rudrayamal tantra ke Gauri Tantra** ka ansh maana jaata hai, aur kai prakashit Durga Saptashati sanskaranon ke saath ise alag se chhapa jaata hai. Maanyata hai ki yeh stotra kunji (chaabi) ki tarah Saptashati path ka phal kholne wala hai, isiliye isey "Kunjika" kaha gaya. ## Arth (meaning)

Stotra ka saar yeh hai ki jaise tala kunji se khulta hai, waise hi Saptashati ka path, kuch parampara ke anusaar, is stotra ke saath poora phalit hota hai. Stotra mein pehle Shiv ji Devi ko is stotra ka mahatva batate hain. Phir beej mantron ki ek shrinkhala aati hai, jo Devi ke alag-alag roopon aur shaktiyon ko sambodhit karti hai. In beej mantron ka koi saral shabdik arth nahi hota. Unhe shakti ke naad ya sankalp ki tarah maana jaata hai. Ant mein stotra ke phal ka varnan hota hai. Hum yahan koi vishesh phal ya chamatkar ka dawa nahi karte. Shraddha aur niyam se paath ko hi pradhan maanein.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

- **Kab:** Navratri mein roz subah ya shaam, Durga puja ke samay, ya Devi upasna ke kisi bhi samay. Kuch bhakt ise Saptashati path ke baad ya uski jagah padhte hain. Parampara alag ho sakti hai.
- **Kitni baar:** Aam taur par ek baar poora paath. Adhik sankhya ya anushthan ke liye pandit ji ya guru se poochhein.
- **Aasan aur shuddhta:** Snaan karke saaf vastra pehnein, saaf aasan par baithein, Devi ke saamne diya jalayein. Aam taur par purv ya uttar ki taraf munh karna prachlit hai.
- **Uchcharan:** Beej mantron ka uchcharan saaf aur sahi hona chahiye. Agar aap naye hain, to pehle kisi jaankar se sunkar seekhein.
- **Gurumukhi parampara:** Tantrik stotron ke baare mein kai parampara maanti hain ki unka paath guru ya jaankar pandit ki salaah se karna behtar hai. Yeh sab parivar aur sampradaay par nirbhar hai.
