Mangala Gauri vrat Shravan maas ke har Mangalwar ko Devi Gauri (Parvati) ki puja ka vrat hai. Parampara mein ise vishesh roop se vivahit mahilayen, khaaskar navvivahit, akhand saubhagya aur parivar ke sukh ki kaamna se rakhti hain. Yeh vrat kai kshetron mein, jaise Maharashtra, Gujarat aur Uttar Bharat mein, apne-apne roop mein manaya jaata hai. Yeh page vrat ki saral puja vidhi deta hai. Kshetra aur parivar ke hisaab se rasmein badalti hain, isliye apne parivar ke bade ya pandit ji se poochhein. Yahan **prachlit roop** diya gaya hai.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Taiyari">
    Subah snaan karke saaf (aam taur par laal ya hare) kapde pehnein. Puja sthaan saaf karke chowki par kapda bichayein aur Gauri ji ko virajman karein. Kai mahilayen is din vrat rakhti hain aur ek samay falahar ya sattvik bhojan leti hain. Yeh parivar ki parampara hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sankalp">
    Haath mein jal, akshat aur phool lekar apna naam, gotra (agar pata ho) aur tithi bolte hue sankalp lein. Saral roop: "Main Mangala Gauri vrat ki puja shraddha se karne ka sankalp leti hoon. Gauri Maa meri puja sweekar karein." Jal zameen par chhod dein. Pehle Ganesh ji ka smaran karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dhyan aur avahan">
    Haath jodkar Gauri Maa ka dhyan karein.
    <Mantra dev={`सर्वमङ्गलमाङ्गल्ये शिवे सर्वार्थसाधिके ।
शरण्ये त्र्यम्बके गौरि नारायणि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥`} rom={`Sarvamangala-mangalye Shive sarvartha-sadhike
Sharanye Tryambake Gauri Narayani namostu te`} arth="He sabka mangal karne wali, Shiva, sab prayojan siddh karne wali, sharan dene wali, teen netron wali Gauri Narayani, aapko namaskar hai." />
    Phir avahan karein: <Mantra dev="ॐ गौर्यै नमः, आवाहयामि" rom="Om Gauryai namah, aavahayami" arth="Main Gauri Maa ka avahan (aamantran) karti hoon." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Snaan, vastra aur shringar">
    Gauri ji ko jal se snaan karayein, vastra aur chunri arpit karein. Roli, haldi, akshat aur chandan lagayein. Phir sindoor, bindi, chudiyan jaise shringar ki vastuyen arpit karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Phool, dhoop, deep aur naivedya">
    Phool, durva aur bel patra chadhayein. Dhoop aur deep dikhayein. Phal aur mithai ka naivedya arpit karein. Kai parivar mein 16 diye, 16 phool ya 16 laddu arpit karne ki parampara hai. Yeh sankhya kshetra aur parivar ke hisaab se badalti hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Katha shravan">
    Mangala Gauri vrat katha sunna ya padhna parampara ka hissa hai. Katha ke bina bhi shraddha se puja manya hai. Ek prachlit katha neeche di gayi hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aarti aur kshama prarthana">
    Kapoor ya ghee ki batti se Gauri Maa ki aarti karein. Kshama prarthana karke prasad baantein. Shringar ki vastuyen kai parivar mein suhaagin mahilaon ko ya parivar ki badi mahila ko di jaati hain.
    <Mantra dev={`आवाहनं न जानामि न जानामि विसर्जनम् ।
पूजां चैव न जानामि क्षमस्व परमेश्वरि ॥`} rom={`Aavahanam na jaanami na jaanami visarjanam
Poojam chaiva na jaanami kshamasva Parameshwari`} arth="Main na avahan jaanti hoon, na visarjan, na puja ki vidhi. Mujhse jo bhool hui ho, use kshama karein." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Udyapan">
    Parampara mein vrat kuch varshon tak rakhne ke baad udyapan (samapan) ki baat milti hai. Isme kitne varsh aur kya vidhi hai, yeh parivar aur pandit ji se poochhein. Hum koi ek sankhya nahi bata rahe.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Tithi ka bhram:** Shravan maas purnimanta aur amanta panchang mein alag samay par aata hai. Isliye Mangalwar ki tithi apne kshetra ke panchang se dekhein.
- **Shuddhta par dhyan:** Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Chhoti chook se ghabrayein nahi.
- **Samagri ki fikr:** Koi vastu na mile to jal, phool aur akshat se bhi puja shraddha se poori maani jaati hai.
- **Vrat ka aahar:** Vrat ke din sattvik bhojan lena prachlit hai. Swasthya sambandhi dikkat ho to upvaas halka rakhein ya doctor se poochhein.
- **Zabardasti nahi:** Yeh vrat shraddha ka vishay hai. Kisi par dabaav na dalein.

## Mahatva aur katha

Mangala Gauri vrat Devi Parvati ke Gauri roop ko samarpit hai. Maanyata hai ki yeh vrat parivar ke sukh aur dampatya jeevan ke mangal ke liye rakha jaata hai. Ek prachlit katha ke anusaar ek dharmik vyapari aur uski patni ko santaan ka sukh nahi tha. Devi ki kripa se unhe putra hua. Aage ki katha mein vrat ke prabhav se putra ki ayu ya kathinaiyon ke door hone ka varnan aata hai. Katha ke vistar kshetron aur pustakon mein bhinn milte hain, isliye hum yahan saar hi de rahe hain. Kisi phal ki guarantee nahi di ja rahi. Yeh page vishwas aur parampara ke baare mein jaankari ke liye hai. Parvati ji ki roz ki puja ke liye [Navratri puja vidhi](/navratri-devi/navratri-puja-vidhi) bhi dekh sakte hain.
