Shanivar (Saturday) Shani Dev ka din maana jaata hai. Is din ghar par ya mandir mein saral puja, til ya sarson ke tel ka diya, Shani mantra jaap aur Shani Chalisa ka paath karne ki parampara hai. Is page mein ek saral, **prachlit roop** ki vidhi di gayi hai. Kisi ke dar ya bhay ke aadhar par nahi, balki shraddha aur anushasan ke bhaav se puja karein. Parivar ya sampraday ki alag parampara ho to wahi follow karein.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Taiyari">
    Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Parampara mein kaale ya gehre rang ke kapde pehne jaate hain, par yeh zaroori nahi hai. Puja ki jagah saaf karein aur asan par baithein. Disha ke baare mein parivar ki parampara follow karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sankalp">
    Haath mein jal lekar mann mein sankalp lein ki aap shraddha se Shani Dev ki puja kar rahe hain. Saral sankalp ke liye [Puja Sankalp Kaise Lein](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) dekhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Diya aur dhoop">
    Sarson ya til ke tel ka diya jalayein, agarbatti lagayein. Murti ya tasveer ke saamne jal arpit karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arpan">
    Kaale til aur phool arpit karein. Prasad rakhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mantra jaap">
    Shani mantra ka jaap karein. Sankhya apni shraddha ke anusaar rakhein, jaise 11, 21 ya 108 baar. Mala se jaap karna aasan rehta hai.
    <Mantra dev="ॐ शं शनैश्चराय नमः" rom="Om Sham Shanaishcharaya namah" arth="Dheere chalne wale Shanaishchar (Shani Dev) ko pranam." />
    Beej mantra, jise kai log prachlit roop mein jaap karte hain:
    <Mantra dev="ॐ प्रां प्रीं प्रौं सः शनैश्चराय नमः" rom="Om Pram Preem Praum Sah Shanaishcharaya namah" arth="Shani Dev ka beej mantra, jaap ke liye prachlit." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chalisa paath aur aarti">
    Shani Chalisa ka paath karein, phir [Shani Dev Ki Aarti](/surya-shani-navgraha/shani-dev-ki-aarti) gayein. Ant mein pranam karke galtiyon ke liye kshama prarthna karein aur prasad baantein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Peepal par jal aur daan (parampara)">
    Sham ko peepal ke ped par jal chadhane aur uske paas diya jalane ki parampara kai ghar mein hai. Isi tarah garib ko khana ya tel/til ka daan bhi prachlit hai. Yeh sab apni shraddha aur samarthya se karein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Shani Chalisa ke baare mein

Shani Chalisa Shani Dev ki stuti mein likhi gayi chalisa hai, jisme 40 chaupaiyan aur shuru mein doha hota hai. Kai chhapi hui pustakon mein iske paath mein antar milta hai. Kyunki hum paath mein koi galti nahi chhodna chahte, **poora paath abhi is page par nahi diya gaya**. Aap Gita Press jaisi kisi prakashit pustak se paath karein. Reviewer ki proofreading ke baad is site par jaancha hua paath jod diya jaayega. Chalisa mein Shani Dev ke roop, vahan aur prabhav ka varnan aur bhakt ki raksha ki prarthna ki jaati hai. Paath sthir man se, saaf jagah par baithkar, shuddh uchcharan ke saath karein. Chalisa ke baare mein aur aarti ke liye [Aarti Sangrah](/puja-basics/aarti-sangrah) bhi dekh sakte hain.

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- Shani Dev ko lekar darr phailane wali baaton par bharosa na karein. Puja ka bhaav shraddha aur anushasan hona chahiye.
- Daan apni samarthya se karein, kisi dabav mein nahi. Zarooratmand ko dena hi mool bhaav hai.
- Puja mein saaf-safai aur shanti rakhein.
- Agar aapne kisi pandit ya jyotishi se salah li hai to unki bataayi vidhi follow karein, par kisi bhi paisa-kharch wale upay ke liye jaldbaazi na karein. Kharch ke baare mein [Puja Ka Kharcha aur Pandit Dakshina](/puja-basics/puja-ka-kharcha-pandit-dakshina) padh sakte hain.
- Tel ke diye ko hawa se door aur surakshit jagah par rakhein. Aag ka dhyan zaroor rakhein.

## Mahatva

Shani Dev ko Surya aur Chhaya ke putra aur navgrahon mein nyay ke karak maana jaata hai. Parampara mein kaha jaata hai ki shraddha, sacchai aur mehnat ke saath kiya gaya karm hi sabse bada upay hai. Shani ki puja iski yaad dilaati hai. Navgraha ki saamoohik puja ke liye [Navgraha Puja Vidhi aur Mantra](/surya-shani-navgraha/navgraha-puja-vidhi-mantra) bhi dekhein.
