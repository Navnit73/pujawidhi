Navgraha puja mein Surya, Chandra, Mangal, Budh, Guru (Brihaspati), Shukra, Shani, Rahu aur Ketu, in nau grahon ki ek saath puja ki jaati hai. Ise graha-shanti, grih-pravesh, naye kaam ki shuruaat aur kai bade anushthanon mein prachlit roop se kiya jaata hai. Yeh page ghar ke liye ek **saral prachlit vidhi** deta hai. Bade havan ya graha-shanti anushthan ke liye kisi yogya pandit se milein, aur apni kundli ya jyotish ki salah ke baare mein bhi unse hi baat karein.

## Navgraha ek nazar mein

Neeche prachlit sanyojan diya gaya hai. Anaj, rang aur din ki suchi alag-alag paramparaon mein thodi alag milti hai, isliye apne pandit ya parivaar ki parampara ko pramaan maanein.

- **Surya**: Rawivar, laal rang, gehun.
- **Chandra**: Somvar, safed rang, chawal.
- **Mangal**: Mangalvar, laal rang, masoor ya arhar ki daal (parampara ke anusaar).
- **Budh**: Budhvar, hara rang, moong.
- **Guru (Brihaspati)**: Guruvar, peela rang, chana daal.
- **Shukra**: Shukravar, safed ya halka rang, chawal ya cheeni (parampara ke anusaar).
- **Shani**: Shanivar, kaala ya neela rang, kaale til ya urad.
- **Rahu**: Dhuaan ya gehra rang, urad (parampara ke anusaar).
- **Ketu**: Rangbirangi ya dhoomil rang, kulthi ya til (parampara ke anusaar).

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Taiyari aur sthapna">
    Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Chowki par kapda bichayein. Navgraha yantra ya 9 dheri taiyar karein: beech mein Surya, aur baaki grah apni disha mein, jaise parivar ya pandit ji batayein. Kalash mein jal bharkar rakhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ganesh puja">
    Kisi bhi puja ki tarah pehle Ganesh ji ka smaran karein, kyunki unhein pratham puja ka adhikar hai. Ganesh puja ke liye [Ganesh Puja Vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-puja-vidhi) dekh sakte hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sankalp">
    Haath mein jal, akshat aur phool lekar sankalp lein ki aap graha-shanti ya shubh karya ke liye navgraha ki puja shraddha se kar rahe hain. [Puja Sankalp Kaise Lein](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) mein saral tareeka diya hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navgraha ka avahan aur puja">
    Har graha ko roli, akshat, phool, dhoop aur diya arpit karein aur uska mantra bolein. Neeche prachlit beej mantra diye gaye hain.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Graha ke anaj arpit karein">
    Har graha ke saamne uska anaj thoda-thoda arpit karein. Chahein to alag-alag kapde ke tukde ya rang ke phool bhi rakh sakte hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aarti aur pranam">
    Diya aur kapoor jalakar aarti karein, phir sabhi grahon ko pranam karke shanti aur kalyan ki prarthna karein. Kisi bhi mantra ya vidhi mein galti ho to kshama prarthna karein. Prasad baantein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Daan (yathashakti)">
    Puja ke ant mein zarooratmand ko anaj, vastra ya apni samarthya ke anusaar daan dene ki parampara hai. Daan dabav mein nahi, shraddha se karein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- Graha-shanti ke naam par darr phailane wali baaton ya mehenge upayon ke dabav mein na aayein. Kisi bhi bade upay se pehle ghar ke bade ya bharosemand pandit se salah lein.
- Kisi ek hi graha ki puja karni ho to uski din aur parampara dekhein; sab grahon ko ek saath pooja karna zaroori nahi.
- Beej mantra ka uchcharan sahi rakhein. Na aaye to sirf naam-mantra, jaise Om Suryaya namah, padhein.
- Havan karna ho to khuli, hawadar jagah aur surakshit tareeka apnayein.
- Puja ka phal koi guarantee nahi hota; ise shraddha aur sad-karm ki prerna ke roop mein dekhein. Kharch ke baare mein [Puja Ka Kharcha aur Pandit Dakshina](/puja-basics/puja-ka-kharcha-pandit-dakshina) padhein.

## Mahatva

Jyotish ki parampara mein navgraha ko manushya ke jeevan ke alag-alag pehluon se joda jaata hai. Kai log inki puja shanti aur santulan ki kaamna se karte hain. Alag-alag grahon ki puja ke liye [Surya Dev Ki Aarti aur Arghya Vidhi](/surya-shani-navgraha/surya-dev-ki-aarti-arghya-vidhi) aur [Shanivar Puja Vidhi](/surya-shani-navgraha/shanivar-puja-vidhi-shani-chalisa) bhi dekh sakte hain.

## FAQ

<FAQ>
  <Q q="Navgraha puja kab karni chahiye?">Shubh muhurat mein, ya grih-pravesh aur naye kaam ke saath. Muhurat apne shehar ke panchang se dekhein, kyunki tithi aur samay jagah ke hisaab se badalte hain.</Q>
  <Q q="Kya navgraha puja ghar par ho sakti hai?">Haan, saral roop mein ghar par ho sakti hai. Havan ya bada graha-shanti anushthan ho to yogya pandit ji se karwana behtar hai.</Q>
  <Q q="Kya navgraha ke sab mantra ek din mein bolne zaroori hain?">Saral puja mein sabhi nau beej mantra ek-ek baar bole jaate hain. Jaap ki sankhya pandit ji ya parampara ke anusaar tay hoti hai.</Q>
  <Q q="Navgraha puja mein kaun se rang ke kapde chahiye?">Koi ek anivarya rang nahi hai. Kai log har graha ke rang ke kapde ya phool rakhte hain, par saaf kapde aur shraddha mukhya hai.</Q>
  <Q q="Kya navgraha puja se kundli ke dosh door ho jaate hain?">Is par koi guarantee nahi di ja sakti. Puja shraddha aur shanti ka madhyam hai. Kundli ke baare mein kisi jaankaar jyotishi se salah leni chahiye.</Q>
</FAQ>
