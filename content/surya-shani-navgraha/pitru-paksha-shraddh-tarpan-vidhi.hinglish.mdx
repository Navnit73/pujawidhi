Pitru Paksha mein hum apne divangat purvajon (pitron) ko shraddha se yaad karte hain. Is dauran **shraddh** (bhojan aur daan ke saath smaran) aur **tarpan** (jal arpan) kiya jaata hai. Yeh page ghar ke liye ek saral, **prachlit roop** ki vidhi batata hai. Shraddh ke niyam kul, kshetra aur sampraday ke hisaab se kaafi alag ho sakte hain, isliye gotra, tithi aur vidhi ke baare mein apne kul-purohit ya ghar ke bujurg se zaroor poochhein. Gaya, Haridwar ya Prayagraj jaise tirthon par shraddh ki apni alag paramparayein hain.

## Pitru Paksha kya hai

Bhadrapad Purnima ke baad Krishna paksha ke pandrah din, jo Ashwin Krishna Amavasya tak chalte hain, Pitru Paksha ke roop mein jaane jaate hain. Maanyata hai ki is kaal mein pitron ke naam par kiya gaya shraddh aur tarpan unhein tript karta hai. Iske peeche shraddha, kritagyata aur parivaar ki yaad ka bhaav hai. Bengal aur kai doosre kshetron mein is paksha ka ant Mahalaya ke roop mein manaya jaata hai. Mahalaya ke baare mein [Mahalaya Puja aur Tarpan Vidhi](/navratri-devi/mahalaya-puja-tarpan-vidhi) dekhein.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tithi aur sankalp">
    Pehle tay karein ki kis tithi par shraddh karna hai. Tithi panchang ke saath apne kul-purohit se milakar tay karein. Subah snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein aur shraddh ka sankalp lein. [Puja Sankalp Kaise Lein](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) mein sankalp ka saral roop diya hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ghar aur sthaan ki safai">
    Ghar saaf karein. Shraddh karne ki jagah, jaise aangan ya chhat, saaf karke gobar ya jal se shuddh karne ki parampara hai. Dakshin disha ki taraf baithne ki jagah rakhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tarpan">
    Ek patra mein jal, kaale til aur kusha rakhein. Apne pitron ka naam aur gotra lekar, dakshin ki taraf munh karke, jal ko angootha aur tarjani ke beech se dheere-dheere patra mein ya zameen par girayein. Tarpan har pitar ke liye (pita, pitamah, prapitamah, mata paksh ke purvaj) alag-alag naam se hota hai, jaisa pandit ji bataayein. Saral roop mein neeche ka mantra bolte hain.
    <Mantra dev="ॐ पितृदेवताभ्यो नमः" rom="Om Pitri-devatabhyo namah" arth="Pitron aur pitri-devtaon ko pranam." />
    Tarpan ke dauran janeu ko apsavya (daayen kandhe par) rakhne aur dakshin ki taraf munh karne ki parampara hai, par yeh vidhi parivaar ke anusaar badalti hai. Sahi vidhi ke liye pandit ji se seekh lein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pind daan (jahan parampara ho)">
    Jahan parivaar mein pind daan ki parampara hai, wahan pakke chawal, jau ya til ke aate se pind banakar naam ke saath pitron ko arpit kiye jaate hain. Yeh vidhi pandit ji ke maargdarshan mein karna uchit hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bhojan aur panchbali">
    Shuddh sattvik bhojan banakar uska ek bhaag gaay, kutte, kauwe, devta aur cheentiyon ke liye nikaalne ki parampara hai, jise panchbali kaha jaata hai. Phir Brahman ya zarooratmand ko shraddha se bhojan karayein. Antar mein parivaar ke log prasad ke roop mein bhojan karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Daan aur pranam">
    Vastra, anaj ya dakshina yathashakti daan karein. Antar mein pitron ko pranam karke unke aashirwad aur parivaar ki shanti ki prarthna karein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- Tithi ki jankari panchang aur purohit se pakki kar lein. Alag-alag panchang mein tithi ka samay thoda alag ho sakta hai.
- Shraddh ke bhojan mein lehsun, pyaaz aur tamasik cheezein avoid karne ki parampara hai.
- Pitru Paksha mein naya vyapar ya shubh karya (jaise vivah) shuru na karne ki prachlit maanyata hai. Yeh kaafi parivaron aur kshetron mein alag hoti hai, isliye ghar ke bade se poochhein.
- Shraddh dikhave ke liye nahi, shraddha ke liye hai. Apni aarthik sthiti ke anusaar saral roop mein bhi kiya ja sakta hai. Kharch ke baare mein [Puja Ka Kharcha aur Pandit Dakshina](/puja-basics/puja-ka-kharcha-pandit-dakshina) padhein.
- Jinka koi shraddh karne wala na ho ya vidhi na aati ho, unke liye bhi saral jal-arpan aur smaran ki parampara hai. Apne purohit se sahi tareeka poochhein.

## Mahatva

Hindu parampara mein pitru-rin ki bhavna rahi hai, yaani mata-pita aur purvajon ke prati kritagyata. Pitru Paksha is rin ko yaad karne aur chukane ka avsar maana jaata hai. Kai kathayein is paksha se judi hain, jinmein se kuch kshetra-vishesh hain, isliye hum yahan kisi ek ko nishchit roop se nahi likh rahe hain. Ghar ki katha-parampara ko hi maanein.

  <Q q="Kya mahilayein tarpan kar sakti hain?">Alag-alag paramparaon mein is par alag mat hain. Kai parivaron mein putra, pautra ya purush sadasya karte hain, aur kai jagah anya sadasya bhi. Apne kul-purohit se poochhein.</Q>

  <Q q="Kya shraddh aur Mahalaya ek hi hain?">Mahalaya Pitru Paksha ki Amavasya ko kaha jaata hai, khaaskar Bengal mein. Poora paksha shraddh ke liye maana jaata hai, aur Mahalaya uska ant din hai.</Q>
