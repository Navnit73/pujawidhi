## Aarti ke bol

Shani Dev ki aarti ("Jai Jai Shri Shanidev, bhaktan hitkari" se shuru hone wali) kai chhapi hui pustakon mein thode alag shabdon aur stanzas ke saath milti hai. ## Arth (meaning)

Aarti ka saar yeh hai ki Shani Dev Surya ke putra aur Chhaya ke santan hain, jinka roop shyam (kaala) varn aur vahan kaag (kauwa) ya anya prachlit vahan maana jaata hai. Unko nyay ke devta ke roop mein yaad kiya jaata hai, jo har vyakti ko uske karm ke anusaar phal dete hain. Aarti mein bhakt shanti, raksha aur sahi marg par chalne ki shakti ki prarthna karta hai. Kuch shabd aur vishesh upnaam kshetra ke anusaar alag ho sakte hain, isliye bhavarth ko hi mool maanein.

## Kab aur kaise gayein

Shanivar ko snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Shani Dev ki murti ya tasveer ke saamne ya Shani mandir mein sarson ya til ke tel ka diya jalayein. Pehle Shani mantra ya naam-jaap karein, phir aarti gayein. Aarti ke dauran thali ko ghadi ki disha mein ghumayein aur ant mein "kshama prarthana" karein.

## Sources aur variants

Shani aarti ke alag-alag sanskaran (versions) alag pustakon aur sthaanon par milte hain. Mool bhaav ek hi rehta hai. Mandir ki parampara aur ghar ki parampara mein thoda antar ho sakta hai. Jo paath aapke parivar mein gaya jaata hai usi ko prathamikta dein.
