Chhath Puja Surya Dev aur Chhathi Maiya ko samarpit char din ka vrat hai. Yeh mukhya roop se Bihar, Jharkhand, purvi Uttar Pradesh aur Nepal ke Terai kshetra mein manaya jaata hai, aur ab desh-videsh mein jahan bhi in kshetron ke log hain wahan bhi. Neeche **prachlit roop** ki vidhi di gayi hai. Gaon, parivar aur ghat ke hisaab se chhoti-moti baatein badal sakti hain, isliye ghar ke bade ya anubhavi vratdhari se zaroor poochhein.

## Chhath Puja kya hai

Chhath ka naam 'shashthi' (chhathi tithi) se juda hai, kyunki mukhya puja Kartik Shukla Shashthi ko hoti hai. Isme Surya ki upasana pratyaksh devta ke roop mein ki jaati hai aur santan ke swasthya aur parivar ki sukh-shanti ki kaamna se vrat rakha jaata hai. Isme koi murti ya pujari ki zaroorat nahi hoti; vratdhari (parvaitin) khud jal ke kinare, nadi, talab ya ghar ki banayi hui jagah par arghya deta hai. Safai aur shuddhata is parv ka sabse bada niyam maana jaata hai.

## Din 1: Nahay-khay

Pehle din vratdhari snaan karke ghar aur rasoi ki poori safai karte hain. Phir shuddh bhojan banta hai, jisme prachlit roop se kaddu (lauki) ki sabzi, chana dal aur chawal hota hai. Yeh bhojan pehle vratdhari grahan karte hain, uske baad parivar. Kai parivaron mein is din se lehsun-pyaaz ka tyag kiya jaata hai aur saindha namak prayog hota hai, par yeh parivar ki parampara par nirbhar hai.

## Din 2: Kharna (Lohanda)

Doosre din, yaani Panchami ko, vratdhari din bhar bina jal ke upvaas rakhte hain. Sham ko naye chulhe par, aam ki lakdi ya mitti ke chulhe par, gud ki kheer (rasiya), rotiyan aur kele ka prasad banta hai. Surya aur Chhathi Maiya ko bhog lagakar vratdhari ise grahan karte hain, phir parivar aur padosi prasad lete hain. Isi ke baad lagbhag 36 ghante ka nirjala vrat shuru hota hai. Prasad grahan karte samay shaanti rakhne ki parampara hai.

## Din 3: Sandhya Arghya

Teesra din sabse mukhya din hai. Din mein prasad taiyaar hota hai: **thekua**, chawal ke aate ke laddu (kasar), phal, ganna, nariyal aur mausami phal. Inhe baans ke soop ya daura (tokri) mein saja kar rakha jaata hai. Sham ko parivar ke saath ghat par jaate hain. Prachlit roop mein purush ya parivar ke sadasya daura sir par uthakar le jaate hain, aur raste mein Chhath ke geet gaye jaate hain.

Ghat par vratdhari kamar tak ya ghutne tak jal mein khade hokar doobte Surya ki taraf munh karte hain. Soop haath mein lekar jal ya doodh se arghya diya jaata hai, aur parivar ke log soop mein jal ya doodh chadhate hain. Diye jalakar soop mein rakhe jaate hain. Raat ko kai parivar ghar par kosi bharte hain: ganne ke samooh ke neeche mitti ke haathi aur diye rakhkar geet gaye jaate hain (yeh kshetra aur parivar ke anusaar hota hai).

## Din 4: Usha Arghya aur paran

Chauthe din subah suryoday se pehle hi ghat par pahunch jaate hain. Phir jal mein khade hokar ugte Surya ko arghya diya jaata hai. Pooja ke baad vratdhari adrak aur thoda sa prasad ya jal grahan karke vrat ka **paran** karte hain, phir prasad baanta jaata hai. Ghat par bheed ho to dhairya aur anushasan rakhein.

## Samagri ki saamanya suchi

Soop ya daura, thekua, kasar ke laddu, kele ka ghaur (gucha), nariyal, ganna, nimbu, mausami phal, supari, paan, haldi ya adrak ka paudha, diya, agarbatti, sindoor, akshat aur doodh ya jal ka lota. Sabki saamagri parivaron mein thodi alag hoti hai, aur kuch cheezein kshetra par nirbhar karti hain. Koi vastu na mile to bade se poochhein, ya jo sambhav ho wahi shraddha se arpit karein. Aur saamagri ke liye [Puja Samagri Master List](/puja-basics/puja-samagri-master-list) ko bhi dekh sakte hain.

## Niyam aur savdhaniyan

- Safai ka poora dhyan rakhein. Prasad banane se pehle haath dhoyein aur alag bartan prayog karein.
- Prasad ke liye naya ya sirf isi kaam ke liye rakha chulha aur bartan prayog karne ki parampara hai.
- Nirjala vrat sabke liye aasan nahi hota. Garbhavati mahilaon, bujurgon, bimaar ya dawai lene wale log doctor aur ghar ke bade se salah lekar hi vrat ka roop tay karein. Vrat parampara ka hissa hai, par swasthya sabse pehle.
- Ghat par jaate samay tairna na aane wale bachchon aur bujurgon ko jal ke paas akela na chhodein. Gehre paani mein na utrein, sthaniya prashasan ya samiti dwara bataye gaye safe ghat par hi khade ho.
- Raat ya subah ke arghya mein diye jalakar ghat par le jaate samay kapde aur sooje ko aag se bachayein.
- Kai ghaton par prashasan pehle se tarikh aur vyavastha ki soochna deta hai. Apne shehar ke ghat ka sthaniya update zaroor dekh lein.

## Chhathi Maiya aur Surya ki maanyata

Lok parampara mein Chhathi Maiya ko santan ki raksha karne wali devi maana jaata hai, aur Surya ko jeevan aur urja ka srot. Iski kai kathayein prachlit hain jo kshetra ke anusaar alag hain, isliye yahan ek katha ko hi poora satya bata kar nahi likha ja raha. Aap apne parivar ki katha aur geet ka paath jaise sunte aaye hain waise hi karein.
