Surya Dev ko hamari parampara mein pratyaksh devta kaha jaata hai, kyunki unke darshan roz hote hain. Subah suryoday ke samay jal se arghya dena aur Surya ki aarti ya stuti karna ghar ki sabse saral aur purani pujaon mein se ek hai. Is page mein **arghya dene ki vidhi**, mantra aur aarti gane ka tareeka diya gaya hai.

## Aarti ke bol aur paath ke baare mein

Surya Dev ki aarti ("Om Jai Surya Bhagwan" ke roop mein prachlit) alag-alag kshetron aur chhapi hui aarti sangrah pustakon mein thode alag shabdon ke saath milti hai. Hum yahan poora paath tab tak nahi likh rahe jab tak kisi jaankaar pandit se uska satyapan na ho jaaye. Tab tak aap Gita Press jaisi kisi prakashit aarti sangrah pustak se gayein. Is site par poora, jaancha hua paath reviewer ki proofreading ke baad jod diya jaayega. Bhaav ke liye aarti mein Surya ko jagat ke netra, andhkar nashak, prakash aur urja ke datta ke roop mein yaad kiya jaata hai. Doosre aarti paath ke liye [Aarti Sangrah](/puja-basics/aarti-sangrah) dekhein.

## Arghya dene ki vidhi

<Steps>
  <Step title="Snaan aur taiyari">
    Subah snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Ek saaf lote mein jal bharein. Parampara mein usme thoda laal phool, roli ya chawal daalte hain. Jo na ho to sirf jal bhi paryapt hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sahi jagah par khade hon">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Arghya mantra">

## Kab aur kaise gayein

Aarti ya stuti arghya ke baad ya shaam ki puja mein gayi ja sakti hai. Diya jalakar thali ko Surya ya Surya ki tasveer ke saamne ghadi ki disha mein ghumayein. Aarti ke baad jal ya prasad grahan karein. Adityahridayam stotra ka paath bhi Surya upasana mein prachlit hai. Ise Gita Press ya kisi prakashit pustak se hi padhein, aur yadi samajh na aaye to kisi jaankaar se seekh lein.

## Niyam aur dhyan dene ki baatein

- Surya ko arghya dene ke liye vrat rakhna zaroori nahi hai; yeh roz ki saral saadhna hai.
- Taambe ke lote ka prayog parampara mein prachlit hai, par agar na ho to shraddha se kisi bhi saaf bartan ka prayog theek hai.
- Arghya dete samay shaant man rakhein. Mobile se door rehna achha hai.
- Shaaririk dikkat ho to baith kar bhi arghya diya ja sakta hai; bhaav sabse mahatvapoorn hai.
- Chhath jaise bade parv par arghya ki vidhi alag aur lambi hoti hai. Uske liye [Chhath Puja Vidhi](/surya-shani-navgraha/chhath-puja-vidhi-4-din) padhein.

## Variants aur sources

Arghya mantra aur Surya naam alag-alag parivaron aur sampradayon mein alag ho sakte hain. Yahan diya gaya mantra prachlit roop hai. Sahi uchcharan ke liye kisi jaankaar ya prakashit granth ki madad lein.
