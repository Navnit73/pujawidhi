Maha Shivratri Shiv ji ka sabse bada parv maana jaata hai. Prachlit manyata ke anusaar yeh Phalgun maas ki Krishna paksha ki Chaturdashi ko manayi jaati hai (Amanta calendar mein ise Magha maas ki Krishna Chaturdashi kehte hain). Is raat vrat, jagran aur Shiv puja ki parampara hai. Yeh page ghar par **4 prahar ki puja** ki saaf vidhi deta hai. Tithi aur muhurat shehar ke hisaab se badalte hain, isliye apne shehar ka panchang zaroor dekhein. Aapke parivar mein alag paddhati ho to wahi maanein.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Subah ka sankalp">
    Subah snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Shiv ji ke saamne haath mein jal, akshat aur phool lekar vrat aur jagran ka sankalp lein. Saral roop mein: "Main aaj Maha Shivratri ka vrat aur Shiv puja shraddha se kar raha/rahi hoon." Din bhar upvaas ya phalahar prachlit hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shaam ki taiyari">
    Shaam ko dobara snaan karein. Puja sthaan saaf karein. Shivling ya tasveer ko saaf thali mein rakhein. Samagri paas mein saja lein, taaki raat mein hadbadi na ho.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pehla prahar">
    Prachlit roop mein raat ko chaar prahar mein puja ki jaati hai. Pehle prahar mein Shivling par jal, phir doodh se abhishek karein. Chandan, bel patra aur phool chadhayein. "Om Namah Shivaya" ka jaap karein.
    <Mantra dev="ॐ नमः शिवाय" rom="Om Namah Shivaya" arth="Main Shiv ji ko pranaam karta/karti hoon." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Doosra prahar">
    Doosre prahar mein jal ke baad dahi se abhishek kar sakte hain. Bel patra aur phool chadhayein. Dhoop, deep dikhayein aur jaap karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Teesra prahar">
    Teesre prahar mein ghee se abhishek kar sakte hain. Is samay ko Nishita kaal ke aas-paas maana jaata hai. Nishita kaal ka sateek samay apne shehar ke panchang se dekhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chautha prahar">
    Chauthe prahar mein shahad se abhishek kar sakte hain. Ant mein saaf jal se snaan karayein. Phir [Shiv ji ki aarti](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-aarti) gayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paran">
    Agli subah snaan karke puja karein aur prasad grahan karke vrat kholein. Paran ka samay panchang ke anusaar dekhein. Kuch parampara mein kram aur dravya alag hote hain, jaise chaar prahar mein alag-alag dravya. Apne pandit ji se poochh lein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Kshamta ke anusaar vrat:** Bimar, garbhvati ya bujurg log kathin upvaas na karein. Phalahar bhi sweekar hai. Swasthya ka sawaal ho to doctor se salaah lein.
- **Jagran:** Jagran ka arth hai raat mein Shiv naam, bhajan aur paath mein man lagana. Neend aaye to apni kshamta ke hisaab se aaram karein.
- **Tulsi aur ketaki:** Prachlit parampara mein Shivling par tulsi aur ketaki nahi chadhayi jaati. Vistaar ke liye [Shiv ji ki puja vidhi](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-puja-vidhi) dekhein.
- **Bheed aur suraksha:** Mandir jayein to bheed ke samay dhairya rakhein, bachchon aur bujurgon ka dhyan rakhein, aur mandir prashasan ke niyam maanein.
- **Parampara:** Chaar prahar ke kram aur dravya mein antar milta hai. Apne kul ki paddhati maanein.

## Mahatva aur katha

Prachlit manyata ke anusaar Maha Shivratri ko Shiv ji aur Parvati ji ke vivah se jodkar bhi dekha jaata hai, aur kuch parampara mein ise Shiv ke prakat hone ki raat maana jaata hai. Alag-alag purano mein alag kathayein milti hain. Ek lokpriya katha shikari ki hai, jo anjaane mein bel ke ped par chadhkar raat bhar patte neeche Shivling par girata raha, aur use Shiv ji ki kripa mili. Yeh katha aastha aur parampara ke roop mein sunayi jaati hai. Har maas ki Krishna Chaturdashi ko Masik Shivratri bhi hoti hai. Abhishek ki vidhi ke liye [Shivling abhishek vidhi](/shiv-ji/shivling-abhishek-vidhi) aur bade anushthan ke liye [Rudrabhishek vidhi aur samagri](/shiv-ji/rudrabhishek-vidhi-samagri) dekhein.
