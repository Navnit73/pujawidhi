Sawan (Shravan maas) Shiv ji ki upasana ka sabse khaas mahina maana jaata hai, aur is maas ke Somvar ka vishesh mahatva hai. Yeh guide batata hai ki Sawan kab shuru hota hai, Somvar vrat aur puja kaise karein, Kanwar yatra ke baare mein aam jaankari, aur kin baaton ka dhyan rakhein. Sawan ki tithiyan aur Somvar ki tarikhein har saal badalti hain, isliye apne shehar ka panchang dekhein. Hum koi tarikh nahi de rahe.

## Sawan ka mahatva

Prachlit manyata ke anusaar Samudra manthan isi samay se jodkar dekha jaata hai: jab halahal vish nikla, Shiv ji ne use kanth mein dharan karke sansar ki raksha ki, aur devtaon ne jal se unka abhishek kiya. Isiliye Sawan mein Shivling par jal chadhane ki parampara bahut vishesh hai. Yeh vishwas aur parampara ki baat hai. Is mahine mein barsaat ka mausam bhi hota hai, aur kai kshetron mein haraa-bhara, shaant vatavaran bhakti ke liye mahatvapoorn maana jaata hai.

## Sawan Somvar mein kya karein

- **Somvar vrat:** Sawan ke har Somvar ko vrat rakhne ki parampara hai. Vidhi aur katha ke liye [Somvar vrat vidhi aur katha](/shiv-ji/somvar-vrat-vidhi-katha) dekhein.
- **Roz ki puja:** Kuch log poore Sawan roz Shivling par jal chadhate hain. Vidhi ke liye [Shiv ji ki puja vidhi](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-puja-vidhi) aur [Shivling abhishek vidhi](/shiv-ji/shivling-abhishek-vidhi) dekhein.
- **Jaap:** "Om Namah Shivaya" ka jaap. Vistaar ke liye [Om Namah Shivaya jaap vidhi](/shiv-ji/om-namah-shivaya-jaap-vidhi) dekhein.
- **Pradosh:** Sawan ki Trayodashi par [Pradosh vrat](/shiv-ji/pradosh-vrat-vidhi-katha) bhi prachlit hai.
- **Rudrabhishek:** Kuch log Sawan mein pandit ji se [Rudrabhishek](/shiv-ji/rudrabhishek-vidhi-samagri) karwate hain.
- **Aarti aur paath:** [Shiv ji ki aarti](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-aarti) aur [Shiv Chalisa](/shiv-ji/shiv-chalisa).

## Ghar par Sawan Somvar puja ki saral vidhi

1. Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein aur Shiv ji ke saamne diya jalayein.
2. Sankalp lein: "Main aaj Sawan Somvar ka vrat aur puja shraddha se kar raha/rahi hoon."
3. Shivling par saaf jal chadhayein, "Om Namah Shivaya" bolte hue. Chahein to doodh, dahi, shahad se bhi abhishek karein.
4. Chandan, bhasma, bel patra (teen patti wale) aur safed phool arpit karein.
5. Dhoop, deep dikhayein, bhog lagayein aur aarti gayein.
6. Shaam ko dobara diya jalakar aarti karein. Vrat ka paran puja ke baad prasad se karein.

## Kanwar yatra

Sawan mein kai kshetron mein Kanwar yatra ki parampara hai, jisme shraddhalu pavitra nadi (jaise Ganga) se jal laakar Shiv mandir mein Shivling par chadhate hain. Yeh parampara mukhya roop se uttar Bharat mein dekhi jaati hai. Agar aap yatra mein shamil hon ya yatra ke raste se guzarna ho, to kuch aam baaton ka dhyan rakhein: sthaniya prashasan ki yatra aur traffic ke nirdesh dekhein, bheed mein bachchon aur bujurgon ka khayal rakhein, paani aur aaram ka dhyan rakhein, aur kisi bhi vyavastha ya marg ki taza jaankari ke liye sthaniya prashasan ya aayojakon ki official suchna dekhein. Hum yatra ke marg ya samay ki jaankari nahi de rahe.

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Kshamta ke anusaar vrat:** Bimar, garbhvati ya bujurg log kathin upvaas na karein. Phalahar sweekar hai. Swasthya ka sawaal ho to doctor se poochhein.
- **Tulsi aur ketaki:** Prachlit parampara mein Shivling par tulsi aur ketaki nahi chadhayi jaati.
- **Kshetra ka antar:** Sawan ki shuruaat aur Somvar ki ginti panchang ke prakar ke anusaar badalti hai. Apne shehar ka panchang dekhein.
- **Bheed:** Mandir mein Sawan Somvar ko bheed hoti hai. Dhairya rakhein aur mandir ke niyam maanein.
- **Parampara:** Puja aur bhojan ke niyam parivar ke anusaar badalte hain. Bade ya pandit ji se poochhein.
