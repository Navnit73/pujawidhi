Rudrabhishek Shiv ji ke Rudra roop ka vaidik mantron ke saath kiya jaane wala abhishek hai. Isme aam taur par Yajurveda ke Rudradhyay (Shri Rudram, jiske Namak aur Chamak bhaag prasiddh hain) ka paath hota hai, aur saath mein Shivling par jal ya anya dravya chadhaye jaate hain. Yeh saral jal abhishek se alag, ek vidhi-vidhaan wala anushthan hai, jo aam taur par yogya pandit ji ke maargdarshan mein karwaya jaata hai. Is page par samagri, kram aur taiyari ki jaankari hai. Kharch aur dakshina ke liye koi rakam nahi di gayi hai, kyunki yeh shehar, pandit ji aur anushthan ke aakaar par nirbhar karta hai.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pandit ji aur muhurat tay karein">
    Anushthan ki tithi aur samay pandit ji se tay karein. Panchang dekhkar achha din chunein (Somvar, Pradosh, Shivratri jaise). Poochh lein ki kitni baar paath hoga, kitne pandit honge aur kaun si samagri aap laayenge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shuddhi aur sankalp">
    Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Pandit ji ke saath aachman aur sankalp karein. Sankalp mein apna naam, gotra, sthaan, tithi aur anushthan ka uddeshya bola jaata hai. Vistaar ke liye [Puja sankalp kaise lein](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) dekhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ganesh aur kalash puja">
    Kisi bhi bade anushthan se pehle Ganesh ji ki puja ki jaati hai. Phir kalash sthapit kar jal bharkar usme dhyan kiya jaata hai. Ganesh ji ki puja ke liye [Ganesh puja vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-puja-vidhi) dekhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shivling ki sthapna aur avahan">
    Shivling ko abhishek paatr mein rakhein. Dhyan karein aur avahan karein.
    <Mantra dev="ॐ नमः शिवाय" rom="Om Namah Shivaya" arth="Main Shiv ji ko pranaam karta/karti hoon." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rudri paath ke saath abhishek">
    Pandit ji Rudri (Rudradhyay) ka paath karte hain aur har khand par Shivling par dhaar se jal chadhate hain. Phir parampara ke anusaar doodh, dahi, ghee, shahad, cheeni ka abhishek hota hai, aur ant mein saaf jal se snaan. Aap bhi saath mein "Om Namah Shivaya" bol sakte hain. Paath chhapi pustak se hi hona chahiye, uchcharan ka dhyan rakhte hue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chandan, bel patra, phool">
    Abhishek ke baad Shivling ko poochhkar chandan, bhasma aur bel patra arpit karein. Phir safed phool chadhayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dhoop, deep, naivedya, aarti">
    Dhoop aur deep dikhayein. Naivedya arpit karein. Phir [Shiv ji ki aarti](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-aarti) gayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Purnahuti, dakshina aur prasad">
    Anushthan ke ant mein kshama prarthana karein, pandit ji ko dakshina dein aur prasad baantein. Kuch jagah havan bhi hota hai. Yeh pandit ji ke saath pehle tay kar lein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Bina jaanch ke paath nahi:** Vaidik mantron ka uchcharan sahi hona chahiye. Isliye yogya pandit ji se hi karwayein, ya chhapi pustak dekhkar dhyan se padhein.
- **Samagri pehle se poochh lein:** Pandit ji se pehle hi poochh lein ki kya aap laayenge aur kya ve laayenge.
- **Dakshina saaf rakhein:** Rakam, samay aur kya-kya shamil hai, yeh pehle tay kar lein. Is page par hum koi rakam nahi bata rahe, kyunki yeh shehar aur anushthan par nirbhar karta hai. Alag se jaankari ke liye [Puja ka kharcha aur pandit dakshina](/puja-basics/puja-ka-kharcha-pandit-dakshina) dekhein.
- **Parampara:** Dravya ka kram, kitne aavartan, kaun se phal ke liye kaun sa dravya, yeh sab parampara aur pandit ji ke anusaar alag hote hain. Kisi bhi prakar ke parinaam ka daava nahi kiya jaata.
- **Shuddhta:** Snaan, saaf kapde aur shaant man. Anushthan ke samay mobile silent rakhein.

## Mahatva

Rudra Shiv ji ka ugra aur sanharak roop maana jaata hai, jinki stuti Yajurveda ke Rudradhyay mein milti hai. Rudrabhishek ko parampara mein Shiv ji ki vishesh aaradhna maana jaata hai. Alag-alag dravya se abhishek ke alag-alag phal ki baat kuch granthon aur lok-parampara mein milti hai, par yeh vishwas ka vishay hai aur hum koi guarantee nahi dete. Laghu anushthan ke liye [Laghu Rudra Path vidhi](/shiv-ji/laghu-rudra-path-vidhi) aur saral abhishek ke liye [Shivling abhishek vidhi](/shiv-ji/shivling-abhishek-vidhi) dekhein.

## FAQ

<FAQ>
  <Q q="Rudrabhishek kab karna chahiye?">Somvar, Pradosh, Maha Shivratri aur Sawan mein prachlit hai. Kisi bhi shubh avsar par bhi karwaya ja sakta hai. Muhurat panchang ya pandit ji se tay karein.</Q>
  <Q q="Kya Rudrabhishek ghar par ho sakta hai?">Haan. Pandit ji ko ghar bulakar ya mandir mein karwa sakte hain. Saral roop mein khud bhi shraddha se Shivling abhishek kar sakte hain.</Q>
  <Q q="Rudrabhishek mein kitna kharch aata hai?">Yeh shehar, pandit ji, paath ki sankhya aur samagri par nirbhar karta hai. Pehle pandit ji se saaf baat kar lein ki kya shamil hai.</Q>
  <Q q="Rudrabhishek aur Shivling abhishek mein kya antar hai?">Shivling abhishek saral jal ya panchamrit abhishek hai. Rudrabhishek mein Rudri (Rudradhyay) ke vaidik mantron ka paath hota hai.</Q>
  <Q q="Rudrabhishek ke liye kaun si samagri pandit ji laate hain?">Yeh pandit ji aur jagah par nirbhar karta hai. Kuch pandit ji puja samagri saath laate hain, kuch aapse mangwate hain. Pehle poochh lein.</Q>
</FAQ>
