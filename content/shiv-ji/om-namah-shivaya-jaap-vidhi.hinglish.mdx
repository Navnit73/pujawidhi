"Om Namah Shivaya" Shiv ji ka sabse saral aur lokpriya mantra hai. Ise **Panchakshar mantra** kehte hain, kyunki "Na-Mah-Shi-Va-Ya" paanch akshar hain (Om ke saath ise shadakshar bhi kehte hain). Yeh Shiv upasana ka mool mantra maana jaata hai. Ise koi bhi, kisi bhi umar mein, shraddha se jaap sakta hai. Neeche arth, jaap ki vidhi aur niyam diye gaye hain.

## Arth (meaning)

- **Om:** Pranav, jise srishti ki mool dhwani maana jaata hai.
- **Namah:** pranaam, samarpan.
- **Shivaya:** Shiv ko, yaani kalyankari tattva ko.

Prachlit vyakhya mein paanch akshar ko paanch tattvon (prithvi, jal, agni, vayu, aakash) se jodkar bhi samjhaya jaata hai. Yeh ek parampara-aadharit vyakhya hai. Adi Shankaracharya ke "Shiv Panchakshar Stotra" mein is mantra ke paanch aksharon par stuti milti hai. Mantra ka bhaav hai: Shiv ke prati poorn samarpan.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

- **Kab:** Kisi bhi samay jaap sakte hain. Subah ka samay aur Somvar, Pradosh, Maha Shivratri, Sawan prachlit hain. Chalte-phirte man mein bhi smaran kiya ja sakta hai.
- **Kaise:** Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Shiv ji ke saamne diya jalakar aasan par baithein. Purv ya uttar ki taraf munh karna prachlit hai.
- **Mala:** Rudraksh ki mala se jaap prachlit hai. Ek mala mein 108 manke hote hain. Mala ko ungli aur angoothe se ghumayein, aam taur par tarjani (pehli ungli) ka istemal na karein.
- **Kitni baar:** Roz 1 mala (108 baar) ya apni kshamta ke hisaab se 11, 21, 51 baar. Kuch log Somvar ya Sawan mein adhik mala ka sankalp lete hain.
- **Jaap ke prakaar:** Vaikhari (bolkar), upanshu (dheere se, honth hilte hain) aur manasik (man mein). Jo aapko sahaj lage, wahi karein.
- **Sath mein:** Jaap ke baad [Shiv ji ki aarti](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-aarti) gayein. Puja ke liye [Shiv ji ki puja vidhi](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-puja-vidhi) dekhein. Vaidik mantra ke liye [Maha Mrityunjaya mantra](/shiv-ji/maha-mrityunjaya-mantra-jaap-vidhi) dekhein.

## Labh aur sahi apeksha

Parampara mein is mantra ko man ki shaanti, ekagrata aur bhakti badhane wala maana jaata hai. Hum koi guaranteed natija nahi batate. Jaap ka sabse pakka laabh yeh hai ki roz kuch der shaant baithne se man sthir mehsoos hota hai. Swasthya ya aarthik samasya mein vyavaharik upaay aur doctor ki salaah zaroori hain.

## Sources aur variants

"Om Namah Shivaya" ka ullekh Yajurveda ke Rudradhyay mein "Namah Shivaya" roop mein milta hai, aur Shaiv parampara mein ise mool mantra maana jaata hai. Kuch parampara mein "Namah Shivaya" bina "Om" ke jaapa jaata hai. Gurumantra ke roop mein deeksha lena ho to guru ya pandit ji se maargdarshan lein.
