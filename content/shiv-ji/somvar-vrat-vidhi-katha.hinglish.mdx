Somvar (Monday) Shiv ji ka priya din maana jaata hai, aur is din vrat rakhne ki parampara bahut purani hai. Kuch log sirf ek Somvar rakhte hain, kuch Sawan ke saare Somvar, aur kuch "solah Somvar" ka sankalp lete hain. Yeh page vrat ki vidhi, niyam aur prachlit katha saaf bhasha mein batata hai. Vrat ke niyam parivar aur kshetra ke hisaab se badalte hain, isliye apne bade ya pandit ji se bhi poochh lein.

## Vrat vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pehle din ki taiyari">
    Pichhli raat halka aur saatvik bhojan karein. Subah jaldi uthkar snaan karein aur saaf kapde pehnein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sankalp">
    Puja sthaan par baithkar haath mein jal, akshat aur phool lein. Mann mein vrat ka sankalp karein: "Main aaj Somvar ka vrat Shiv ji ke charnon mein shraddha se rakh raha/rahi hoon." Jal chhod dein. Vistaar ke liye [Puja sankalp kaise lein](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) dekhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shiv puja aur abhishek">
    Shivling par jal chadhayein, bel patra aur safed phool arpit karein. Poori vidhi ke liye [Shiv ji ki puja vidhi](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-puja-vidhi) aur [Shivling abhishek vidhi](/shiv-ji/shivling-abhishek-vidhi) dekhein.
    <Mantra dev="ॐ नमः शिवाय" rom="Om Namah Shivaya" arth="Main Shiv ji ko pranaam karta/karti hoon." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Din bhar">
    Mann mein "Om Namah Shivaya" ka smaran karte rahein. Krodh aur kadve vachan se bachein. Jo log upvaas nahi kar sakte, ve phalahar kar sakte hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Katha">
    Shaam ko Somvar vrat katha padhein ya sunein (neeche prachlit katha ka saar hai).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shaam ki puja aur aarti">
    Shaam ko dobara diya jalakar [Shiv ji ki aarti](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-aarti) gayein aur prasad baantein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paran">
    Puja ke baad prasad se bhojan grahan karein. Parampara mein ek samay saatvik bhojan karne ka niyam hai. Kuch parivar mein namak ya anaaj ke niyam alag hote hain. Apne parivar ke niyam maanein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Vrat kshamta ke hisaab se:** Bimar, garbhvati, bujurg ya bachche kathin upvaas na karein. Phalahar ya ek samay bhojan bhi sweekar hai. Swasthya sambandhi sawaal ho to doctor se salaah lein.
- **Jabardasti nahi:** Vrat shraddha ka vishay hai, dikhave ya dabaav ka nahi.
- **Pavitrata:** Snaan aur saaf kapde. Puja ke samay man shaant rakhein.
- **Sankalp poora karein:** Jitne Somvar ka sankalp liya ho, utne poore karne ki koshish karein. Kisi karan se chhoot jaye to pandit ji ya bade se poochhein.
- **Parampara:** Kya khaya jaye aur kya nahi, yeh kshetra aur parivar par nirbhar karta hai.

## Mahatva aur katha

Prachlit katha ke anusaar ek sahukar (vyapari) Shiv ji ka param bhakt tha, par uski koi santaan nahi thi. Uski bhakti dekhkar Parvati ji ne Shiv ji se kaha ki ise santaan ka vardaan dein. Shiv ji ne kaha ki karm ke anusaar iske bhaag mein santaan nahi, phir bhi Parvati ji ke aagrah par vardaan diya, par saath mein bataya ki putra alpayu hoga. Sahukar ne yeh baat chhupa li aur bhakti jaari rakhi. Bade hone par putra ko uske mama ke saath Kashi ki yatra par bheja gaya aur raste mein daan aur yagya karne ko kaha gaya. Katha ke anusaar yatra mein kai ghatnayein hoti hain, aur ant mein Shiv-Parvati ki kripa se putra ko deergh jeevan milta hai, tatha parivar mein khushi lautti hai. Katha ka sandesh yeh hai ki saccha bhakt Shiv ji par vishwas rakhta hai. Katha ke kuch ansh alag-alag pustakon mein alag milte hain. Poori katha ke liye Gita Press jaisi kisi chhapi vrat-katha pustak ka paath karein. Yeh katha aastha aur parampara ke roop mein hai.

Somvar ke saath Sawan ka sambandh bhi hai. Uske liye [Sawan Somvar puja guide](/shiv-ji/sawan-somvar-puja-guide) dekhein. Pradosh vrat ke liye [Pradosh vrat vidhi aur katha](/shiv-ji/pradosh-vrat-vidhi-katha) dekhein.
