Laghu Rudra ek bada vaidik anushthan hai, jisme Shiv ji ke Rudra roop ka paath kai aavartan mein kiya jaata hai. Is page par hum is anushthan ki saral jaankari de rahe hain: yeh kya hai, iski ganana kaise ki jaati hai, kaise taiyari karein aur kin baaton ka dhyan rakhein. **Vaidik mantron ka poora paath yahan nahi diya gaya hai.** Rudri ka paath bahut lamba hai aur uchcharan ki shuddhta zaroori hai, isliye ise kisi chhapi pustak (jaise Gita Press ka sanskaran) se ya yogya pandit ji ke maargdarshan mein hi padhein. ## Poora paath

Laghu Rudra mein jis paath ka aavartan hota hai, use **Rudri** ya **Rudradhyay** (Shri Rudram) kehte hain. Yeh Yajurveda ka stuti paath hai, jisme Rudra ko namaskar kiya gaya hai. Prachlit manyata ke anusaar yeh Shukla Yajurveda (Vajasaneyi Samhita) mein aur Krishna Yajurveda (Taittiriya Samhita) mein, dono mein milta hai, aur dakshin mein Shri Rudram naam se Namak-Chamak ka paath prasiddh hai. Is paath ke do prasiddh bhaag hain: **Namak** (jisme "namah" shabd baar-baar aata hai) aur **Chamak** (jisme "cha me" shabd aata hai). Shuddh uchcharan ke liye is paath ko pustak dekhkar ya guru/pandit ji ke saath seekhna chahiye.

Ek panktiyon ka chhota ansh nahi, balki poora paath hi pandit ji aur chhapi pustak se lein. Saral bhakti ke liye aap [Om Namah Shivaya jaap vidhi](/shiv-ji/om-namah-shivaya-jaap-vidhi) bhi dekh sakte hain.

## Arth (meaning)

Rudradhyay ka saar yeh hai ki isme Rudra (Shiv) ko sabhi roopon, jeevon aur shaktiyon mein vyaapt maankar baar-baar namaskar kiya gaya hai. Namak bhaag mein vividh roopon ko namah kaha gaya hai aur shaanti, mangal aur raksha ki prarthana hai. Chamak bhaag mein jeevan ki avashyakta aur sampannata ki prarthana "cha me" (mujhe ho) kehkar ki gayi hai. Is paath ka shabd-ba-shabd arth kisi vidvan pandit ya chhapi tika se samjhein.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

**Ganana (prachlit roop):** Is paath ki ganana ke liye ek prachlit kram bataya jaata hai.

- **Rudri paath:** Rudri ka ek aavartan.
- **Ekadashini:** Rudri ke 11 aavartan.
- **Laghu Rudra:** Ekadashini ke 11 aavartan, yaani kul 121 aavartan.
- **Maha Rudra:** Laghu Rudra ke 11 aavartan, yaani 1331.
- **Ati Rudra:** Maha Rudra ke 11 aavartan, yaani 14641.

Is ganana mein bhi kshetra aur pandit ji ke anusaar antar ho sakta hai. Kai baar 11 pandit ji milkar ek-ek ekadashini karte hain, jisse 121 aavartan ek din mein poore ho jaate hain. Aap pandit ji se yeh pehle tay kar lein.

**Kab:** Maha Shivratri, Sawan, Somvar, Pradosh ya kisi bhi shubh avsar par. Muhurat panchang ya pandit ji se poochhein.

**Kaise taiyari karein:**

1. Pandit ji se tithi, samay, pandit ji ki sankhya aur samagri ki list tay karein.
2. Shuddh sthaan chunein, Shivling ya Shiv ji ki murti ka prakshalan (snaan) karein.
3. Ganesh puja, kalash sthapana aur sankalp se anushthan shuru hota hai. [Ganesh puja vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-puja-vidhi) dekhein.
4. Rudri paath ke saath ya uske baad Shivling ka abhishek hota hai. [Rudrabhishek vidhi aur samagri](/shiv-ji/rudrabhishek-vidhi-samagri) dekhein.
5. Kai jagah ant mein havan aur aarti hoti hai. [Shiv ji ki aarti](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-aarti) gayi ja sakti hai.

**Dakshina aur kharch:** Yeh pandit ji ki sankhya, shehar aur anushthan ke aakaar par nirbhar karta hai. Is page par koi rakam nahi di gayi hai. Pehle saaf baat kar lein.
