Shiv ji ki sabse prachlit aarti **"Om Jai Shiv Omkara"** hai. Yeh Somvar, Pradosh, Maha Shivratri aur Sawan ki puja ke ant mein ghar aur mandir, dono jagah gayi jaati hai. Neeche poori aarti Devanagari aur Hinglish mein hai. Har do panktiyon ke baad refrain **"Om Har Har Har Mahadev"** bola jaata hai. ## Arth (meaning)

Aarti mein Shiv ji ko Omkar swaroop, yaani Pranav (Om) ke roop mein yaad kiya gaya hai. Pehle bhaag mein unke alag-alag roopon (ek, chaar, paanch mukh; do, chaar, das bhuja) aur vahanon ka varnan hai. Beech ke bhaag mein unki mala, vastra aur haathon ke chinhon ka varnan hai. Phir kaha gaya hai ki Brahma, Vishnu aur Sadashiv alag-alag nahi, ek hi tattva ke roop hain. Ant mein Kashi ke Vishwanath ka smaran aur phalashruti ki panktiyan hain.

## Kab aur kaise gayein

- **Kab:** Shiv puja ke ant mein. Somvar, [Pradosh vrat](/shiv-ji/pradosh-vrat-vidhi-katha), [Maha Shivratri](/shiv-ji/maha-shivratri-puja-vidhi) aur Sawan mein khaas taur par. Roz ki puja mein bhi gayi ja sakti hai.
- **Diya aur thali:** Ghee ka diya ya kapoor jalayein. Thali mein diya, phool aur akshat rakh sakte hain. Thali ko Shiv ji ke saamne seedhe (ghadi ki disha mein) ghumayein.
- **Ghanti aur taal:** Aarti ke saath ghanti ya taali bajane ki parampara hai. Shor itna na ho ki shraddha bhang ho.
- **Aarti ke baad:** Haath se aarti ki lau lekar aankhon par lagayein, phir prasad baantein. Puja ki poori vidhi ke liye [Shiv ji ki puja vidhi](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-puja-vidhi) dekhein.
- **Kitni baar:** Koi tay sankhya nahi hai. Ek baar poore bhaav se gana kaafi maana jaata hai.

## Sources aur variants

Yeh aarti "Om Jai Shiv Omkara" ke naam se prachlit roop mein di gayi hai. Alag-alag chhapi kitabon aur gaayakon mein kuch shabdon ka antar milta hai, jaise antim panktiyon mein "manvanchhit phal paave" ya "sukh sampatti paave", aur kuch sanskarnon mein ek-do aur panktiyan bhi jodi jaati hain. Apne parivar ya mandir mein jo roop chalta hai, wahi gayein. Hamare pandit reviewer is paath ko ek baar aur dekhenge.
