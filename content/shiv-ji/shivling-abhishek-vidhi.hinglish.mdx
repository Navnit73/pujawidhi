Shivling abhishek Shiv puja ka sabse prachlit hissa hai. Abhishek ka arth hai Shivling par shraddha se jal ya anya pavitra dravya arpit karna. Yeh page ghar par karne layak saaf vidhi batata hai: kaunsi cheez kis kram mein chadhayein, bel patra kaise arpit karein aur kin baaton ka dhyan rakhein. Parampara kshetra aur sampraday ke hisaab se badalti hai. Yahan **prachlit roop** diya gaya hai, aur apne kul ki paddhati ho to wahi follow karein.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Taiyari aur sankalp">
    Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Shivling ko thali mein rakhein ya mandir ki jalhari ke saamne baithein. Aachman karke sankalp lein: "Main aaj Shri Mahadev ji ka abhishek shraddha se kar raha/rahi hoon." Sankalp ke baare mein [Puja sankalp kaise lein](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) dekhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Jal abhishek">
    Lote se patli dhaar mein jal chadhayein. Dhaar aaram se aur lagataar ho, aur saath mein "Om Namah Shivaya" bolte rahein. Jal tambe ya peetal ke paatr se chadhana prachlit hai.
    <Mantra dev="ॐ नमः शिवाय" rom="Om Namah Shivaya" arth="Main Shiv ji ko pranaam karta/karti hoon. Yeh panchakshar mantra hai." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Doodh, dahi, ghee, shahad, cheeni (chahein to)">
    Panchamrit se abhishek karna ho to ek-ek dravya alag chadhayein, har ke baad thoda jal chadhayein. Prachlit kram hai: doodh, dahi, ghee, shahad, cheeni. Kuch parampara mein kram alag hota hai. Har dravya ke saath "Om Namah Shivaya" bolein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ant mein saaf jal">
    Sab dravya ke baad Shivling ko saaf jal se achhi tarah snaan karayein, phir saaf kapde se halke haath se poochh dein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chandan aur bhasma">
    Safed chandan lagayein. Bhasma se tripund (teen aadi rekhayein) banayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bel patra aur phool">
    Bel patra arpit karein. Patte teen patti wale, saaf aur bina kate-phate hon. Aam taur par patte ka chikna hissa Shivling ki taraf rakha jaata hai, par is par bhi parampara ka antar hai. Phir safed phool chadhayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dhoop, deep, naivedya aur aarti">
    Dhoop, agarbatti aur diya dikhayein. Phal ya mithai ka bhog lagayein. Phir [Shiv ji ki aarti](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-aarti) gayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aadhi pradakshina aur kshama prarthana">
    Shivling ki pradakshina aadhi ki jaati hai: jalhari (jal nikalne ki nali) ko langhe bina wapas aayein. Ant mein kshama prarthana karein. Mantra ke liye [Shiv ji ki puja vidhi](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-puja-vidhi) dekhein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Tulsi aur ketaki:** Prachlit parampara mein Shivling par tulsi aur ketaki (kewda) nahi chadhayi jaati.
- **Haldi aur kumkum:** Aam taur par Shivling par haldi aur kumkum nahi lagate. Chandan aur bhasma lagayein.
- **Shankh:** Shiv abhishek mein shankh ka istemal aam taur par nahi hota. Kul ki parampara dekhein.
- **Doodh ka istemal:** Doodh kam matra mein aur saaf rakhein. Bada kharch ya barbaadi karna zaroori nahi, shraddha se thoda hi kaafi hai. Abhishek ka jal paudhe mein daal sakte hain, ise paon ke neeche na aane dein.
- **Jalhari ko na langhein:** Aadhi pradakshina karein.
- **Sthapit Shivling:** Ghar mein sthapit Shivling ki nitya puja ke apne niyam hote hain. Pandit ji se pooch lein.
- **Bimar ya asamarth:** Jo sthiti mein na ho, ve saaf jal aur "Om Namah Shivaya" se bhi abhishek kar sakte hain.

## Mahatva aur katha

Shaiv parampara mein Shivling Shiv tattva ka prateek hai. Samudra manthan ki katha mein jab halahal vish nikla, tab Shiv ji ne use kanth mein dharan kiya. Isliye jal aur sheetal dravya chadhane ki parampara ko jodkar dekha jaata hai. Yeh vishwas aur parampara ki baat hai. Bade roop mein abhishek ke liye [Rudrabhishek vidhi aur samagri](/shiv-ji/rudrabhishek-vidhi-samagri) dekhein.

## FAQ

<FAQ>
  <Q q="Shivling par jal chadhane ka sahi samay kaunsa hai?">Aam taur par subah ka samay prachlit hai. Pradosh kaal aur Somvar ko bhi vishesh maana jaata hai. Tyohaar ka muhurat apne shehar ke panchang se dekhein.</Q>
  <Q q="Kya Shivling par doodh chadhana zaroori hai?">Zaroori nahi. Sirf jal aur bel patra se bhi abhishek poora maana jaata hai. Doodh chadhana ho to thodi matra mein shraddha se chadhayein.</Q>
  <Q q="Bel patra kitne chadhane chahiye?">Koi tay sankhya nahi. Aam taur par 3, 5, 11 ya 21. Patte saabut aur teen patti wale hon.</Q>
  <Q q="Kya abhishek ka kram badal sakte hain?">Alag-alag parampara mein kram mein antar hai. Ant mein saaf jal se snaan karana lagbhag sabhi mein hota hai. Apne pandit ji se poochh lein.</Q>
  <Q q="Kya mahilayein Shivling par abhishek kar sakti hain?">Ghar par mahilayein bhi shraddha se abhishek karti hain. Kuch mandiron mein alag niyam ho sakte hain, wahan ke niyam maanein.</Q>
</FAQ>
