Pradosh vrat Shiv ji ko samarpit vrat hai, jo har maas ki Trayodashi tithi par rakha jaata hai, yaani Shukla aur Krishna dono paksha mein, maas mein do baar. Is vrat ki puja **Pradosh kaal** mein hoti hai, jo suryast ke aas-paas ka samay maana jaata hai. Yeh page vidhi, niyam aur prachlit katha saaf bhasha mein batata hai. Tithi aur Pradosh kaal ka sateek samay shehar ke hisaab se badalta hai, isliye apne shehar ka panchang zaroor dekhein. Parampara parivar aur kshetra ke anusaar alag ho sakti hai.

## Vrat vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Subah ka sankalp">
    Subah snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Shiv ji ke saamne haath mein jal, akshat aur phool lekar vrat ka sankalp lein: "Main aaj Pradosh vrat Shiv ji ke charnon mein shraddha se rakh raha/rahi hoon." Din bhar upvaas ya phalahar karein. Vistaar ke liye [Puja sankalp kaise lein](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) dekhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Din bhar">
    Mann mein "Om Namah Shivaya" ka smaran karein. Krodh aur kadve vachan se bachein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pradosh kaal ki taiyari">
    Suryast se kuch pehle dobara snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Puja sthaan saaf karein aur samagri saja lein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shiv puja aur abhishek">
    Pradosh kaal mein Shivling par jal chadhayein. Chahein to doodh, dahi, ghee, shahad se bhi abhishek karein. Bel patra, safed phool arpit karein. Poori vidhi ke liye [Shiv ji ki puja vidhi](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-puja-vidhi) aur [Shivling abhishek vidhi](/shiv-ji/shivling-abhishek-vidhi) dekhein.
    <Mantra dev="ॐ नमः शिवाय" rom="Om Namah Shivaya" arth="Main Shiv ji ko pranaam karta/karti hoon." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Katha">
    Pradosh vrat katha padhein ya sunein (neeche prachlit katha ka saar hai).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aarti">
    [Shiv ji ki aarti](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-aarti) gayein aur prasad baantein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paran">
    Puja ke baad prasad se saatvik bhojan grahan karein. Kuch parivar agle din subah paran karte hain. Apne parivar ka niyam maanein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Tithi ka dhyan:** Trayodashi ki tithi do din par pad sakti hai. Pradosh kaal mein jis din Trayodashi ho, wahi din prachlit roop mein vrat ke liye liya jaata hai. Panchang ya pandit ji se confirm karein.
- **Kshamta ke anusaar vrat:** Bimar, garbhvati ya bujurg log kathin upvaas na karein. Phalahar bhi sweekar hai. Swasthya ka sawaal ho to doctor se poochhein.
- **Tulsi aur ketaki:** Prachlit parampara mein Shivling par tulsi aur ketaki nahi chadhayi jaati.
- **Parampara:** Puja ka samay, kram aur bhojan ke niyam kshetra aur parivar ke hisaab se badalte hain.

## Mahatva aur katha

Pradosh vrat ko Samudra manthan ki katha se bhi jodkar dekha jaata hai: jab halahal vish nikla, Shiv ji ne use kanth mein dharan karke sansar ki raksha ki. Prachlit manyata ke anusaar Trayodashi ka Pradosh kaal Shiv ji ki upasana ke liye vishesh hai. Ek lokpriya katha ke anusaar ek vidhwa brahmani apne putra ke saath bhiksha maangkar jeevan chalati thi aur Pradosh vrat rakhti thi. Ek din unhe ek ghayal rajkumar mila jise unhone apne saath rakha. Vrat aur Shiv ji ki kripa se kathin samay ke baad unke jeevan mein sukh laut aaya, aisa katha mein kaha gaya hai. Katha ke kuch ansh alag-alag pustakon mein alag milte hain. Poori katha ke liye Gita Press jaisi kisi chhapi vrat-katha pustak ka paath karein. Yeh aastha aur parampara ki katha hai.
