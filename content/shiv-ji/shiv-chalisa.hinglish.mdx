## Arth (meaning)

Shiv Chalisa mein aam taur par yeh vishay aate hain:

- Shuru mein Ganesh ji ki vandana aur Shiv ji ka dayalu, santon ka paalan karne wala roop.
- Shiv ji ka swaroop: mastak par chandrama, kanon mein kundal, jataon mein Ganga aur gale mein sarp.
- Shiv ji ki kai prasiddh kathayein, jaise Samudra manthan mein halahal vish ka paan karke sansar ki raksha aur Kailash par Parvati ji ke saath nivas.
- Bhakton par unki kripa aur sharan mein aaye ki raksha.
- Ant mein phalashruti ki panktiyan aur rachnakar ki prarthana.

Har chaupai ka poora arth kisi chhapi tika ya pandit ji se samjhein. Verified paath aane ke baad hum yahan arth bhi jodenge.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

- **Kab:** Somvar, Pradosh, Maha Shivratri aur Sawan mein prachlit hai. Roz bhi padh sakte hain.
- **Kaise:** Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Shiv ji ke saamne diya jalayein. Aasan par baithkar shant man se padhein. Padhne se pehle Ganesh ji ka smaran karein.
- **Kitni baar:** Ek baar padhna kaafi maana jaata hai. Kuch log 11 ya 108 baar paath ka sankalp lete hain. Yeh apni shraddha aur kshamta par nirbhar hai.
- **Aasan aur disha:** Purv ya uttar ki taraf munh karke baithna prachlit hai.
- **Puja ke saath:** Paath ke baad [Shiv ji ki aarti](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-aarti) gayein. Poori puja ke liye [Shiv ji ki puja vidhi](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-puja-vidhi) dekhein.
