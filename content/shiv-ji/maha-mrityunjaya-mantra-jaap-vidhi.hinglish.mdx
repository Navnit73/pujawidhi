Maha Mrityunjaya Mantra Shiv ji ka ek prasiddh vaidik mantra hai. Yeh Rigveda (7.59.12) mein milta hai aur Yajurveda mein bhi aata hai. Parampara mein ise rishi Vasishtha se jodkar dekha jaata hai. Isse "Tryambakam mantra" bhi kehte hain. Neeche mantra Devanagari aur Hinglish mein, arth aur jaap ki vidhi ke saath diya gaya hai. Yeh mantra shaanti, nirbhayta aur swasthya ki prarthana ke roop mein jaapa jaata hai. **Yeh kisi bimari ka ilaaj nahi hai.** Swasthya sambandhi kisi bhi samasya mein doctor se salaah zaroor lein.

## Arth (meaning)

- **Tryambakam:** teen netron wale Shiv ji. Teen netron ko surya, chandra aur agni ke roop mein bhi samjha jaata hai.
- **Yajamahe:** hum upasana (puja) karte hain.
- **Sugandhim pushtivardhanam:** jo sugandh yukt hain aur poshan, vriddhi dene wale hain.
- **Urvarukamiva bandhanat:** jaise urvaruk (kakdi ya kharbuja jaisa phal) pakkar apni bel ke bandhan se chhoot jaata hai.
- **Mrityor mukshiya ma amritat:** hum mrityu ke bhay se mukt hon, amrit tattva (moksh) se nahi.

Mantra ka saar yeh hai: jaise phal sahaj roop se bel se alag ho jaata hai, waise hi mrityu ke bandhan se hum sahajta se mukt hon aur amrit tattva se juden. Mrityunjaya ka shabdik arth hai "mrityu ko jeetne wala". Kuch vyakhya mein is mantra ko "Rudra mantra" ya "Mrit-sanjeevani mantra" bhi kaha jaata hai.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

- **Kab:** Roz subah ya Somvar, Pradosh, Maha Shivratri aur Sawan mein. Kisi shubh sankalp ya anushthan ke roop mein bhi.
- **Kaise:** Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Shiv ji ya Shivling ke saamne diya jalayein aur aasan par shaant man se baithein. Purv ya uttar ki taraf munh karna prachlit hai.
- **Mala:** Rudraksh ki mala se jaap karna prachlit hai. Aam taur par ek mala 108 manke ki hoti hai.
- **Kitni baar:** Roz 11, 21, 51 ya 108 baar jaap prachlit hai. Kuch parampara mein sankalp lekar 1008 baar ya adhik jaap, ya pandit ji dwara purashcharan (sava lakh jaap) karwaya jaata hai. Jaap ki sankhya apni kshamta aur shraddha ke hisaab se rakhein.
- **Uchcharan:** Vaidik mantra hai, isliye uchcharan sahi hona chahiye. Pehle chhapi pustak ya pandit ji se seekh lein. Dheeme aur saaf swar mein jaap karein.
- **Anushthan:** Bade jaap ya havan ke liye yogya pandit ji ka maargdarshan lein. Kharch aur dakshina ke baare mein pehle saaf baat kar lein. Rakam is page par nahi di gayi hai.
- **Sath mein:** Jaap ke baad [Shiv ji ki aarti](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-aarti) gayein. Poori puja ke liye [Shiv ji ki puja vidhi](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-puja-vidhi) dekhein. Saral jaap ke liye [Om Namah Shivaya jaap vidhi](/shiv-ji/om-namah-shivaya-jaap-vidhi) bhi dekh sakte hain.

## Sources aur variants

Mantra ka paath Rigveda (7.59.12) aur Yajurveda mein thode antar ke saath milta hai. Upar diya gaya prachlit paath hai, jo ghar mein jaapne ke liye aam taur par bola jaata hai. Kuch jagah mantra ke aage "Om" ya "Hraum Joom Sah" jaise beej akshar jodkar bhi jaapa jaata hai (jise sampuT kehte hain). Yeh parampara aur pandit ji ke maargdarshan ka vishay hai.
