"Shankar Bhagwan ki aarti" Shiv ji ki aarti ka hi doosra lokpriya naam hai. Shankar, Shambhu, Mahadev, Bholenath aur Shiv ek hi ishwar ke naam maane jaate hain. Jab log Shankar Bhagwan ki aarti dhoondhte hain, to aam taur par unka matlab **"Om Jai Shiv Omkara"** hota hai. Alag-alag kshetron mein Shiv ji ki kuch aur aartiyan bhi gayi jaati hain. Is page par hum bata rahe hain ki kaun si aarti kab gayi jaati hai aur gaane ka tareeka kya hai.

## Arth (meaning)

Shiv ji ki aartiyon mein aam taur par yeh vishay aate hain:

- Shiv ji ka swaroop: jataon mein Ganga, mastak par chandrama, gale mein sarp, haath mein trishul aur damru.
- Kailash par Parvati ji ke saath unka nivas.
- Unka bholapan aur bhakton par jaldi prasann hona.
- Neelkanth roop, jab unhone samudra manthan mein nikla vish apne kanth mein dharan kiya.
- Aarti ke ant mein bhakt ki prarthana.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

- **Kab:** Shiv puja ke ant mein. Somvar, [Pradosh](/shiv-ji/pradosh-vrat-vidhi-katha), [Maha Shivratri](/shiv-ji/maha-shivratri-puja-vidhi) aur Sawan mein khaas taur par.
- **Kaise:** Ghee ka diya ya kapoor jalayein, thali ko Shiv ji ke saamne ghumayein aur ghanti bajayein. Aarti ke baad jal ki chhinte lekar aur lau lekar prasad baantein.
- **Kitni baar:** Koi tay sankhya nahi hai. Ek baar bhaav se gana kaafi maana jaata hai.
- **Puja ki vidhi:** Poori puja ke liye [Shiv ji ki puja vidhi](/shiv-ji/shiv-ji-ki-puja-vidhi) dekhein.
