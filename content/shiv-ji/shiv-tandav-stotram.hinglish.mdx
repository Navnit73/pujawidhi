## Arth (meaning)

Stotra ka saar yeh hai ki isme Shiv ji ke tandav roop ka varnan hai:

- Jataon se behti Ganga ki dhara.
- Gale mein sarp ki mala aur damru ka naad.
- Mastak par chandrama aur trinetra roop.
- Kailash par unka nivas aur Parvati ji ke saath unka sambandh.
- Ant mein Shiv ji ki stuti ka phal aur prarthana.

Har shlok ka shabd-ba-shabd arth kisi chhapi tika ya pandit ji se samjhein. Verified paath aane ke baad hum yahan shlok-wise arth jodenge.
