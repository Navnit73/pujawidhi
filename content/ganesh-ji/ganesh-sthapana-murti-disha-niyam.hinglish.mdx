Ganesh Chaturthi par ghar mein Ganesh ji ki murti ki sthapna karte samay sabse zyada sawal teen cheezon par uthte hain: kaunsi murti, kis disha mein aur kin niyamon ke saath. Is page par in teeno ka **prachlit roop** diya gaya hai. Parivar, kshetra aur pandit ji ke tareeke alag ho sakte hain. Aise mein apne kul ki parampara ko pradhanta dein.

## Poori jaankari

### Murti kaisi chunein

1. **Mitti ki murti:** Ganesh Chaturthi ke liye prachlit parampara mitti ki murti ki hai, kyunki woh visarjan ke baad jal mein ghul jaati hai. Aajkal paryavaran-hit ki drishti se bhi iska chalan badh raha hai.
2. **Size:** Ghar mein sthapna ke liye choti-badi dono murtiyan chalti hain. Dhyan rakhein ki murti ko aap aaram se rakh bhi sakein aur visarjan ke liye le bhi ja sakein.
3. **Sund ki disha:** Is par alag-alag mat sunne ko milte hain. Kuch log baayein taraf mudi sund ko prachlit batate hain, kuch dayein ko. Dono ki parampara hai. Apne parivar ki parampara dekhein ya pandit ji se poochhein.
4. **Visheshta:** Murti mein koi tuti-faati jagah na ho. Saaf, shant mukh wali murti chunein.
5. **Kharidne ka samay:** Murti sthapna se pehle ghar laakar saaf kapde se dhak kar rakhein. Pran pratishtha puja ke samay hi hoti hai.

### Disha aur sthan

- **Jagah:** Saaf, shant aur aisi jagah jahan bheed ya shor kam ho. Puja ghar ya ghar ka uttar-purv (ishan) kona aam taur par prachlit hai.
- **Aadhar:** Murti ko chowki ya saaf aasan par rakhein. Chowki par laal ya peela kapda bichayein. Murti seedhi aur sthir rakhi jaye.
- **Disha ke mat:** Vastu aur parampara mein murti aur puja karne wale ki disha par alag-alag mat milte hain. Hum koi ek niyam nahi thop rahe. Apne pandit ji ya kul parampara se pooch kar tay karein.
- **Jagah ki shuddhta:** Sthapna se pehle jagah ko saaf karein, jal chhidkein aur chowki sthapit karein.

### Sthapna ke aam niyam

- **Shuddhta:** Sthapna aur puja se pehle snaan karein aur saaf kapde pehnein.
- **Pran pratishtha:** Sthapna ke samay murti mein pran pratishtha ka sankalp aur mantra pandit ji se karwane ki parampara hai. Ghar par saral vidhi ke liye [Ganesh Chaturthi puja vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-chaturthi-puja-vidhi) dekhein.
- **Daily seva:** Jitne din murti ghar mein rahe, roz subah-shaam diya, aarti aur bhog ki parampara hai. Chhoti seva bhi shraddha se ho to theek hai.
- **Murti akeli na chhodein:** Parampara mein sthapna ke dino mein ghar mein kisi ka rehna achha maana jaata hai. Bahar jaana pade to parivar ke kisi sadasya ko seva ki zimmedari de dein.
- **Murti ki sankhya:** Parampara mein aam taur par ek hi murti ki sthapna ki jaati hai. Kul ki parampara dekhein.
- **Chandra darshan:** Ganesh Chaturthi par chandrama na dekhne ki ek prachlit manyata hai. Is bare mein apne parivar ya pandit ji se poochhein.
- **Sthapna ki avadhi:** Aam taur par 1.5, 3, 5, 7 ya 10 din ki avadhi prachlit hai. Visarjan ki vidhi ke liye [Ganesh visarjan puja vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-visarjan-puja-vidhi) dekhein.

### Sthapna se pehle ki checklist

1. Murti, chowki aur kapde ka intezam.
2. Samagri ki list ke liye [Ganesh pujan samagri list](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-pujan-samagri-list).
3. Panchang se apne shehar ka muhurat.
4. Visarjan ki yojna: ghar mein, kisi kritrim kund mein ya sthaniya prashasan ki vyavastha mein.
5. Parivar mein sabhi ko seva ke niyam batana.
