Vinayak Chaturthi (jise Vinayaka Chaturthi ya Varad Chaturthi bhi kaha jaata hai) har maas ke Shukla paksha ki Chaturthi tithi par Ganesh ji ko samarpit hoti hai. Kai bhakt is din vrat rakhkar Vinayak ji ki puja karte hain. Isse alag Krishna paksha ki Chaturthi Sankashti Chaturthi kehlati hai ([uski vidhi alag page par](/ganesh-ji/sankashti-chaturthi-vrat-vidhi)). Tithi ka samay har shehar mein alag hota hai, isliye apne panchang se dekhein. Hum koi tarikh nahi likhte. Vrat ke niyam parivar aur kshetra ke hisaab se badalte hain.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Subah ki taiyari">
    Subah snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Puja ki jagah saaf karein aur chowki par Ganesh ji ko virajman karein. Parampara mein laal vastra pehnna achha maana jaata hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sankalp aur vrat">
    Haath mein jal, akshat aur phool lekar vrat aur puja ka sankalp lein. Vrat ho to uski sima khud tay karein: nirjala, phalahar ya ek samay ka saatvik bhojan. Vrat ke niyam parivar aur kshetra ke anusaar alag hain. Sehat sambandhi dikkat ho to bina vrat ke puja karna bhi theek hai. Bade-buzurgon ya pandit ji ki salah lein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aachman aur dhyan">
    Teen baar aachman karein aur dhyan karein.
    <Mantra dev={`शुक्लाम्बरधरं विष्णुं शशिवर्णं चतुर्भुजम् ।
प्रसन्नवदनं ध्यायेत् सर्वविघ्नोपशान्तये ॥`} rom={`Shuklambaradharam Vishnum shashivarnam chaturbhujam
Prasannavadanam dhyayet sarvavighnopashantaye`} arth="Shwet vastra dharan karne wale, chaar bhujaon wale, prasann mukh Ganesh ji ka vighna shanti ke liye dhyan karein." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Avahan aur snaan">
    <Mantra dev="ॐ गं गणपतये नमः" rom="Om Gan Ganapataye namah" arth="Ganapati ji ko namaskar." />
    Mantra bolkar avahan karein, phir jal (chahein to panchamrit) se snaan karayein aur saaf kapde se poochhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Vastra, tilak aur sindoor">
    Vastra ya mauli, janeu aur chandan arpit karein. Sindoor lagayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Durva aur phool">
    21 durva ek-ek karke chadhayein, har baar "Om Gan Ganapataye namah" bolein. Laal phool aur haar chadhayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Naivedya">
    Modak ya laddu ka bhog lagayein. Dhoop aur deep dikhayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Katha aur aarti">
    Vinayak Chaturthi ki vrat katha (apni pothi ya parivar ki parampara se) padhein ya sunein. Phir [Ganesh ji ki aarti](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-ji-ki-aarti) gayein aur prasad baantein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kshama aur vrat paran">
    Kshama prarthana karein.
    <Mantra dev={`आवाहनं न जानामि न जानामि विसर्जनम् ।
पूजां चैव न जानामि क्षमस्व परमेश्वर ॥`} rom={`Aavahanam na jaanami na jaanami visarjanam
Poojam chaiva na jaanami kshamasva Parameshwar`} arth="Main na avahan jaanta hoon, na visarjan, na puja ki vidhi. Mujhse jo bhool hui ho, use kshama karein." />
    Vrat rakha ho to puja ke baad parivar ki parampara ke anusaar saatvik bhojan se paran karein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Tithi ka bhram:** Shukla paksha ki Chaturthi Vinayak Chaturthi hai aur Krishna paksha ki Sankashti. Dono ko alag samjhein.
- **Vrat zabardasti na karein:** Bimar, bujurg, garbhvati ya bachche apni sehat ke hisaab se vrat tay karein. Hum koi medical salah nahi dete.
- **Tulsi:** Prachlit parampara mein Ganesh ji ko tulsi nahi chadhate.
- **Muhurat:** Madhyahna ka samay shehar ke hisaab se badalta hai. Panchang dekhein.
- **Chandra darshan:** Chaturthi ke chandra darshan ke bare mein parampara mein alag-alag manyata milti hain. Parivar ya pandit ji se poochhein.
- **Parampara:** Vrat katha aur niyam kshetra ke hisaab se alag hote hain.

## Mahatva aur katha

Chaturthi tithi Ganesh ji ko priya maani jaati hai. Shukla paksha ki Chaturthi par vrat aur puja karne ki parampara ko bhakt vighna door karne ki prarthana ke roop mein dekhte hain. Bhadrapada maas ki Shukla Chaturthi sabse badi hai aur woh [Ganesh Chaturthi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-chaturthi-puja-vidhi) ke roop mein manayi jaati hai. Har maas ki Vinayak Chaturthi ki alag-alag kathayein pothiyon mein milti hain. Hum yahan koi ek katha nishchit roop se nahi likh rahe. Apni vrat-katha pothi ya pandit ji se sunein.

</FAQ>
