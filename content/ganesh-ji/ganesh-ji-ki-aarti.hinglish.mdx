## Arth (meaning)

- **Jai Ganesh Deva:** Ganesh ji ki jai ho. Unki mata Parvati ji aur pita Mahadev (Shiv ji) hain.
- **Ek dant, chaar bhuja:** Ganesh ji ek dant wale, daya karne wale aur chaar bhujaon wale hain. Unke mastak par sindoor shobhit hai aur vahan moose (chuhe) ki savari hai.
- **Andhan ko aankh det...:** Yeh pankti parampara mein Ganesh ji ki kripa ki mahima batati hai. Ise bhakti ke bhav mein padha jaata hai, kisi cheez ki guarantee ke roop mein nahi.
- **Haar, phool, meva, laddu:** Bhakt Ganesh ji ko haar, phool, meva aur laddu ka bhog arpit karte hain aur sant unki seva karte hain.
- **Deenan ki laaj raakho:** Antim verse mein prarthana hai ki Shambhu (Shiv ji) ke putra, deen-dukhiyon ki laaj rakhein aur hamari kamnayein poori karein.

## Kab aur kaise gayein

- **Kab:** Puja ke ant mein. Ganesh Chaturthi par subah-shaam dono samay. Budhvar ko bhi bahut se log Ganesh ji ki aarti karte hain.
- **Kaise:** Diya jalayein, thali mein phool, akshat aur modak ya laddu rakhein. Dheere aur saaf uchcharan ke saath gayein. Aarti ke dauraan ghanti ya taali bajana prachlit hai.
- **Aarti ke baad:** Thali ki aarti lein, prasad baantein aur Ganesh ji ko pranam karein.
- **Poori puja:** Poori vidhi ke liye [Ganesh puja vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-puja-vidhi) dekhein.
