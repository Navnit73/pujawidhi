### Kuch cheezon par chhoti baat

- **Durva:** Yeh Ganesh ji ki sabse prachlit chadhawa mani jaati hai. Saaf, taazi aur teen ya paanch patti wali durva lein. Chadhane se pehle jal se dho lein.
- **Laal phool:** Gudhal (hibiscus) ko Ganesh ji ko priya maana jaata hai. Dusre laal phool bhi chalte hain. Phool saaf ho, murjhaye hue ya sunghe hue na hon.
- **Modak:** Modak Ganesh ji ka priya bhog maane jaate hain. Ghar mein modak na ban sake to laddu ya koi saaf mithai bhi chadhayi ja sakti hai.
- **Sindoor:** Ganesh ji ko sindoor lagana prachlit hai, vishesh roop se Maharashtra mein. Parivar ki parampara dekhein.
- **Tulsi:** Prachlit parampara mein Ganesh ji ko tulsi nahi chadhate. Iske peeche ki katha alag-alag sthanon par alag batayi jaati hai, isliye hum use ek tathya ke roop mein nahi likh rahe.

### Kharidne aur taiyari ke vyavharik tips

1. Puja se ek din pehle list banakar saman le aayein, taki subah ki bhaag-daud na ho.
2. Durva aur phool taaze lene ke liye subah ka samay achha rehta hai. Phool ko geele kapde mein lapet kar rakhein.
3. Murti kharidte samay uski sthirta aur chhote-bade size ka dhyan rakhein. Murti ke chunaav ke liye [sthapna aur murti ka page](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-sthapana-murti-disha-niyam) padhein.
4. Prasad aur bhog ghar mein taza banana prachlit hai. Bazaar se lein to saaf aur taaza mithai chunein.
5. Samagri ki keemat sthan aur mausam ke hisaab se badalti hai. Isliye is page par koi daam nahi diye gaye hain.
