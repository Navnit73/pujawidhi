## Arth (meaning)

Ganesh ji ke har naam mein unka koi guna ya roop chhupa hai:

- **Vakratund:** Mudi hui sund wale.
- **Ekdant:** Ek dant wale.
- **Lambodar:** Bade udar wale. Bhav yeh hai ki woh sab kuchh apne mein samaa lene wale hain.
- **Vighnaraj, Vighnaharta:** Vighnon ke swami aur unhe door karne wale.
- **Vinayak:** Jinke upar koi neta nahi, yaani param netritva wale.
- **Ganapati, Ganesh:** Ganon (sevak-ganon) ke adhipati.
- **Gajanan:** Hathi ke mukh wale.
- **Bhalachandra:** Mastak par chandrama dharan karne wale.

108 naamon mein se jyadatar isi tarah ke guna, roop aur leelaon par aadharit hain.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

- **Kab:** Subah snaan ke baad, Budhvar ya Chaturthi par. Kisi naye kaam se pehle bhi.
- **Kitni baar:** "Om Gan Ganapataye namah" ko aam taur par 11, 21 ya 108 baar jap sakte hain. 108 ke liye mala ka prayog prachlit hai. Sankhya ki koi zabardasti nahi, apni shraddha aur samay ke hisaab se chunein.
- **Kaise:** Saaf aasan par purv ya uttar ki taraf munh karke baithein. Ganesh ji ke saamne diya jalayein, durva ya laal phool arpit karein. Mala ho to uska 108 vaala dana ginti ke liye lein. Mala ke meru (sabse bada dana) ko laangna nahi chahiye, yeh prachlit niyam hai.
- **Naamavali ke saath:** Agar 108 naam padhein to har naam ke saath "namah" lagakar ek-ek durva ya phool arpit karne ki parampara hai.
- **Puja ke saath:** [Ganesh puja vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-puja-vidhi), [Ganesh Chalisa](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-chalisa) aur [Atharvashirsha](/ganesh-ji/ganpati-atharvashirsha) bhi dekhein.
