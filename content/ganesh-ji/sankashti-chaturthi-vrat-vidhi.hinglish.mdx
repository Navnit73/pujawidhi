Sankashti Chaturthi har maas ke Krishna paksha ki Chaturthi tithi par Ganesh ji ke liye rakha jaane wala vrat hai. Is vrat ki khaas baat yeh hai ki ise **chandrodaya (chandrama ke uday) ke baad** khola jaata hai. Chandrodaya ka samay har shehar mein alag hota hai, isliye hum koi samay nahi likhte. Apne shehar ke panchang ya bharosemand panchang app se dekhein. Jab yeh Chaturthi Mangalvar ko padti hai to ise Angarki Sankashti Chaturthi kaha jaata hai, jise vishesh maana jaata hai. Vrat ke niyam parivar aur kshetra ke hisaab se badalte hain.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Subah ka sankalp">
    Subah snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Ganesh ji ke saamne diya jalakar vrat ka sankalp lein. Sankalp mein apna naam aur vrat ka uddeshya bolein. Vrat ka roop (nirjala, phalahar ya ek samay ka saatvik bhojan) apni sehat aur parivar ki parampara ke hisaab se tay karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Din ka niyam">
    Din bhar vrat ke niyam par rahein. Man shant rakhein, Ganesh ji ka smaran karein aur chaahein to "Om Gan Ganapataye namah" ka jaap karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shaam ki puja ki taiyari">
    Shaam ko chandrodaya se pehle puja ki jagah saaf karein. Chowki par Ganesh ji ko virajman karein. Diya, phool aur bhog taiyar rakhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dhyan aur avahan">
    <Mantra dev={`वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय सूर्यकोटि समप्रभ ।
निर्विघ्नं कुरु मे देव सर्वकार्येषु सर्वदा ॥`} rom={`Vakratund Mahakay Suryakoti Samaprabh
Nirvighnam kuru me Deva sarvakaryeshu sarvada`} arth="He vakratund, vishalkaay, karodon suryon jaise tejasvi Deva, mere sabhi kaaryon mein hamesha vighna door karein." />
    Phir "Om Gan Ganapataye namah" bolkar avahan karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Puja">
    Snaan, vastra, chandan, sindoor, durva, laal phool, dhoop, deep aur naivedya kram se arpit karein. 21 durva ek-ek karke chadhayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Vrat katha">
    Us maas ki Sankashti Chaturthi ki vrat katha apni pothi ya parivar ki parampara se padhein ya sunein. Alag-alag maas ki alag kathayein pothiyon mein milti hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aarti">
    [Ganesh ji ki aarti](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-ji-ki-aarti) gayein aur prasad rakhein. Chahein to [Ganesh chalisa](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-chalisa) ke path ki parampara bhi hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chandrodaya aur arghya">
    Chandrodaya ke samay (panchang se apne shehar ka samay dekhein) chandrama ko jal ka arghya dein. Chandrama ko pranam karein. Phir Ganesh ji ko pranam karke vrat poora karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paran">
    Chandrama ke darshan aur arghya ke baad parivar ki parampara ke anusaar saatvik bhojan karke vrat kholein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Chandrodaya ka samay:** Yeh shehar ke hisaab se alag hota hai. Doosre shehar ka samay na dekhein. Aasman saaf na ho to panchang ka samay hi aadhar mana jaata hai.
- **Vrat zabardasti na karein:** Bimari, garbhavastha, buzurgi ya bachpan mein sehat ke hisaab se vrat tay karein. Hum koi medical salah nahi dete. Sandeh ho to doctor aur parivar ke bade se poochhein.
- **Vrat khulne ka samay:** Parampara mein chandrama ke baad hi vrat kholte hain. Isse pehle vrat tod dena sakht niyam ke khilaf mana jaata hai, par kisi majboori mein parivar ya pandit ji se poochhein.
- **Tulsi:** Prachlit parampara mein Ganesh ji ko tulsi nahi chadhate.
- **Vinayak aur Sankashti:** Krishna paksha ki Chaturthi Sankashti hai aur Shukla paksha ki [Vinayak Chaturthi](/ganesh-ji/vinayak-puja-vidhi). Dono alag hain.
- **Parampara:** Katha, bhog (jaise til ke laddu) aur vrat ke niyam kshetra ke hisaab se alag hote hain.

## Mahatva aur katha

"Sankashti" ka arth hai sankat (pareshani) se mukti dene wali. Is vrat ko bhakt vighna aur pareshaniyon mein Ganesh ji ki kripa maangne ke roop mein rakhte hain. Maharashtra aur dakshin Bharat mein iska vishesh chalan hai. Har maas ki Sankashti Chaturthi ki alag nam wali katha pothiyon mein milti hai. Hum yahan kisi ek katha ko nishchit roop se nahi likh rahe, kyunki alag-alag sangrahon mein kathayein alag hain. Apni vrat-katha pothi ya pandit ji se sunein.
