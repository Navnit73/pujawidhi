Ganesh puja kisi bhi shubh kaam se pehle ki jaati hai, roz ki puja ke roop mein bhi aur Ganesh Chaturthi jaise bade avsar par bhi. Yeh page ghar ke liye ek **saral aur poori vidhi** deta hai. Agar aapke parivar mein koi alag paddhati chalti hai (jaise Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu ya Bengal ke tareeke), to wahi follow karein. Yahan **prachlit roop** diya gaya hai.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Taiyari">
    Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Chowki par kapda bichayein, uspar Ganesh ji ko virajman karein. Puja ke paas ek saaf aasan par baithein. Diya aur dhoop pehle se taiyaar rakhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aachman aur pavitri karan">
    Daayein haath mein thoda jal lekar teen baar aachman karein. Har baar ek naam bolein.
    <Mantra dev={`ॐ केशवाय नमः ।
ॐ नारायणाय नमः ।
ॐ माधवाय नमः ।`} rom={`Om Keshavaya namah
Om Narayanaya namah
Om Madhavaya namah`} arth="Bhagwan Vishnu ke teen naam lekar jal grahan karna shuddhi ka prarambh maana jaata hai." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Diya jalana aur sankalp">
    Diya jalayein. Haath mein jal, akshat aur phool lekar apna naam, gotra (agar pata ho), sthaan aur tithi bolte hue sankalp lein. Saral roop mein: "Main aaj Shri Ganesh ji ki puja shraddha se kar raha/rahi hoon. Meri puja sweekar karein." Phir jal zameen par chhod dein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dhyan aur avahan">
    Haath jodkar Ganesh ji ka dhyan karein.
    <Mantra dev={`वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय सूर्यकोटि समप्रभ ।
निर्विघ्नं कुरु मे देव सर्वकार्येषु सर्वदा ॥`} rom={`Vakratund Mahakay Suryakoti Samaprabh
Nirvighnam kuru me Deva sarvakaryeshu sarvada`} arth="He vakratund, vishalkaay, karodon suryon ke saman tejasvi Deva, mere sabhi kaaryon mein hamesha vighna door karein." />
    Phir avahan karein: <Mantra dev="ॐ गं गणपतये नमः, आवाहयामि" rom="Om Gan Ganapataye namah, aavahayami" arth="Main Ganapati ji ka avahan (aamantran) karta/karti hoon." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aasan, snaan aur abhishek">
    Ganesh ji ko aasan arpit karein, phir jal se snaan karayein. Chahein to panchamrit se abhishek karke ek baar phir saaf jal se snaan karayein, aur kapde se poochhein. Har upchar ke saath "Om Gan Ganapataye namah" bol sakte hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Vastra, janeu, chandan aur sindoor">
    Vastra (ya mauli) aur janeu arpit karein. Chandan aur sindoor lagayein. Ganesh ji ko sindoor priya maana jaata hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Durva aur phool">
    21 durva ki gaanthein ek-ek karke arpit karein, har ek ke saath "Om Gan Ganapataye namah" bolein. Laal phool aur haar chadhayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dhoop, deep aur naivedya">
    Dhoop aur agarbatti dikhayein, phir diya. Modak ya laddu ka bhog lagayein aur thoda jal rakhein. Bhog ke baad thoda jal chhodkar aachman karayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aarti">
    Kapoor ya ghee ki batti se thali ghumate hue [Ganesh ji ki aarti](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-ji-ki-aarti) gayein. Ghanti bajayein. Aarti lekar prasad baantein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pradakshina aur kshama prarthana">
    Jagah par khade hokar teen baar pradakshina karein (ya apni jagah par ghoom jayein). Ant mein kshama prarthana karein.
    <Mantra dev={`आवाहनं न जानामि न जानामि विसर्जनम् ।
पूजां चैव न जानामि क्षमस्व परमेश्वर ॥`} rom={`Aavahanam na jaanami na jaanami visarjanam
Poojam chaiva na jaanami kshamasva Parameshwar`} arth="Main na avahan jaanta hoon, na visarjan, na puja ki vidhi. Mujhse jo bhool hui ho, use kshama karein." />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Tulsi na chadhayein:** Ganesh puja mein tulsi patra chadhane ki parampara nahi hai. Durva aur laal phool prachlit hain.
- **Disha:** Murti aur puja karne waale ki disha ke baare mein alag-alag parampara hain. Apne pandit ji ya kul parampara se poochhein.
- **Shuddhta:** Snaan karke, saaf kapde pehenkar puja shuru karein. Puja ke samay mobile silent rakhein.
- **Samagri ki fikr:** Agar koi samagri na mile to shraddha se jal, akshat aur phool se bhi puja poori maani jaati hai.
- **Bhog:** Taza aur saaf bhog lagayein. Bhog ke baad use prasad ke roop mein baant dein.

## Mahatva aur katha

Ganesh ji ko "Vighnaharta" aur "Pratham Pujya" kaha jaata hai, yaani jinki puja sabse pehle hoti hai. Isiliye naya kaam, griha pravesh, vivah ya yatra jaise avsaron par pehle Ganesh puja ki jaati hai. Ganesh Chaturthi par ghar mein sthapna aur visarjan ki parampara hai. Uska vistar [Ganesh Chaturthi puja vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-chaturthi-puja-vidhi) page par aayega. Yeh page vishwas aur parampara ke baare mein jaankari ke liye hai.
