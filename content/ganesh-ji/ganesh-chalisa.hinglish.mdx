## Arth (meaning)

Ganesh Chalisa ki rachna parampara se Hindi ki awadhi-mishrit bhasha mein hui hai. Is par abhi pankti-dar arth nahi diya gaya, kyunki wo poore path ke saath hi sahi tarah diya ja sakta hai. Chalisa ki aam sanrachna is tarah batayi jaati hai:

- **Aarambh:** Ganesh ji ka naman aur Ganapati, Ganaraj jaise naamon se stuti.
- **Roop-varnan:** Gajanan roop, ek dant, bade udar, moose ki savari, sindoor aur modak ki priyata ka varnan.
- **Mahima:** Ganesh ji ke Parvati-putra hone aur pratham pujya hone ki baat, tatha vighna door karne ki prarthana.
- **Phalshruti:** Ant mein aam taur par chalisa ke path ki mahima aur samarpan ki prarthana aati hai. Phalshruti bhakti ka bhav hai, isse kisi parinaam ki guarantee nahi samajhni chahiye.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

- **Kab:** Budhvar Ganesh ji ka priya din maana jaata hai. Chaturthi tithi par bhi path ki parampara hai. Roz subah puja ke baad bhi padh sakte hain.
- **Kitni baar:** Aam taur par ek baar. Koi vishesh sankalp ho to 11 ya 21 baar ki prachlit parampara hai, par yeh parivar ya pandit ji ki salah par chhodein. Koi zabardasti ki sankhya nahi hai.
- **Kaise:** Snaan ke baad saaf aasan par purv ya uttar ki taraf baithein. Ganesh ji ke saamne diya jalayein, durva ya laal phool arpit karein, phir shant man se path karein. Ant mein [Ganesh ji ki aarti](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-ji-ki-aarti) karein.
- **Puja ke saath:** Poori puja ki vidhi ke liye [Ganesh puja vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-puja-vidhi) dekhein.
