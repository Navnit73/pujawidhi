**"Sukhkarta Dukhharta"** Maharashtra ki sabse jaani-maani Ganpati aarti hai. Ise 17vin sadi ke sant Samarth Ramdas ki rachna maana jaata hai. Ganeshotsav ke dauraan ghar aur pandal, dono jagah yeh subah-shaam gayi jaati hai. Pure Uttar Bharat mein jo "Jai Ganesh Deva" wali aarti gayi jaati hai, woh alag hai: [Ganesh ji ki aarti](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-ji-ki-aarti).

Yeh aarti Marathi mein hai. Neeche **pehla pad (pehla antara)** diya gaya hai, jo bahut prachlit hai. Aage ke pad aur uske baad aane wali "Ghalin Lotangan" aur "Yei O Vitthale" jaisi aartiyan parivar aur kshetra ke hisaab se badalti hain, isliye unhe chhapi hui pothi se padhein.

## Arth (meaning)

- **Sukhkarta, dukhharta:** Ganesh ji ko bhakt sukh dene wale aur dukh door karne wale roop mein pukarte hain.
- **Varta vighnachi:** "Vighna ki varta" yaani vighna ki chinta. Bhav yeh hai ki unki kripa se vighna ki chinta nahi rehti.
- **Uti shendurachi:** "Uti" ka arth lep hai. Ganesh ji ko sindoor lagana Maharashtra ki parampara mein bahut prachlit hai.
- **Mukta-phal ki mala:** Moti ki mala, jo murti ke shringar mein dikhayi jaati hai.
- **Jai Dev jai Dev jai Mangalmurti:** Yeh ant ka tek (dhruvpad) hai, jo har pad ke baad dohraya jaata hai.

## Kab aur kaise gayein

- **Kab:** Ganeshotsav mein subah aur shaam. Aam dinon mein bhi Ganesh puja ke ant mein gayi ja sakti hai.
- **Kaise:** Diya ya kapoor jalakar thali ghumayein. Taal ke saath, dhime aur saaf uchcharan mein gayein. Ghanti, taali ya jhanj ka prayog prachlit hai.
- **Uchcharan:** Marathi shabdon ka uchcharan na aata ho to kisi jaankar se ek-do baar sunkar seekh lein. Bhav mein shraddha hi mukhya hai.
- **Poori puja:** Aarti puja ke ant mein aati hai. Vidhi ke liye [Ganesh puja vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-puja-vidhi) dekhein.
