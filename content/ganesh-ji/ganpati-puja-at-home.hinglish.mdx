## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Taiyari aur shuddhta">
    Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Puja ki jagah saaf karke chowki par kapda bichayein aur Ganesh ji ko virajman karein. Diya, phool aur bhog saamne rakh lein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aachman">
    Daayein haath mein jal lekar teen baar aachman karein.
    <Mantra dev={`ॐ केशवाय नमः ।
ॐ नारायणाय नमः ।
ॐ माधवाय नमः ।`} rom={`Om Keshavaya namah
Om Narayanaya namah
Om Madhavaya namah`} arth="Bhagwan Vishnu ke teen naam lekar jal grahan karna shuddhi ka prarambh maana jaata hai." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dhyan">
    Haath jodkar Ganesh ji ka dhyan karein.
    <Mantra dev={`शुक्लाम्बरधरं विष्णुं शशिवर्णं चतुर्भुजम् ।
प्रसन्नवदनं ध्यायेत् सर्वविघ्नोपशान्तये ॥`} rom={`Shuklambaradharam Vishnum shashivarnam chaturbhujam
Prasannavadanam dhyayet sarvavighnopashantaye`} arth="Shwet vastra dharan karne wale, chaar bhujaon wale, prasann mukh Ganesh ji ka vighna shanti ke liye dhyan karein." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Diya aur sankalp">
    Diya jalayein. Haath mein jal, akshat aur phool lekar apna naam, sthaan aur puja ka uddeshya bolein. Saral roop mein: "Main Shri Ganesh ji ki puja shraddha se kar raha/rahi hoon, meri puja sweekar karein." Jal zameen par chhod dein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Avahan aur snaan">
    <Mantra dev="ॐ गं गणपतये नमः" rom="Om Gan Ganapataye namah" arth="Ganapati ji ko namaskar." />
    Yeh mantra bolkar Ganesh ji ka avahan karein. Phir thode jal se snaan karayein aur saaf kapde se poochhein. Tasveer ho to jal ke chheente de dein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tilak, vastra aur sindoor">
    Chandan ya roli ka tilak lagayein. Vastra ya mauli arpit karein. Ganesh ji ko sindoor lagana prachlit hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Durva aur phool">
    Durva ki gaanthein ek-ek karke arpit karein aur har baar "Om Gan Ganapataye namah" bolein. Laal phool aur haar chadhayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dhoop, deep aur bhog">
    Dhoop aur agarbatti dikhayein. Modak ya laddu ka bhog lagayein aur saath mein jal rakhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aarti">
    [Ganesh ji ki aarti](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-ji-ki-aarti) gayein. Ghanti bajayein, thali ghumayein aur aarti lein. Maharashtra ki parampara mein [Sukhkarta Dukhharta](/ganesh-ji/ganpati-aarti-sukhkarta-dukhharta) bhi gayi jaati hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pradakshina aur kshama">
    Apni jagah par teen baar ghoom kar pradakshina karein. Phir kshama prarthana karein.
    <Mantra dev={`आवाहनं न जानामि न जानामि विसर्जनम् ।
पूजां चैव न जानामि क्षमस्व परमेश्वर ॥`} rom={`Aavahanam na jaanami na jaanami visarjanam
Poojam chaiva na jaanami kshamasva Parameshwar`} arth="Main na avahan jaanta hoon, na visarjan, na puja ki vidhi. Mujhse jo bhool hui ho, use kshama karein." />
    Prasad baant dein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Jaldbaazi na karein:** Chhoti puja bhi shant man se karein. Mobile silent rakhein.
- **Tulsi:** Prachlit parampara mein Ganesh ji ko tulsi nahi chadhate. Durva aur laal phool chadhayein.
- **Samagri ki fikr:** Kuch na ho to jal, akshat aur phool se bhi shraddha se puja poori maani jaati hai.
- **Bhog:** Taaza aur saaf bhog lagayein, aur baad mein prasad ke roop mein baant dein.
- **Shabd ki shuddhta:** Mantra ka uchcharan dhyan se karein. Na aaye to "Om Gan Ganapataye namah" jaise saral mantra se hi puja karein.
- **Parampara:** Alag kul mein alag tareeke hote hain. Pandit ji ya bade-buzurgon ki salah lein.

## Mahatva aur katha

Ganesh ji ko Vighnaharta aur Pratham Pujya kaha jaata hai. Isiliye koi bhi naya kaam shuru karne se pehle unhe yaad karne ki parampara hai. Ghar par ki gayi saral puja bhi isi bhav se ki jaati hai: man mein shraddha, kaam mein sahayata ki prarthana.   <Q q="Kya mantra yaad hona zaroori hai?">Nahi. "Om Gan Ganapataye namah" saral mantra hai. Baki mantra dekhkar bhi padh sakte hain.</Q>
  <Q q="Ganpati ko kya chadhayein?">Durva, laal phool, sindoor, modak ya laddu. Tulsi nahi chadhayi jaati.</Q>
  <Q q="Murti ho to tasveer ki puja chalegi?">Haan. Tasveer ya chhoti murti, dono se puja shraddha se ki ja sakti hai.</Q>
</FAQ>
