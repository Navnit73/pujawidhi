Ganpati Atharvashirsha ek Upanishad-sharini stotra hai, jo Atharvaveda se jodi jaati hai aur Ganapati Upanishad ke naam se bhi jaani jaati hai. Maharashtra mein Ganeshotsav ke dauraan yeh saamuhik roop se padha jaata hai. Yeh page abhi **guide roop** mein hai. Sanskrit ke is path mein ek akshar ka antar bhi uchcharan badal sakta hai, isliye hum reviewer ke proofing se pehle poora path nahi chhap rahe. Poore path ke liye Gita Press ya kisi pramanik Sanskrit pothi se padhein.

## Arth (meaning)

Atharvashirsha mein Ganapati ko sirf ek devta nahi, balki param tattva (Brahm) ke roop mein dekha gaya hai. Mote taur par is path ke bhaag aise batate jaate hain:

- **Namaskar aur tattva-stuti:** Ganapati ko pratyaksh Brahm kehkar naman, jo upar ki panktiyon mein dikhta hai.
- **Rakshan ki prarthana:** Path karne waale ki raksha ki prarthana.
- **Swaroop-chintan:** Ganapati ka swaroop, unki mahima aur ve sab tattvon mein vyapt hain, yeh bhav.
- **Mantra aur dhyan:** Ganapati ka beej mantra aur dhyan shlok.
- **Phalshruti:** Ant mein path ki mahima. Yeh shraddha ka bhav hai, kisi parinaam ki guarantee nahi.

Pankti-dar arth poore path ke saath hi sahi tarah diya ja sakta hai, isliye abhi nahi diya gaya.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

- **Kab:** Subah snaan ke baad. Ganesh Chaturthi aur Ganeshotsav mein iska vishesh chalan hai. Budhvar aur Sankashti Chaturthi par bhi log path karte hain.
- **Kitni baar:** Aam taur par ek baar. Sankalp ke saath 11 ya 21 avritti ya samuhik path (Maharashtra mein jise "sahasravartan" bhi kaha jaata hai) ki parampara hai, par yeh sthaniya parampara aur pandit ji ki salah par nirbhar hai.
- **Kaise:** Saaf aasan par purv ya uttar ki taraf baithein. Ganesh ji ke saamne diya jalayein. Uchcharan par dhyan dein, kyunki yeh Vedic stotra hai. Seekhne ke liye kisi jaankar se ek baar sunna aur sudharwana achha rehta hai.
- **Niyam:** Shuddhta aur shant man mukhya hai. Agar uchcharan mein hichak ho to pehle chhote hisse seekhein, ya [Ganesh ji ke 108 naam aur mantra](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-108-naam-mantra) jaise saral jaap se shuruaat karein.
- **Puja ke saath:** Poori puja ke liye [Ganesh puja vidhi](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-puja-vidhi) dekhein.
