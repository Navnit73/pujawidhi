## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Shuddhta">
    Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Puja ki jagah par jhaadu-poncha karke jal ke chheente dein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Diya aur agarbatti">
    Ganesh ji ke saamne diya aur agarbatti jalayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dhyan">
    Haath jodkar shant man se dhyan karein.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Arpan">
    Durva ya phool arpit karein. Jo bhi ho, gud ya mithai ka bhog lagayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Jaap">
    <Mantra dev="ॐ गं गणपतये नमः" rom="Om Gan Ganapataye namah" arth="Ganapati ji ko namaskar." />

  </Step>
  <Step title="Aarti ya pranam">
    Samay ho to [Ganesh ji ki aarti](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-ji-ki-aarti) gayein. Na ho to teen baar pranam karke din ka sankalp lein.
  </Step>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Roz ki niyamitta:** Lambi puja kabhi-kabhi karne se behtar hai ki chhoti puja roz ho.
- **Samay ki dikkat:** Subah samay na ho to shaam ko diya jalakar do minute ke liye bhi bhakti ki ja sakti hai.
- **Tulsi:** Prachlit parampara mein Ganesh ji ko tulsi nahi chadhate.
- **Bhog:** Na ho to sirf jal ya gud bhi chalta hai. Shraddha sabse zaroori maani jaati hai.
- **Parampara:** Apne parivar ki parampara aur pandit ji ki salah ko pradhanta dein.

## Mahatva aur katha

Ganesh ji ko Vighnaharta kaha jaata hai, aur roz unka naam lekar din shuru karne ki parampara bahut purani hai. Bhav yeh hai ki din ke sab kaam shubh aur nirvighna ho. Is parampara ko ek achhi aadat ki tarah bhi dekha ja sakta hai. Parivar ke saath ghar par puja ke liye [ghar par Ganpati puja](/ganesh-ji/ganpati-puja-at-home) bhi dekhein.
