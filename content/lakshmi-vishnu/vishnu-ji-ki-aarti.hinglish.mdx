"Om Jai Jagdish Hare" Bhagwan Vishnu (Jagdish) ki sabse zyada gayi jaane waali aarti hai. Satyanarayan katha, Ekadashi, Guruvar aur Diwali ki puja ke ant mein yeh aksar poore parivar ke saath gayi jaati hai. Neeche poori aarti Devanagari aur Hinglish mein hai. Har stanza ke baad refrain "Om jai Jagdish Hare" dohraya jaata hai.

## Arth (meaning)

Is aarti ka bhaav poorn sharanagati hai. Pehle stanza mein Bhagwan ko sankat harne waale ke roop mein yaad kiya gaya hai. Beech ke stanzaon mein unhe mata-pita, antaryami, karuna ke sagar aur deen-bandhu kehkar bhakt apni asamarthta swikar karta hai. Antim stanza mein vishay-vikar mitane aur shraddha-bhakti badhane ki prarthana hai. Yani aarti sirf sukh-sampatti maangne ki nahi, man ko shuddh karne ki prarthana bhi hai.

## Kab aur kaise gayein

- **Kab:** Satyanarayan puja, Ekadashi, Guruvar aur Diwali ki puja ke ant mein. Roz ki subah-shaam ki puja mein bhi gayi jaati hai.
- **Kaise:** Pehle diya jalayein, phir ghee ki batti ya kapoor se thali ko Bhagwan ke saamne ghumate hue aarti karein. Ghanti bajana prachlit hai. Aarti ke baad aarti lekar prasad baantein. Tulsi dal arpit karna Vishnu puja mein prachlit hai.
- **Kitni baar:** Koi tay sankhya nahi. Ek baar poori aarti kaafi hai.
- **Aage padhein:** Poori puja ke liye [Satyanarayan puja vidhi aur katha](/lakshmi-vishnu/satyanarayan-puja-vidhi-katha) aur [Ekadashi vrat vidhi](/lakshmi-vishnu/ekadashi-vrat-vidhi-calendar) dekhein.
