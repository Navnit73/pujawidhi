Guruvar (Brihaspativar) ka vrat Bhagwan Vishnu aur Devguru Brihaspati ko samarpit maana jaata hai. Bahut se parivar yeh vrat vidya, vivah, parivar ki sukh-shanti ya kisi manokamna ke sankalp se rakhte hain. Kai log ise lagataar 16 Guruvar ya kuch tay Guruvar tak rakhte hain. ## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Subah ki taiyari">
    Subah snaan karke saaf, aur ho sake to peele kapde pehnein. Puja-sthan saaf karke chowki par peela kapda bichayein aur Vishnu ji ko virajman karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sankalp">
    Haath mein jal, akshat aur phool lekar apne vrat ka sankalp lein, jaise kitne Guruvar rakhna chahte hain. Sankalp aap apni shraddha aur kshamta ke anusaar lein.
    <Mantra dev={`ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥`} rom={`Om namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya`} arth="Bhagwan Vasudev (Vishnu) ko pranam. Vishnu puja ka prachlit mantra." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Puja">
    Vishnu ji ko peele phool, chandan, haldi, chane ki dal aur gud arpit karein. Brihaspati dev ka smaran karein. Kai parivar kele ke ped ke paas jaakar jal aur dal arpit karte hain, aur ped ki puja karte hain. Dhoop-diya dikhayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Katha">
    Guruvar vrat katha padhein ya sunein. Katha ke kai roop hain, isliye chhapi hui pustak (jaise Gita Press) se padhna uttam hai. Poori katha ka text yahan nahi diya gaya hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aarti">
    [Vishnu ji ki aarti](/lakshmi-vishnu/vishnu-ji-ki-aarti) (Om Jai Jagdish Hare) gayein. Chahein to [Vishnu Chalisa](/lakshmi-vishnu/vishnu-chalisa) bhi padhein. Prasad baantein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Din ka vrat aur shaam">
    Din bhar vrat ke niyam apni kshamta ke anusaar nibhayein. Shaam ko ya parampara ke anusaar ek samay saatvik bhojan karein. Bhojan mein peele padarth, jaise chane ki dal, prachlit hain.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Parivar ka niyam:** Kuch parivar is din baal dhona, naakhun kaatna ya shave karna avoid karte hain. Yeh niyam har ghar mein alag hota hai aur kisi kadi shart ki tarah nahi lena chahiye. Apne parivar ki riti poochein.
- **Vrat ka roop:** Namak, anaaj aur bhojan ke niyam kshetra ke anusaar badalte hain. Swasthya ke hisaab se doctor aur buzurgon se poochkar tay karein.
- **Sankalp nibhana:** Jitne Guruvar ka sankalp liya ho, utne poore karne ka prayas karein. Beech mein chhoot jaye to apne pandit ya buzurg se poochein.
- **Dikhawa nahi:** Shraddha mukhya hai. Mehngi samagri zaroori nahi hai.
- **Yeh site koi phal ki guarantee nahi deti.** Vrat bhakti ke liye hai.

## Mahatva aur katha

Parampara mein Guruvar Vishnu aur Brihaspati dev ka din maana jaata hai. Brihaspati ko devtaon ke guru aur gyan ka karak maana jaata hai. Isliye is din vidya, gyan aur parivar ki shanti ki kamna se vrat rakhne ki riti hai. Guruvar ki vrat katha ke alag-alag roop hain, jinme aksar shraddha se vrat karne, daan aur sanyam ki seekh milti hai. Hum yahan katha ka ek hi roop nishchit nahi bata rahe. Poori katha ke liye chhapi hui pustak dekhein.
