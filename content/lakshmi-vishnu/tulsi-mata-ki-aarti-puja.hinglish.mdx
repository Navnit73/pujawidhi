## Arth (meaning)

Aarti mein Tulsi ko jagat ki sukhdata, vardata aur Vishnu ki priya ke roop mein smaran kiya jaata hai. Parampara mein Tulsi ko Lakshmi ka roop aur Vrinda ka swaroop bhi maana jaata hai, aur Vishnu tatha Shaligram ki puja Tulsi dal ke bina adhoori maani jaati hai. Yeh sab shraddha ki baatein hain, aur alag-alag kshetron mein inki vyakhya thodi alag hoti hai.

Prachlit tareeka yeh hai. Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Tulsi ke gamle ya vrindavan ke paas jaakar thoda jal arpit karein, phir roli ya haldi ka tilak lagayein aur akshat chadhayein. Diya jalayein, aarti karein aur parikrama karein. Shaam ko Tulsi ke paas ghee ya til ke tel ka diya jalana bahut ghar mein ki jaane waali parampara hai.

## Tulsi se jude niyam

- Tulsi ke patte aam taur par subah snaan ke baad, saaf haath se aur tod-ne se pehle prarthana karke tode jaate hain. Naakhun se na todkar ungliyon se todna prachlit hai.
- Bahut se parivar **Ekadashi, Ravivar, Sankranti, Amavasya aur Purnima** ko patte nahi todte. Jarurat ho to ek din pehle tod kar rakh lein. Yeh niyam parivar aur kshetra ke anusaar alag hota hai, isliye apne bade-buzurgon se poochein.
- Tulsi dal Vishnu aur Shaligram ko arpit hota hai. Ganesh ji ki puja mein Tulsi ka prayog parampara mein varjit maana jaata hai.
- Sukhe huye Tulsi ke paudhe ko pavitra jagah, jaise bahte jal ya paudhon ki mitti mein, vidhi se visarjit karein. Kooda mein na phenkein.
- Dev Uthani Ekadashi par Tulsi ka vivah hota hai. Uski poori vidhi [yahan](/lakshmi-vishnu/dev-uthani-ekadashi-tulsi-vivah) hai.

## Kab aur kaise gayein

Tulsi aarti aam taur par roz ki Tulsi puja ke ant mein, Kartik maas mein, aur Tulsi Vivah ke din gayi jaati hai. Diya jalakar, ghanti bajate hue, aur Tulsi ke saamne khade hokar ya baithkar aarti karein. Aarti ke baad Tulsi ko pranam karein. Aarti ke baad [Vishnu ji ki aarti](/lakshmi-vishnu/vishnu-ji-ki-aarti) bhi gayi ja sakti hai.
