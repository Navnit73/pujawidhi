Ekadashi har chandra maas ki gyarahvin tithi ko aati hai. Har maas mein do Ekadashi hoti hain, ek Shukla paksh (badhte chandrama) mein aur ek Krishna paksh (ghatte chandrama) mein. Yeh tithi Bhagwan Vishnu ko samarpit maani jaati hai aur isme vrat rakhne ki lambi parampara hai. Is page mein Ekadashi vrat ki saral vidhi, aam niyam, parana ki jaankari aur 24 Ekadashi ke prachlit naam diye gaye hain. **Kisi bhi saal ki Ekadashi ki tarikh aur parana ka samay is page par nahi diya gaya hai.** Woh aapke shehar ke panchang se dekhein, kyunki tithi ka samay sthaan ke anusaar badalta hai.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Dashami ki taiyari">
    Dashami (Ekadashi se ek din pehle) ko saatvik aur halka bhojan karein. Bahut se parivar Dashami ki shaam ke baad anaaj nahi lete. Mann mein vrat ka sankalp karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ekadashi ki subah">
    Brahma muhurat ya subah jaldi uthkar snaan karein. Saaf kapde pehnein. Vishnu ji ke saamne diya jalayein aur sankalp lein.
    <Mantra dev={`ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥`} rom={`Om namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya`} arth="Bhagwan Vasudev (Vishnu) ko pranam. Vishnu puja ka prachlit mantra, jise vrat ke din jap ke roop mein bhi padha jaata hai." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Vishnu puja">
    Vishnu ji ko jal, peele phool, chandan, Tulsi dal aur dhoop-diya arpit karein. [Vishnu Chalisa](/lakshmi-vishnu/vishnu-chalisa) ya Vishnu ke naam ka jap karein. Ekadashi ki katha sunna ya padhna bhi prachlit hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Din ka vrat">
    Din bhar vrat ke niyam apni kshamta ke anusaar nibhayein. Bahut se log anaaj nahi khate aur falahar karte hain. Kuch log nirjala vrat rakhte hain. Jo log bimaar hain, bujurg hain, bachche hain ya garbhvati hain, woh apne doctor aur buzurgon se poochkar hi vrat ka roop tay karein. Vrat kisi ko kasht dene ke liye nahi hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shaam ki aarti">
    Shaam ko diya jalakar [Vishnu ji ki aarti](/lakshmi-vishnu/vishnu-ji-ki-aarti) gayein. Bahut se bhakt raat ko bhajan-kirtan karke jaagran bhi karte hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Parana (Dwadashi)">
    Agle din Dwadashi ko snaan aur puja ke baad, panchang mein bataye gaye parana ke samay ke andar vrat kholein. Parana ke liye aam taur par pehle jal ya Tulsi jal lete hain aur phir saatvik bhojan karte hain. Dwadashi tithi ke samapt hone se pehle parana karne ki baat kahi jaati hai. Samay panchang mein dekhein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Tarikh ki bhool:** Kabhi-kabhi Ekadashi do din padti hai. Bahut se Smarta parivar pehle din aur Vaishnav sampraday doosre din vrat karte hain. Apne parivar ya guru ki parampara dekhein.
- **Parana ka samay:** Parana ka samay panchang ke anusaar hota hai. Samay chhodkar der se ya jaldi vrat na kholein.
- **Anna ka niyam:** Parampara mein Ekadashi ko chawal, gehun, dal jaise anaaj nahi liye jaate. Falahar ka roop parivar ke anusaar badalta hai.
- **Zabardasti na karein:** Swasthya ke kaaran vrat mushkil ho to doctor aur buzurgon se poochkar hi karein. Zaroori hai bhakti, kasht nahi.
- **Tulsi:** Ekadashi ko Tulsi ke patte todne se bahut parivar bachte hain. Ek din pehle tod kar rakh lein. [Tulsi puja ke niyam](/lakshmi-vishnu/tulsi-mata-ki-aarti-puja) alag page par hain.

## Mahatva aur katha

Parampara mein Ekadashi ko Vishnu ki priya tithi maana gaya hai. Alag-alag Ekadashi ke naam aur unki alag-alag kathayein purano mein aur lokparampara mein milti hain. Yahan 24 Ekadashi ke **prachlit naam** (Purnimanta maas ke anusaar) diye gaye hain. Maas ka kram kshetra ke anusaar amant ya purnimant ho sakta hai, aur kuch naam alag-alag bhi bole jaate hain.

- **Chaitra:** Papmochani (Krishna), Kamada (Shukla)
- **Vaishakh:** Varuthini (Krishna), Mohini (Shukla)
- **Jyeshtha:** Apara (Krishna), Nirjala (Shukla)
- **Ashadha:** Yogini (Krishna), Devshayani (Shukla)
- **Shravan:** Kamika (Krishna), Putrada (Shukla)
- **Bhadrapad:** Aja (Krishna), Parivartini (Shukla)
- **Ashwin:** Indira (Krishna), Papankusha (Shukla)
- **Kartik:** Rama (Krishna), Devutthani (Shukla)
- **Margashirsha:** Utpanna (Krishna), Mokshada (Shukla)
- **Paush:** Saphala (Krishna), Putrada (Shukla)
- **Magha:** Shattila (Krishna), Jaya (Shukla)
- **Phalguna:** Vijaya (Krishna), Amalaki (Shukla)

Adhik maas aane par do aur Ekadashi (aam taur par Padmini aur Parama naam se) jodi jaati hain. Inme Devshayani Ekadashi se Vishnu ke yognidra mein jaane ki aur Devutthani se jaagne ki manyata hai. Uski vidhi [Dev Uthani Ekadashi aur Tulsi Vivah](/lakshmi-vishnu/dev-uthani-ekadashi-tulsi-vivah) page par hai.

## FAQ

<FAQ>
  <Q q="Ekadashi vrat mein kya kha sakte hain?">Parampara mein anaaj (chawal, gehun, dal) nahi liya jaata. Falahar mein phal, doodh, sabudana, singhada ya kuttu ka aata, aalu aur sendha namak aam taur par liye jaate hain. Yeh parivar aur kshetra ke anusaar alag hota hai, isliye apne buzurgon ya pandit se poochein.</Q>
  <Q q="Ekadashi ki tarikh aur parana ka samay kahan dekhein?">Apne shehar ke panchang mein. Sthaan ke anusaar samay badalta hai, isliye kisi aur shehar ka samay na lein. Is page par hum tarikh ya samay nahi de rahe.</Q>
  <Q q="Kya Ekadashi par chawal na khana zaroori hai?">Vrat rakhne waale ke liye yeh parampara hai. Jo vrat nahi rakhte unke liye bhi kai ghar mein is din chawal na banane ki riti hai. Yeh parivar par nirbhar hai.</Q>
  <Q q="Agar Ekadashi vrat mushkil ho to?">Swasthya ya umar ki wajah se mushkil ho to doctor aur parivar ke buzurgon se poochkar halka falahar le sakte hain, ya sirf puja aur Vishnu naam-jap karein. Vrat bhakti ke liye hai, kasht dene ke liye nahi.</Q>
  <Q q="Smarta aur Vaishnav Ekadashi mein kya antar hai?">Kabhi-kabhi tithi ke samay ke karan Ekadashi do din padti hai. Smarta parampara aur Vaishnav sampraday ke niyam alag hone se vrat ka din alag ho sakta hai. Apne parivar ya guru ki parampara se poochein.</Q>
  <Q q="Ekadashi par Vishnu ke saath kiski puja karein?">Vishnu ji ki puja mukhya hai. Kai ghar Lakshmi ji aur Tulsi ki bhi puja karte hain. [Lakshmi ji ki aarti](/lakshmi-vishnu/lakshmi-ji-ki-aarti) bhi saath gayi ja sakti hai.</Q>
</FAQ>
