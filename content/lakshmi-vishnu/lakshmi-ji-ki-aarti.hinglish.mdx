"Om Jai Lakshmi Mata" Maa Lakshmi ki sabse prachlit aarti hai. Diwali ki Lakshmi-Ganesh puja, Shukravar ki puja aur roz ki puja ke ant mein ise gaya jaata hai. Neeche poori aarti Devanagari aur Hinglish mein arth ke saath hai. Har stanza ke ant mein "Om jai Lakshmi Mata" ka refrain dohraya jaata hai.

## Arth (meaning)

Aarti Lakshmi ji ko Vishnu priya, Uma-Rama-Brahmani ke roop mein jagat ki mata aur sukh-sampatti ki data ke roop mein yaad karti hai. "Ratn chaturdash" ka sandarbh samudra-manthan se hai, jisse Lakshmi ji ka prakat hona maana jaata hai. Beech ke stanzaon mein kaha gaya hai ki jahan Lakshmi ka nivas hota hai wahan sadgun aur sukh-vaibhav rehta hai. Dhyan rahe ki parampara mein Lakshmi sirf dhan nahi, shubh, sadgun aur sanyam ki bhi pratik hain.

## Kab aur kaise gayein

- **Kab:** Diwali ki [Lakshmi-Ganesh puja](/lakshmi-vishnu/lakshmi-ganesh-puja-vidhi-diwali) ke ant mein. Shukravar ko bhi bahut se parivar Lakshmi ji ki puja aur aarti karte hain. Roz ki sandhya puja mein bhi gayi jaati hai.
- **Kaise:** Diya jalayein, ghee ki batti ya kapoor se thali ko ghumate hue aarti karein, ghanti bajayein. Aarti ke baad prasad baantein. Diwali par aam taur par Ganesh ji ki aarti pehle aur Lakshmi ji ki aarti uske baad gayi jaati hai. [Ganesh ji ki aarti yahan hai](/ganesh-ji/ganesh-ji-ki-aarti).
- **Kitni baar:** Koi tay sankhya nahi. Ek baar poori aarti kaafi hai.
