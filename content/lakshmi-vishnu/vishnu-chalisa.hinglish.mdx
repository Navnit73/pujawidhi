## Arth (meaning)

Vishnu Chalisa ki sanrachna aam taur par chalisa-parampara jaisi hoti hai: shuru mein ek ya do doha, phir chaupaiyan, aur ant mein phalashruti jaisa doha. Bhaav ki drishti se ise teen hisson mein samjha ja sakta hai.

- **Aarambh:** Bhakt Vishnu ji se vinay karta hai aur unki kripa maangta hai.
- **Madhya:** Vishnu ke roop, gun aur leelaon ka smaran. Isme Shesh-shaiyya, Lakshmi ji ke saath unka sthan, Garud vahan, Sudarshan chakra aur panchajanya jaise prateekon ki charcha prachlit hai. Dashavatar aur Vishnu ke bhakton ki kathaon ka sandarbh bhi alag-alag sanskaranon mein milta hai.
- **Samapan:** Paath karne wale ke liye shraddha, sanyam aur Hari-bhakti ki kamna.

Chalisa mein sansarik sukh ki prarthana bhi hoti hai, par us ko "guarantee" ki tarah nahi padhna chahiye. Yeh paath bhakti aur man ki shanti ke liye hai.

Phir dhyan se, dhire aur shuddh uchcharan ke saath Chalisa padhein. Ant mein [Vishnu ji ki aarti](/lakshmi-vishnu/vishnu-ji-ki-aarti) gayein.
