Satyanarayan puja Bhagwan Vishnu ke "Satya" swaroop ki puja hai, jo mukhya roop se kisi shubh avsar par, manokamna poori hone ke dhanyavad mein, grih-pravesh, vivah ya janmadin jaise mauke par ki jaati hai. Bahut se parivar ise Purnima ko, ya har maas ek baar karte hain. Katha ko aam taur par Skand Puran ke Revakhand se jodkar bataya jaata hai. Katha ke paanch adhyay hote hain. Yeh page ghar ke liye ek saral vidhi aur katha ka saar deta hai. Parivar aur kshetra ke anusaar vidhi badalti hai, isliye yahan **prachlit roop** diya gaya hai.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Taiyari">
    Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Ghar ke puja-sthan ko saaf karke chowki par peela kapda bichayein. Kele ke patte ya phool se chhota mandap sajayein. Sooji ya aate ka halwa (prasad) pehle se taiyar karein, jisme Tulsi dal daalna prachlit hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sankalp aur Ganesh puja">
    Haath mein jal, akshat aur phool lekar sankalp lein ki aap Satyanarayan bhagwan ki puja shraddha se kar rahe hain. Phir Ganesh ji ki puja karein, kyunki har shubh karya ke aarambh mein unki puja hoti hai.
    <Mantra dev={`ॐ गणेशाय नमः ॥`} rom={`Om Ganeshaya namah`} arth="Ganesh ji ko pranam. Puja ke aarambh ka sabse saral mantra." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kalash aur Vishnu ji ka avahan">
    Kalash sthapit karein aur Satyanarayan ji ko chowki par virajman karein. Dhyan karte hue unka avahan karein.
    <Mantra dev={`ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥`} rom={`Om namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya`} arth="Bhagwan Vasudev (Vishnu) ko pranam. Vishnu puja ka prachlit dwadashakshar mantra." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Snaan aur arpan">
    Panchamrit se aur phir saaf jal se snaan karayein. Vastra, mauli, chandan, roli, peele phool aur Tulsi dal arpit karein. Dhoop aur diya dikhayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Katha">
    Ab Satyanarayan katha padhein ya sunein. Katha paanch adhyay mein hoti hai. Parivar ke sab sadasya baithkar sunein. Katha kisi chhapi hui pustak (jaise Gita Press) se padhna uttam hai, kyunki katha ke kai sanskaran milte hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aarti aur prasad">
    Katha ke baad [Vishnu ji ki aarti](/lakshmi-vishnu/vishnu-ji-ki-aarti) (Om Jai Jagdish Hare) gayein. Phir prasad aur charnamrit baantein. Parivar aur padosiyon ko bulakar prasad grahan karna prachlit hai.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- Prasad ki matra aur samagri ke baare mein koi kadi sankhya nahi hai, jo bhi aap shraddha se bana sakein, arpit karein.
- Katha ko beech mein na chhodein. Agar kuch der ke liye uthna pade to katha ke baad prasad zaroor lein.
- Prasad ko arpit kiye bina grahan na karein.
- Puja ke din parivar mein sab saaf kapde pehnein aur man shaant rakhein.
- Dakshina aur samagri ki matra apni kshamta ke anusaar rakhein. Sankhya ya raashi ke baare mein koi tay niyam yahan nahi hai.

## Mahatva aur katha

Satyanarayan katha ka mool vichar yeh hai ki satya aur vishwas ke saath Bhagwan ki puja karni chahiye, aur kiya hua sankalp nibhana chahiye. Katha ke adhyaayon mein alag-alag patron ke jeevan ke prasang aate hain, jinme kisi ne sankalp lekar puja nahi ki ya bhool gaya, to use kasht hua, aur phir shraddha se puja karne par sab theek hua. Is tarah katha sankalp nibhane aur satya ke paalan ki seekh deti hai. Katha ke vistaar aur patron ke naam alag-alag sanskaranon mein bhinn hain, isliye puri katha ke liye chhapi hui pustak dekhein. Hum yahan poori katha ka text nahi de rahe.
