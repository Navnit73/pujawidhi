## Arth (meaning)

Shri Suktam mein Agni (Jatavedas) se prarthana ki jaati hai ki woh Lakshmi ko bulayein, aur phir alag-alag mantron mein Lakshmi ke swaroop ka varnan hota hai: swarn-varna, kamal par virajman, hathi ki dhwani se swagat paane waali, tej aur kirti se yukt, aur dari-dray tatha alakshmi ko door karne ki prarthana. Sukt ke sabhi mantron ka ek-ek shabd ka arth sanskrit ke vidwanon ke anusaar thoda alag ho sakta hai, isliye tippani ke saath padhna uttam hai. Shri ki kamna yahan sirf dhan nahi, sampoorn samriddhi, shobha aur shubh gunon ke roop mein maani jaati hai.

Snaan karke saaf kapde pehnein. Lakshmi ji ki tasveer ya murti ke saamne ghee ka diya jalayein, kamal ya laal-gulaabi phool arpit karein aur sankalp lein. Phir Sukt ka paath dhire aur spasht uchcharan se karein. Paath ke ant mein [Lakshmi ji ki aarti](/lakshmi-vishnu/lakshmi-ji-ki-aarti) gayein. Diwali par Sukt ka paath [Lakshmi-Ganesh puja](/lakshmi-vishnu/lakshmi-ganesh-puja-vidhi-diwali) mein kiya ja sakta hai, aur kai ghar usme Shri Sukt se abhishek bhi karte hain. Yeh sab prachlit roop hai, aapke parivar mein alag ho sakta hai.

Sukt ke ant mein phalashruti-style mantra aate hain, jinme paath ke phal ka varnan hai. Unhe bhakti ki kamna ke roop mein padhein, kisi guarantee ki tarah nahi.
