Dev Uthani Ekadashi (Prabodhini Ekadashi) Kartik maas ke Shukla paksh ki Ekadashi ko aati hai. Parampara mein maana jaata hai ki Bhagwan Vishnu Ashadha Shukla Ekadashi (Devshayani) se chaturmas tak yognidra mein rehte hain aur is din jaagte hain. Is din se vivah aadi shubh karya phir se shuru hone ki manyata hai. Bahut se parivar isi din ya iske aas-paas Tulsi Vivah karte hain, jisme Tulsi ka vivah Shaligram ya Vishnu ji ke saath hota hai. Yeh page ghar par Tulsi Vivah ki saral vidhi deta hai. Parivar aur kshetra ke anusaar vidhi alag hoti hai, isliye yahan **prachlit roop** diya gaya hai.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Dev Uthani ki subah">
    Brahma muhurat ya subah jaldi uthkar snaan karein. Vrat rakhna ho to sankalp lein. Ghar ke puja-sthan par Vishnu ji ki puja karein. Bahut se ghar mein aangan mein chowk bana kar Vishnu ji ke charan chinh (pairon ke nishan) banaye jaate hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Vishnu ko jagana">
    Shaam ko Vishnu ji ko jagane ki parampara hai. Thali ya soop bajakar jagane ke geet gaaye jaate hain. Yeh sab kshetra ke anusaar alag hota hai. Ek prachlit shlok isse jodkar padha jaata hai.
    <Mantra dev={`उत्तिष्ठ गोविन्द त्यज निद्रां जगत्पते ।
त्वया चोत्थीयमानेन उत्थितं भुवनत्रयम् ॥`} rom={`Uttishtha Govinda tyaja nidram jagatpate
Tvaya chotthiyamanena utthitam bhuvanatrayam`} arth="He Govind, jagat ke pati, nidra tyaag kar uthiye. Aapke uthne se teeno lok utth jaate hain. Yeh Dev Uthani par Vishnu ko jagane ka prachlit shlok hai." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tulsi ka mandap sajana">
    Tulsi ke gamle ko saaf karke geru ya rangoli se sajayein. Charon taraf ganne ka mandap banayein aur upar chunri odhayein. Tulsi ko dulhan ki tarah sajayein: chunri, bindi, sindoor aur phool. Gamle mein Shaligram ya Vishnu ji ki murti rakhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sankalp aur puja">
    Haath mein jal, akshat aur phool lekar sankalp lein ki aap Tulsi Vivah shraddha se kar rahe hain. Pehle Ganesh ji ka smaran karein. Phir Tulsi aur Shaligram ko roli-haldi-chandan, phool aur Tulsi dal se poojein. Tulsi ke saath [Tulsi puja ke niyam](/lakshmi-vishnu/tulsi-mata-ki-aarti-puja) yaad rakhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Vivah ki rasmein">
    Parampara ke anusaar Tulsi aur Shaligram ke beech haldi, mehendi, phere ya mangal geet jaise rasm kiye jaate hain. Shaligram ko dulha maankar Tulsi ke paas laya jaata hai. Kai ghar mein pandit ji vivah ke mantra padhte hain. Jo rasmein aapke parivar mein hoti hain, wahi karein. Yeh pratha ghar-ghar badalti hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bhog aur aarti">
    Ganna, singhada, phal aur mithai ka bhog lagayein. Phir [Tulsi aarti](/lakshmi-vishnu/tulsi-mata-ki-aarti-puja) aur [Vishnu ji ki aarti](/lakshmi-vishnu/vishnu-ji-ki-aarti) gayein. Prasad sabme baantein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Tarikh aur muhurat:** Dev Uthani ki tarikh aur Tulsi Vivah ka muhurat panchang se dekhein. Hum yahan koi tarikh nahi de rahe.
- **Tulsi ke patte:** Ekadashi ko Tulsi ke patte todne se bahut parivar bachte hain, isliye ek din pehle tod kar rakh lein.
- **Shaligram:** Shaligram ki puja ka vishesh niyam hota hai. Ghar mein na ho to Vishnu ji ki chhoti murti ya tasveer ka prayog kar sakte hain.
- **Vrat aur sehat:** Vrat rakhna swechha ki baat hai. Swasthya theek na ho to doctor aur buzurgon se poochein.
- **Dikhawa nahi:** Sajawat aur dawat se zyada zaroori shraddha aur saf-safai hai.

## Mahatva aur katha

Dev Uthani Ekadashi ko chaturmas ke ant ka din maana jaata hai. Is din se shubh karya shuru hone ki manyata hai, isliye isse vivah mausam ka aarambh bhi kaha jaata hai. Tulsi Vivah ke peeche ek prachlit katha Vrinda aur Jalandhar ki hai, jisme Tulsi ko Vrinda ka roop aur Shaligram ko Vishnu ka roop maana jaata hai. Katha ke roop kshetra aur granth ke anusaar alag hain, isliye hum ek hi roop ko nishchit nahi bata rahe. Tulsi Vivah ka sandesh shraddha aur Tulsi ke prati samman ka hai.
