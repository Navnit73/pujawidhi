Govardhan Puja Diwali ke agle din (prachlit roop mein Kartik Shukla Paksha pratipada) manayi jaati hai. Is din Krishna ke Govardhan parvat uthane ki katha ko yaad karke Govardhan ki puja, gau-puja aur **Annakut** (anaaj aur pakwano ka vishal bhog) ki parampara hai. Yeh page ghar ke liye saral vidhi deta hai. Mathura-Vrindavan, Nathdwara aur anya kshetron ki apni vishesh paramparaen hain, aur aapke parivar ki vidhi alag ho sakti hai.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Snan aur sankalp">
    Subah snan karke saaf kapde pahnein. Hath mein jal, akshat aur phool lekar sankalp lein: "Main Govardhan puja aur Annakut shraddha se kar raha/rahi hoon." Sankalp ki saral vidhi [yahan](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) dekh sakte hain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Govardhan ki aakriti banayein">
    Aangan ya saaf jagah par gobar se Govardhan parvat ki aakriti banayein. Aksar manushya jaisi leta hui aakriti banayi jaati hai jiske saath gaay, gwaale aur ped bhi saja diye jaate hain. Chhote roop mein ek gol dher bhi chalta hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Puja">
    Aakriti par roli, akshat aur phool chadhayein. Deep aur dhoop dikhayein. Govardhan ka dhyan karte hue yeh bol sakte hain.
    <Mantra dev={`गोवर्धन धराधार गोकुलत्राणकारक ।
विष्णुबाहुकृतोच्छ्राय गवां कोटिप्रदो भव ॥`} rom={`Govardhan dharadhar Gokultranakarak
Vishnu-bahu-kritochchhraya gavam koti-prado bhav`} arth="Prachlit shlok. Anuvaad ka bhaav: he Govardhan, dharti ke aadhar aur Gokul ki raksha karne waale, Vishnu ki bhuja se uthaye gaye, gaayon ki vriddhi karein. Is shlok ke path ke roop sources mein thode alag mil sakte hain." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Annakut ka bhog">
    Tarah-tarah ke pakwan aur anaaj ek bade pyale ya thali mein ya alag-alag paatron mein saja kar Krishna aur Govardhan ko arpit karein. Bahut se mandir mein 56 prakar (chhappan bhog) ki parampara hai, par ghar mein jitna shraddha se ban sake utna kaafi hai. Bhog mein tulsi dal rakhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gau-puja">
    Gaay ko tilak lagakar, gud-roti ya chara khilayein aur pradakshina karein. Gaay na ho to gaushala mein daan ya sthaniya gau-seva ka vikalp hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aarti aur pradakshina">
    [Aarti Kunj Bihari Ki](/krishna-radha/krishna-ji-ki-aarti-kunj-bihari) gayein. Govardhan ki pradakshina bhi karein. Phir Annakut ka prasad parivar aur padosiyon mein baantein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Tithi ka bharam:** Diwali ke baad ka din kabhi-kabhi amavasya ya pratipada ke tithi-sandhi ke karan alag date par padta hai. Panchang se dekhein.
- **Bhog ki shudhdata:** Taza, saaf aur satvik bhojan banayein. Parivar ka sahyog lein; akele sab karne ka dabav na lein.
- **Anna ka sammaan:** Annakut ka bhaav anaaj ka sammaan aur baant kar khana hai, isliye prasad zarur baantein, bhog ko bekaar na jaane dein.
- **Gobar ki suvidha:** Shehar mein gobar na mile to mitti ya aate se chhoti aakriti bana sakte hain.

## Mahatva aur katha

Bhagavat Puran mein katha aati hai ki Gokul ke log Indra ki puja karte the. Krishna ne unhe Govardhan parvat ki puja karne ko kaha, kyunki wahi unki gaayon ko ghaas aur gokul ko jeevan deta tha. Kruddh hokar Indra ne bhayankar varsha ki. Tab Krishna ne Govardhan parvat ko apni chhoti ungli par uthakar saare Gokul-vasiyon aur gaayon ko sharan di. Parampara ke anusaar yeh kai din (saat din) chala. Ant mein Indra ka abhiman toota. Yeh katha prakriti, gaay aur parvat ke prati kritagyata aur abhiman ke tyaag ka sandesh deti hai. Annakut mein isi sandesh ke saath anaaj ka bhog saja kar baanta jaata hai.

Diwali ki poori shrinkhala ke liye [Lakshmi-Ganesh puja Diwali par](/lakshmi-vishnu/lakshmi-ganesh-puja-vidhi-diwali) dekh sakte hain.

## FAQ

<FAQ>
  <Q q="Govardhan puja kab hoti hai?">Prachlit roop mein Diwali ke agle din, Kartik Shukla pratipada ko. Date ke liye apne shehar ka panchang dekhein, kyunki kabhi-kabhi ek din ka antar aa jaata hai.</Q>
  <Q q="Annakut mein kitne vyanjan banane chahiye?">Koi ek sakht ginti sabhi ke liye nahi hai. Mandiron mein bahut vyanjan (jaise 56 bhog) hote hain. Ghar mein shraddha se jitna ban sake, utna hi kaafi hai.</Q>
  <Q q="Gobar na mile to kya karein?">Mitti, aate ya haldi-roli se Govardhan ka pratik bana kar puja kar sakte hain. Bhaav hi mukhya hai.</Q>
  <Q q="Kya Govardhan puja aur Annakut ek hi hai?">Dono saath hote hain. Govardhan puja parvat ki puja hai aur Annakut anaaj ke bade bhog ki parampara hai.</Q>
  <Q q="Kya yeh puja sirf Mathura-Vrindavan mein hoti hai?">Nahi. Poore Bharat mein manayi jaati hai, par Mathura, Vrindavan aur Nathdwara mein iske vishesh utsav hote hain.</Q>
</FAQ>
