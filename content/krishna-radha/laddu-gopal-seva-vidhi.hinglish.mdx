Laddu Gopal Bhagwan Krishna ke baal swaroop hain. Bahut se parivaar ghar mein Laddu Gopal ko ghar ke chhote bachche ki tarah maankar unki roz seva karte hain: subah jagana, snan, shringar, bhog, aarti aur raat ko shayan. Yeh page seva ka ek **saral aur prachlit** kram batata hai. Alag sampraday (Pushtimarg, Gaudiya, Nimbarka aadi) aur alag parivaron ke niyam alag ho sakte hain. Agar aapke guru ya kul ki koi vidhi hai, to wahi sarvopari hai.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Subah jagana">
    Sabse pehle apna snan karein aur saaf kapde pahnein. Phir dheere se Laddu Gopal ko jagayein. Pyaar se "uthiye Gopal ji" kehkar bhajan ya naam le sakte hain. Bahut se ghar mein yeh kaam taare ya sooraj ke nikalne ke aas-paas kiya jaata hai; samay ki koi sakht seema zaroori nahi.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Snan">
    Saaf jal se snan karayein (kabhi-kabhi panchamrit se bhi). Mulayam kapde se ponchhein. Mantra ke roop mein "Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya" ya "Shri Krishna Govind Hare Murare" ka uchcharan kar sakte hain.
    <Mantra dev={`ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय`} rom={`Om namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya`} arth="Bhagwan Vasudev (Krishna) ko namaskar." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shringar">
    Poshak pahnayein, chandan ya tilak lagayein, mukut aur bansuri se sajayein aur phool-mala arpit karein. Roz naya shringar karna zaroori nahi; saaf aur prem se kiya gaya shringar hi mukhya hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bhog">
    Taza, satvik bhog lagayein jisme tulsi dal ho. Parampara mein subah (makhan-mishri ya doodh), dopahar aur shaam ko alag bhog lagane ka kram milta hai. Aap jitni baar nibha sakein, utni baar lagayein; ek baar bhi pyaar se lagaya bhog swikaar hai.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aarti">
    Diya jalakar [Aarti Kunj Bihari Ki](/krishna-radha/krishna-ji-ki-aarti-kunj-bihari) gayein. Ghanti bajayein, thali ghumayein aur prasad baantein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shayan">
    Raat ko bhog aur aarti ke baad Laddu Gopal ko halke kapde se dhak kar shayan karayein. Bahut se ghar mein unke liye chhota bistar ya palna hota hai.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Kathin niyam ka dabav:** Kuch log sochte hain ki seva mein ek bhi chook se dosh lagta hai. Aisa bhay parampara ka bhaav nahi hai. Apni kshamata ke hisaab se saral niyam lein aur unhe nibhayein.
- **Satvik bhog:** Parampara mein Krishna ke bhog ke liye pyaaz-lehsun rahit satvik bhojan prachlit hai.
- **Yatra ya bahar jaana:** Bahar jaate samay kuch ghar Laddu Gopal ko saath le jaate hain, kuch kisi vishwast parivarjan ko seva saunp dete hain. Aapki parampara jo kahe, wahi karein.
- **Murti ka sthan:** Saaf, shaant jagah par virajman karein. Disha ke baare mein [ghar ke mandir ki disha](/puja-basics/ghar-ke-mandir-disha-vastu) page dekhein.
- **Dikhawa nahi, bhaav:** Mehngi poshak ya shringar zaroori nahi.

## Mahatva aur katha

Bal Krishna ki leelaen (makhan chori, Yashoda ka vatsalya, gokul ki gopiyan) Bhagavat Puran aur lokparampara mein varnit hain. Laddu Gopal seva mein bhakt Krishna ko bhagwan ke saath-saath apne ghar ka laadla bachcha maanta hai. Is bhav ko vatsalya bhakti kaha jaata hai. Utsav ke din, jaise [Janmashtami](/krishna-radha/janmashtami-puja-vidhi), Radha Ashtami aur Govardhan puja par seva vishesh roop se ki jaati hai. Kuch sampraday seva ke liye diksha aur vishesh niyam bhi batate hain; uske liye apne guru se poochhein.
