Radha Ashtami Shri Radha rani ka janmotsav hai. Parampara ke anusaar yeh Bhadrapad maas ki Shukla Paksha ashtami ko manayi jaati hai, yaani Krishna Janmashtami ke lagbhag paandrah din baad. Barsana, Vrindavan aur Mathura kshetra mein ise bade utsav ki tarah manaya jaata hai. Ghar mein bhi bahut se bhakt vrat aur puja karte hain. Yahan **prachlit roop** ki saral vidhi di gayi hai; aapke sampraday ya parivar ki vidhi alag ho to wahi apnayein.

## Vidhi step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Subah ki taiyari aur sankalp">
    Snan karke saaf kapde pahnein. Vrat rakhne waale sankalp lein: "Main Radha Ashtami ka vrat aur puja shraddha se kar raha/rahi hoon." Sankalp ke tareeke ke liye [Puja sankalp kaise lein](/puja-basics/puja-sankalp-kaise-lein) dekhein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sthan sajayein">
    Chauki par kapda bichakar Radha-Krishna ko virajman karein. Phool aur diya se sthan sajayein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aachman aur dhyan">
    Teen baar aachman karein, diya jalayein aur yugal roop ka dhyan karein. Radha ji ke dhyan ke liye prachlit Radha Gayatri ka paath bhi kiya jaata hai.
    <Mantra dev={`ॐ वृषभानुजायै विद्महे कृष्णप्रियायै धीमहि ।
तन्नो राधा प्रचोदयात् ॥`} rom={`Om Vrishabhanujayai vidmahe Krishnapriyayai dhimahi
Tanno Radha prachodayat`} arth="Hum Vrishabhanu ki putri Radha ko jaanne ka prayas karte hain aur Krishna ki priya Radha ka dhyan karte hain. Radha hamari buddhi ko prerit karein. Yeh Radha Gayatri ka prachlit roop hai; paath ke roop alag sources mein thode alag mil sakte hain." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Abhishek aur shringar">
    Murti ko pehle jal se, phir panchamrit se aur phir saaf jal se snan karayein. Naye vastra pahnayein, chandan lagayein aur phool-mala arpit karein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Puja aur bhog">
    Dhoop, deep dikhayein. Makhan-mishri, fal aur mithai ka bhog lagayein jisme tulsi dal ho. Bhakt "Shri Radhayai namah" ya "Radhe Radhe" ka naam-jaap bhi karte hain.
    <Mantra dev={`ॐ श्री राधायै नमः`} rom={`Om Shri Radhayai namah`} arth="Shri Radha ji ko namaskar." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aarti">
    [Aarti Kunj Bihari Ki](/krishna-radha/krishna-ji-ki-aarti-kunj-bihari) mein Radhika ka bhi smaran hai, ise gaakar aarti karein. Prasad baantein.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paran">
    Vrat rakhne waale parivar ki parampara ke anusaar shaam ko puja ke baad ya agle din vrat kholte hain. Samay ke liye apne pandit ji ya bujurg se poochhein.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Aam galtiyan aur niyam

- **Date ki jaanch:** Ashtami tithi ka samay shehar ke hisaab se badalta hai. Panchang se dekhein, sirf kisi message par bharosa na karein.
- **Vrat ke niyam:** Alag kshetra mein nirjala, falahar ya ek samay bhojan ki parampara hoti hai. Sehat ko dhyan mein rakhte hue apne bujurgon se poochhein. Vrat anivarya shart nahi hai.
- **Tulsi aur satvikta:** Bhog saaf, taza aur satvik ho.
- **Bhaav:** Samagri kam ho to jal, phool aur naam-smaran se bhi shraddha se ki gayi puja poori maani jaati hai.

## Mahatva aur katha

Radha ji ko Krishna ki param priya aur bhakti ka swaroop maana jaata hai. Brij kshetra ki parampara mein Radha ka janm Barsana (Vrishabhanu ji ke yahan) mana jaata hai. Alag-alag granthon aur sampradayon mein Radha ke janm aur swaroop ke varnan mein antar milta hai, isliye hum yahan kisi ek kahani ko hi antim satya ke roop mein nahi rakhte. Barsana mein is din mela aur utsav hota hai. Krishna janm ke utsav ke liye [Janmashtami puja vidhi](/krishna-radha/janmashtami-puja-vidhi) bhi dekh sakte hain.
