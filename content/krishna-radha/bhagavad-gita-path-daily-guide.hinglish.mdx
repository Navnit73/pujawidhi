Shrimad Bhagavad Gita Mahabharat ke Bhishma Parva ka hissa maani jaati hai. Isme Kurukshetra ke yuddh ke arambh mein Shri Krishna aur Arjun ka samvad hai. Gita mein **18 adhyay** aur parampara ke anusaar lagbhag **700 shlok** hain. Yeh page ek saral **18 din ki yojana** deta hai jisme roz ek adhyay padha ja sake. Gita ke shlokon ka poora text aur Hindi anuvaad kisi **chhapi hui pustak (jaise Gita Press ka sanskaran)** se padhein. Site par verified paath tab joda jayega jab pandit ji proofing kar lenge.

## Arth (meaning): 18 adhyay ka saar

Adhyayon ke naam alag sanskaranon mein thode alag likhe milte hain. Yahan prachlit naam aur ek-line saar diya gaya hai.

- **Adhyay 1, Arjun Vishad Yog:** Yuddh ke maidan mein Arjun ka moh aur vishad.
- **Adhyay 2, Sankhya Yog:** Atma ki amarta aur sthitapragya ka varnan.
- **Adhyay 3, Karm Yog:** Nishkam karm ka upadesh.
- **Adhyay 4, Gyan Karm Sanyas Yog:** Gyan aur karm ka sambandh.
- **Adhyay 5, Karm Sanyas Yog:** Karm-sanyas aur karm-yog ki tulna.
- **Adhyay 6, Dhyan Yog (Atmasanyam Yog):** Dhyan aur mann ko sadhne ki vidhi.
- **Adhyay 7, Gyan-Vigyan Yog:** Bhagwan ke swaroop ka gyan.
- **Adhyay 8, Akshar Brahm Yog:** Antkal ka smaran aur param gati.
- **Adhyay 9, Raj-Vidya Raj-Guhya Yog:** Param gopniya gyan aur bhakti.
- **Adhyay 10, Vibhuti Yog:** Bhagwan ki vibhutiyon ka varnan.
- **Adhyay 11, Vishwaroop Darshan Yog:** Arjun ko vishwaroop ka darshan.
- **Adhyay 12, Bhakti Yog:** Bhakti ka marg aur bhakt ke gun.
- **Adhyay 13, Kshetra-Kshetragya Vibhag Yog:** Sharir (kshetra) aur atma (kshetragya) ka antar.
- **Adhyay 14, Gunatraya Vibhag Yog:** Satva, raj, tam gun.
- **Adhyay 15, Purushottam Yog:** Sansar-vriksh aur Purushottam ka swaroop.
- **Adhyay 16, Daivasur Sampad Vibhag Yog:** Daivi aur asuri pravrittiyan.
- **Adhyay 17, Shraddhatraya Vibhag Yog:** Teen prakar ki shraddha, aahar aur tap.
- **Adhyay 18, Moksha Sanyas Yog:** Upadesh ka nichod aur sharanagati.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

- **18 din ki yojana:** Roz ek adhyay padhein. Pehle din Adhyay 1, 18vein din Adhyay 18. Yeh ek saral aur prachlit tareeka hai. Kuch log ek adhyay ek hafte mein bhi padhte hain; apni suvidha dekhein.
- **Kab:** Subah snan ke baad ya shaam ko shaant samay mein. Koi ek samay anivarya nahi hai.
- **Kaise:** Saaf aasan par baithein, Krishna ji ya Gita ke saamne diya jalayein. Sankalp lekar paath shuru karein. Pehle shlok ya dhyan-shlok se aarambh karna prachlit hai.
- **Samajhna zaroori:** Sirf padhna nahi, Hindi anuvaad ke saath samajh kar padhna Gita ki parampara mein zyada mahatvapurn maana jaata hai.
- **Sampuran ke baad:** 18 din poore hone par Krishna ji ko bhog lagakar [Aarti Kunj Bihari Ki](/krishna-radha/krishna-ji-ki-aarti-kunj-bihari) karein.
- **Mool ya anuvaad:** Sanskrit ka uchcharan na aata ho to pehle Hindi anuvaad padhein aur dheere-dheere Sanskrit shlok seekhein.
