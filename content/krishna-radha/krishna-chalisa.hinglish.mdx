## Poora paath

Chalisa ki parampara mein shuruaat do doheon (mangalacharan) se hoti hai, phir chaalis chaupaiyan aati hain aur ant mein samapan doha. Krishna Chalisa ke prachlit roop mein bhi yahi dhancha milta hai. Paath ka kram aur shabd aapki pothi ke anusaar hone chahiye. Apni pothi ko hi praman maanein.

Yeh page paath ki jagah **paath ko samajhne** mein madad karta hai, aur jab tak verified text yahan nahi aata, aap printed pustak se hi padhein. Doosri aartiyon ke liye [Aarti Kunj Bihari Ki](/krishna-radha/krishna-ji-ki-aarti-kunj-bihari) page par poora paath maujood hai.

## Arth (meaning)

Chalisa mein aam taur par yeh vishay aate hain (alag sanskaranon mein krama alag ho sakta hai):

- **Mangalacharan:** Krishna ke roop ka varnan: bansuri, mor-mukut, peetambar aur shyam varn.
- **Bal leela:** Janm, Yashoda-Nand ka vatsalya, makhan chori aur gokul ki leelaen.
- **Vrindavan aur gopiyan:** Raas leela, Radha ka prem aur bansuri ki dhun.
- **Raksha ki kathaen:** Govardhan uthana, Kaliya naag ka daman, Putana jaise asuron ka vadh. In prasangon ke liye [Govardhan puja page](/krishna-radha/govardhan-puja-annakut-vidhi) bhi dekh sakte hain.
- **Mathura aur Dwarka:** Kans vadh aur Dwarka ke raja ke roop mein Krishna.
- **Phalshruti (ant):** Padhne waale ke liye prarthana aur shraddha ka bhaav.

Pratyek chaupai ka line-by-line arth tab hi joda jayega jab verified paath site par aayega, taki arth aur paath mein mel rahe.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

- **Kab:** Subah snan ke baad ya shaam ko puja ke samay. Janmashtami, Radha Ashtami aur guruvar ko bahut se log padhte hain. Sahi tithi ke liye panchang dekhein.
- **Kitni baar:** Koi tay sankhya sabhi ke liye nahi hai. Ek baar poora paath kaafi hai. Kuch log sankalp lekar 11 ya 21 din ya vishesh avsar par zyada baar padhte hain; yeh vyaktigat parampara hai.
- **Aasan aur sthan:** Saaf aasan par shaant sthan par baithein, murti ya tasveer ke saamne diya jalayein. Mukh aam taur par poorv ya uttar ki taraf rakhna prachlit hai, par apni parampara dekhein.
- **Kram:** Pehle aachman aur sankalp, phir Krishna ji ka dhyan, phir chalisa ka paath aur ant mein aarti. Poori puja ke liye [Janmashtami puja vidhi](/krishna-radha/janmashtami-puja-vidhi) aur [Laddu Gopal seva vidhi](/krishna-radha/laddu-gopal-seva-vidhi) dekhein.
- **Shuddhta:** Snan karke saaf kapde pahnein. Paath ke samay mobile silent rakhein.
