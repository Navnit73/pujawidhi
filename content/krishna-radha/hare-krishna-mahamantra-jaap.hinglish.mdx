**Hare Krishna Mahamantra** solah shabdon ka naam-mantra hai: "Hare Krishna Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna Hare Hare, Hare Rama Hare Rama, Rama Rama Hare Hare". Yeh mantra Vaishnav parampara mein, khaaskar Gaudiya Vaishnav sampraday aur ISKCON mein, naam-jaap ka kendra hai. Parampara iska sandarbh **Kali-Santarana Upanishad** se jodti hai. Yeh page mantra, uska arth aur jaap ki saral vidhi batata hai.

Kuch sanskaranon mein pehli panktiyon ka kram "Hare Rama" pehle aur "Hare Krishna" baad mein bhi milta hai. Aaj ke jaap mein sabse prachlit kram upar diya gaya hai.

## Arth (meaning)

- **Hare:** Parampara mein "Hara" shabd ke sambodhan roop ko Bhagwan ki param shakti, Shri Radha, ke liye maana jaata hai. Doosre mat ke anusaar "Hari" (Vishnu, jo kasht harte hain) ka roop bhi kaha jaata hai. Dono vyakhyaen prachlit hain.
- **Krishna:** Sarva-aakarshak, jo bhakton ko apni or khinchte hain.
- **Rama:** Aanand dene waale; Ram naam se kai parampara mein Bhagwan Ram aur kai mein Krishna ka aanand swaroop bhi liya jaata hai.
- **Bhaav:** Mantra ko pukar ya prarthana ki tarah liya jaata hai, jaise bachcha apni maa ko bulata hai. Koi kathin arth samajhna zaroori nahi; naam ko shraddha se lena hi mukhya hai.

## Vidhi: kab, kitni baar aur kaise

- **Kab:** Subah, shaam ya jab bhi shaant samay mile. Vaishnav parampara mein naam-jaap kisi bhi samay kiya ja sakta hai; brahma muhurt mein karna bahut se log pasand karte hain, par yeh anivarya nahi.
- **Kitni baar (ISKCON ki parampara):** ISKCON ke dikshit bhakt roz 16 mala karte hain. Ek mala mein 108 manke hote hain, is tarah 16 mala mein 1728 mantra hote hain. Naye shuru karne waale kam se kam 1 mala se shuruaat kar sakte hain aur dheere-dheere badha sakte hain. Gaudiya aur anya sampradayon mein sankhya alag ho sakti hai.
- **Mala:** Tulsi ki mala ka prayog prachlit hai. Mala ko saaf rakhein aur zameen par na gire, is par dhyan dein. Mala ke "sumeru" (bade manke) ko paar na karein, wahan se ulti disha mein ghoom jaayein, yeh jaap ki parampara hai.
- **Aasan:** Saaf aasan par shaant jagah baithein. Mukh poorv ya uttar ki taraf rakhna prachlit hai, par sakht niyam nahi.
- **Kirtan aur jaap:** Mantra ko dheere-dheere (jaap) ya ucch swar mein saath milkar (kirtan) dono tarah se karna prachlit hai.
- **Niyam:** Koi vishesh dikhawa nahi; saaf mann aur saaf kapde kafi hain. Naam-jaap mein mukhya bhaav hai.
- **Aage:** Krishna ke saath poori puja ke liye [Janmashtami puja vidhi](/krishna-radha/janmashtami-puja-vidhi) aur roz ki seva ke liye [Laddu Gopal seva vidhi](/krishna-radha/laddu-gopal-seva-vidhi) dekhein. Jaap ka saral kram [Pooja path kaise karein](/puja-basics/pooja-path-kaise-karein) page par bhi hai.

## Sources aur variants

Mahamantra ka sandarbh **Kali-Santarana Upanishad** se diya jaata hai. Mantra ke shabd lagbhag sthir hain, par uchcharan (jaise "Krishna" ko "Krishn" ya "Kṛṣṇa" ki tarah) aur panktiyon ka kram sampraday ke anusaar thoda alag ho sakta hai. Mala ki sankhya aur niyam sampradayon mein alag hain; yahan ISKCON ki sankhya ek udaharan ke roop mein di gayi hai, aur kisi sampraday ki sankhya ko hum sabke liye anivarya nahi batate.
