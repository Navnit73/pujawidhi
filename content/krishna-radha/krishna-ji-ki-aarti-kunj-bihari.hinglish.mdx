Krishna ji ki sabse prasiddh aarti **"Aarti Kunj Bihari Ki"** hai. Janmashtami, Radha Ashtami, Laddu Gopal ki roz ki seva aur Vrindavan-Mathura ke mandiron mein yahi aarti gayi jaati hai. Neeche poora paath Devanagari aur Hinglish mein hai. Har pad ke baad "Aarti Kunj Bihari ki" ka refrain dohraya jaata hai. ## Arth (meaning)

- **Kunj Bihari, Giridhar, Murari:** Krishna ke teen priya naam. Kunj Bihari Vrindavan ke kunjon mein leela karne waale, Giridhar Govardhan parvat uthaane waale aur Murari Mur naamak asur ke shatru.
- **Baijanti mala, mor mukut, murali:** Krishna ke prasiddh shringar ke chinh: vanmala, mor-pankh ka mukut aur bansuri.
- **Radhika aur Shyama:** Aarti mein Radha ji ka bhi smaran hai, isliye yeh Radha-Krishna dono ki aarti ki tarah gayi jaati hai.
- **Ganga ka prasang:** Krishna ke charanon se Ganga ke prakat hone ka ullekh hai. Is pad mein Vishnu ke charanon se Ganga ke avtaran ki parampara jhalakti hai.
- **Antim pad:** Bhakt apni deen pukar prabhu tak pahunchne ki prarthana karta hai.

## Kab aur kaise gayein

- **Kab:** Roz ki subah-shaam ki puja ke ant mein. Janmashtami ki raat aur Radha Ashtami par vishesh roop se gayi jaati hai. Laddu Gopal ki seva mein har bhog ke baad aarti ki parampara hai.
- **Kaise:** Diya jalayein, ghanti bajayein aur kapoor ya ghee ki batti se thali ko Krishna ji ki murti ke saamne ghumate hue gayein. Aarti ke baad thali par haath se aarti lekar prasad baantein.
- **Kitni baar:** Koi tay sankhya nahi. Ek baar poori aarti kaafi hai. Mandir mein kai baar "Aarti Kunj Bihari ki" kuch baar dohraya bhi jaata hai.
- **Poora kram:** Janmashtami par poori puja ke liye [Janmashtami puja vidhi](/krishna-radha/janmashtami-puja-vidhi) aur roz ki seva ke liye [Laddu Gopal seva vidhi](/krishna-radha/laddu-gopal-seva-vidhi) dekhein. Doosri aartiyan [Aarti Sangrah](/puja-basics/aarti-sangrah) mein hain.
